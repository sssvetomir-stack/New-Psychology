4.1 Individual practices

Individual practicesThe TREE concept focuses on working with emotions and states through awareness, acceptance, and transformation. They help deepen your connection with yourself, develop emotional resilience, and restore inner balance.

Basic principles of individual practices

  • Mindfulness
  • The ability to notice your emotions, thoughts and physical reactions without judgment.
  • Acceptance
  • Allowing yourself to experience feelings, even if they seem unpleasant or difficult.
  • Active transformation
  • Using practices to direct emotional energy into a creative direction.

Practices for awareness of emotions

  • Diary of emotions
  • Target: Analysis and tracking of emotional states.
  • How to perform:
  • Write down your emotions, their intensity and reasons daily.
  • Ask yourself:What did I feel today? Why did it happen? How did I react to it?
  • Result: Increasing awareness and understanding of your reactions.
  • Emotional Observation Meditation
  • Target: Awareness of emotions without involvement.
  • How to perform:
  • Sit comfortably, close your eyes and focus on your breathing.
  • Allow emotions to arise, observe them, but do not judge or try to change them.
  • Result: The ability to notice emotions and accept them as they are.

Practices for transforming emotions

  • Visualization of the energy of emotion
  • Target: Transforming emotion into a resource.
  • How to perform:
  • Imagine your emotion as a color, shape, or movement.
  • Watch it transform into light, flow, or force that fills you with energy.
  • Result: Releasing negative feelings and using the energy of emotions for creation.
  • Breathing techniques
  • Target: Reduction of emotional stress.
  • How to perform:
  • Technique "4-7-8": inhale for 4 seconds, hold your breath for 7 seconds, exhale for 8 seconds.
  • Focus on the feeling of lightness after each exhalation.
  • Result: Peace and restoration of inner balance.

Practices to strengthen your connection with your body

  • Body scan
  • Target: Awareness of bodily sensations and tensions.
  • How to perform:
  • Lie or sit comfortably. Close your eyes and gradually focus on each part of your body, starting with your head and ending with your toes.
  • Notice where there is tension and imagine it leaving with each exhalation.
  • Result: Relieving physical tension and improving connection with the body.
  • Grounding practice
  • Target: Reconnecting with the present moment.
  • How to perform:
  • Stand barefoot on the ground (or a hard surface), feel the contact with it.
  • Imagine the earth taking away your stress and filling you with strength.
  • Result: Stability and a feeling of internal support.

Practices to restore balance

  • Gratitude practice
  • Target: Switching attention to the positive aspects of life.
  • How to perform:
  • Write down 3-5 things you are grateful for every day.
  • Feel gratitude to yourself, people, or the world for these moments.
  • Result: Improving mood and creating a positive outlook on life.
  • Conscious pauses
  • Target: Slowing down and recovering throughout the day.
  • How to perform:
  • Several times a day, take a break for 1-2 minutes to focus on your breathing and internal state.
  • Ask yourself:What am I feeling right now? What do I need at this moment?
  • Result: Awareness of your needs and stress reduction.

Example of practice: Visualizing the transformation of resentment

  • SituationYou feel resentful because you have been treated unfairly.
  • Practice:
  • Close your eyes and imagine the resentment as a gray fog inside you.
  • With each breath, imagine it becoming lighter.
  • As you exhale, visualize it leaving your body, leaving space for light and peace.
  • Result: Release from resentment and restoration of inner balance.

Conclusion

Individual practices within the TREE concept provide universal tools for working with emotions and states. They help one gain a deeper understanding of oneself, transform inner experiences, and strengthen the connection with one's body and spirit. Regular use of these practices helps restore balance, develop awareness, and harmonize one's life.
