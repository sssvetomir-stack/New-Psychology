Table: Emotion → Strength → Transition

This table helps you understand the power that each emotion holds and how you can channel its energy through a conscious transition to a resourceful state.

EmotionHidden PowerTransition: How to Transform
AngerEnergy of actionAccept anger as a signal of violated boundaries → channel it into constructive changes.
FearAttention acuityRecognize fear as a signal of importance → use it for preparation and planning.
SadnessDepth of feelingsEmbrace sadness as an opportunity to slow down → rethink your values ​​and gain new insights.
JoyMotivationLet joy expand → share positive energy with others.
AlarmNatural readinessRecognize anxiety as an attempt to protect yourself → redirect it to productive actions.
IrritationSensitivity to detailUse irritation as a signal to fix the problem → create a simplified solution.
EnvyThe desire to developRecognize envy as a reflection of your goals → channel your energy into growth and learning.
InsultThe need for respectRecognize resentment as a signal of an unmet need → calmly express your feelings.
FatigueA signal for the need for restAccept fatigue as a need to reboot → make time for recovery.
ShameSelf-analysisRecognize shame as an opportunity to learn → use it for personal growth and correction of mistakes.
HatredThe determination to defend oneselfRecognize hatred as an extreme expression of anger → transform it into setting clear boundaries.
ConfusionWillingness to seek solutionsAccept confusion as a moment of choice → systematize information and make a plan.
DoubtCritical thinkingRecognize doubt as an analytical tool → gather more information and make a decision.
LoveConnection with othersLet love expand → share kindness and gratitude with others.
GratitudeRecognition of valueRecognize gratitude as an internal resource → use it to strengthen positive connections.

Example of using a table

Emotion: Anger

  • Hidden Power:Energy of action.
  • Transition:Recognize that anger indicates a violation of your boundaries. Instead of blaming, find a way to defend your interests constructively.

Emotion: Envy

  • Hidden Power:The desire to develop.
  • Transition:Use envy as an indicator of your unfulfilled desires. Develop a plan to achieve your goal.

Tips for working with tables

  • Identify the emotion.
    Ask yourself:"What am I feeling now?"
  • Find the hidden power.
    Read the table to find out what energy this emotion carries.
  • Follow the transition.
    Use the suggested steps to transform emotion into a resource.
  • Consolidate the experience.
    Write down how this affected your condition and situation.

This chart will become a practical tool for becoming aware of your emotions and transforming them into resources that will help you grow and achieve harmony.
