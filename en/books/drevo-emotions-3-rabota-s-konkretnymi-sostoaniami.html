3. Working with specific states

Emotions and states are not just signals but also sources of energy that can guide us toward awareness and growth. Working with specific states within the TREE concept helps us see each feeling not as a problem, but as a tool that unlocks our inner potential.

3.1 Anger

Anger— is the energy that arises when boundaries are violated or something does not meet expectations.

  • Message of Anger: Anger indicates a need to defend one's values ​​or express suppressed emotions.
  • Practice working with anger:
  • Stop and become aware of what is causing your anger.
  • Take deep breaths and channel your anger into physical activity, such as exercise or dance.
  • Redirect your anger towards problem solving rather than destruction.

3.2 Fear

Fear- This is a natural reaction that protects us from dangers, but can block actions if not consciously realized.

  • Message of fear: It points out areas of uncertainty and the need for preparation.
  • Practice of working with fear:
  • Determine whether the threat causing the fear is real.
  • Create an action plan to reduce uncertainty.
  • Practice grounding: Stand barefoot on the ground to feel supported and stable.

3.3 Wines

Guilt— is a feeling of responsibility for actions or lack thereof, which is often associated with moral convictions.

  • Message of Guilt: It encourages one to correct a mistake or to realize the consequences of one's actions.
  • Practice working with guilt:
  • Admit your guilt and recognize its source.
  • If possible, correct the situation through an apology or action.
  • Forgive yourself for imperfections and learn from them for the future.

3.4 Envy

Envy- is a feeling of dissatisfaction due to the success or achievements of others.

  • Message of Envy: It points to desires that you have not yet realized.
  • Practice of working with envy:
  • Determine what exactly is causing the envy.
  • Turn envy into motivation to achieve your goals.
  • Thank others for the inspiration they have given you.

3.5 Emptiness

Emptiness— this is a feeling of inner emptiness, which can be either a result of fatigue or a stage of rebooting.

  • Message of Emptiness: It gives the opportunity to free up space for something new.
  • Practice of working with emptiness:
  • Accept the state of emptiness as temporary.
  • Use this time for solitude and reflection.
  • Gradually begin to fill your time with small activities that bring joy.

3.6 Confusion

Confusion- it's a feeling that you don't know what to do or where to go.

  • Message of Confusion: It indicates the need to slow down and re-evaluate your priorities.
  • Practice of working with confusion:
  • Pause and list all available options.
  • Break the task into small steps to reduce pressure.
  • Enlist the support of loved ones or a team to find a solution.

3.7 Shame

Shame— this is a feeling associated with the awareness of one’s own inadequacy to expectations, both one’s own and those of others.

  • Message of Shame: It encourages self-knowledge and sincerity.
  • Practice of working with shame:
  • Accept your mistakes and realize that they are part of growth.
  • Realize the temporary nature of shame and use it as a lesson.
  • Show compassion to yourself as a person who is learning and growing.

3.8 Hatred

Hatred— is a strong feeling of rejection or dislike that is often associated with deep wounds or disappointments.

  • Message of Hate: It points to unaccepted parts of yourself or others.
  • Practice of working with hatred:
  • Understand what causes hatred and recognize its roots.
  • Use Compassion Meditation to reduce stress.
  • Redirect the energy of hatred into creation, creativity, or helping others.

3.9 Irritability

Irritability- this is a reaction to overload, fatigue or internal imbalance.

  • Message of Irritability: It signals the need to stop and recover.
  • Practice of working with irritability:
  • Notice what exactly is causing the irritation.
  • Take a break and do a breathing exercise.
  • Think about how you can reduce the overload in your life.

3.10 Doubt

Doubt- is a feeling that arises when there is uncertainty or a lack of confidence in your decisions.

  • Message of Doubt: It encourages analysis and the search for inner clarity.
  • Practice of working with doubt:
  • Write down the pros and cons of your decisions.
  • Trust the process and take the first step.
  • Remember that mistakes are also experience and a lesson.

Conclusion

Every emotional state carries a certain potential for development. The TREE concept suggests viewing emotions not as barriers, but as keys to inner growth. Consciously working with them allows us to transform even difficult states into resources, filling our lives with meaning, harmony, and new opportunities.
