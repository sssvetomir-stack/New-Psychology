3.6 Confusion

Confusion— is a state that arises from a feeling of uncertainty, loss of direction, or the inability to make a decision. It is often accompanied by feelings of anxiety or helplessness. In the TREE concept, confusion is viewed as a signal that prompts one to stop, recognize one's priorities, and find a new path.

The nature of confusion

  • Uncertainty signal
    Confusion arises when a person is faced with a lack of clarity or choice.
  • State of transition
    This is a natural reaction to change, when the old no longer works and the new has not yet been found.
  • The need for awareness
    Confusion indicates a need to slow down to better understand yourself and the situation.

Why is it important to work with confusion?

  • Reduced anxiety
    Working with confusion helps calm the mind and build confidence.
  • Acceptance of the temporary state
    Confusion is part of the change process, not a permanent state.
  • Search for direction
    This state encourages you to rethink your goals, values ​​and priorities.

How to recognize confusion?

  • Confession of feelings
  • Tell yourself:"I feel confused, and that's okay. It's temporary and will help me find a new path."
  • Slow down and pause
  • Instead of rushing into a decision, pause to acknowledge your thoughts and emotions.
  • Investigation of the cause
  • Ask yourself:What exactly is causing my confusion? Is it due to external circumstances or internal beliefs?

Practice of working with confusion

  • Writing thoughts
  • Write down everything that's causing you confusion. This helps organize your thoughts and reduce emotional stress.
  • Breaking the problem down
  • Break down a complex situation into simpler steps. For example, ask yourself:
  • What can I do right now?
  • What resources do I have?
  • Visualization of Clarity
  • Imagine confusion as fog. During meditation, visualize this fog gradually dissipating, revealing a clear path before you.
  • Seeking support
  • Reach out to friends, mentors, or members of your TREE support circle to gain a fresh perspective.

An example of working with confusion

  • SituationYou feel at a loss about changing jobs but don't know where to start.
  • AwarenessRealizing that your confusion is caused by a fear of change, you decide to explore your professional interests.
  • Action: You make a list of your skills, analyze job openings and start updating your resume.

Meditation for dealing with confusion

  • Find a quiet place and sit comfortably.
  • Close your eyes and focus on your breathing.
  • Imagine your confusion as a wind swirling around you.
  • Gradually visualize this wind dying down, leaving behind silence and calm.
  • Tell yourself:"I am open to new solutions and ready to move forward."

What to Avoid When Dealing with Confusion

  • Hasty decisions
  • Don't make decisions just to get rid of the feeling of confusion.
  • Ignoring the problem
  • Confusion is a sign that you need to stop and figure things out, not avoid the situation.
  • Comparisons with others
  • Comparing yourself to others can increase confusion. Remember, everyone has their own path.

Confusion as a resource in TREE

  • Deepening self-knowledgeConfusion prompts you to ask important questions about yourself and your life.
  • Growth pointThis state helps to go beyond the usual and find new directions.
  • Possibility of slowing downConfusion gives time to rethink and prepare for new steps.

Conclusion

Confusion isn't a weakness, but a natural part of the journey of change. In the TREE concept, it becomes a moment of awareness that helps you find clarity and confidence. By working with confusion mindfully, you learn to see it not as an obstacle, but as an opportunity for inner growth and renewal.
