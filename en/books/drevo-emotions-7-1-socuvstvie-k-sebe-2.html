7.1 Self-compassion

Self-compassion— is the ability to accept oneself with warmth and understanding, especially in difficult or painful situations. This isn't pity, but rather an expression of caring for oneself as if one were a friend in need of support. In the TREE concept, self-compassion is the foundation for inner balance and harmony, allowing one to transform difficulties into resources for growth.

What does self-compassion involve?

  • Acceptance of imperfection
  • Realizing that mistakes and difficulties are a natural part of life.
  • Kind attitude towards yourself
  • Refusing self-criticism in favor of care and support.
  • Mindfulness
  • The ability to notice your emotions and states without judgment and denial.
  • Actions to improve the condition
  • Willingness to show self-care through words, actions, or rest.

Why is self-compassion important?

  • Reducing internal conflict
  • Empathy helps replace negative internal dialogue with supportive one.
  • Developing resilience
  • Self-acceptance strengthens the ability to cope with difficulties.
  • Increasing self-esteem
  • Taking care of yourself helps you realize your importance and value.
  • Harmony with others
  • When you have compassion for yourself, it becomes easier to show compassion to others.

Self-compassion practices

1. Technique "Good internal dialogue"

  • How to perform:
  • Notice when you start criticizing yourself.
  • Imagine you are talking to a close friend who is in your situation.
  • Replace criticism with supportive words, such as:
  • "It's okay to make mistakes, I still deserve love and respect."

2. Self-compassion meditation

  • How to perform:
  • Sit in a comfortable position, close your eyes.
  • Place your hand on your heart and feel the warmth of your palm.
  • Repeat to yourself:
  • "I accept my feelings as they are."
  • "May I be free from suffering."
  • "Let me be kind to myself."
  • End the meditation by expressing gratitude to yourself for this moment of caring.

3. Empathy Diary

  • How to perform:
  • At the end of the day, write down:
  • Situations in which you showed kindness to yourself.
  • The feelings you experienced because of it.
  • How you could be even more caring towards yourself.
  • Complete the entry with the phrase:"I'm proud of myself for taking care of myself today."

4. Exercise "Physical calming"

  • How to perform:
  • When you feel tense or stressed, try physically expressing compassion:
  • Give yourself a hug, put your hand on your heart, or take a deep breath.
  • Imagine hugging yourself the way you would hug a friend in a difficult situation.
  • Tell: "I'm here for myself, I can handle it."

Tips for Developing Self-Compassion

  • Allow yourself to feel
  • Acknowledge your emotions, even if they seem unwanted.
  • Give up perfectionism
  • Remind yourself that it's okay and natural to be imperfect.
  • Be patient with yourself
  • View your mistakes as opportunities for growth, not as a reason for self-criticism.
  • Create self-care rituals
  • Find activities that bring you joy and peace and incorporate them into your day regularly.

An example of applying self-compassion to yourself

Situation: You made a mistake at work and feel guilty.

  • Awareness: You notice the feeling of guilt and acknowledge it.
  • Good internal dialogue: Tell yourself:
  • "I made a mistake, but that doesn't make me a bad person. It's a learning opportunity."
  • ActionRemind yourself that you deserve rest and make time for things that bring you joy.

Conclusion

Self-compassion is the foundation for inner peace and resilience. It helps you process your emotions, accept yourself, and find the strength to overcome challenges. Regularly practicing self-compassion allows you to build harmonious relationships with yourself and the world around you, making your life more mindful and fulfilling.
