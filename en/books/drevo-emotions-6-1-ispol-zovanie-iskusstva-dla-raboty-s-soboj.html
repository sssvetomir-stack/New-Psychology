6.1 Using art to work on yourself

Art is a powerful tool for self-expression, awareness, and transformation of internal states. The TREE concept uses art as a means of working with emotions, healing, and unlocking inner potential. The creative process helps safely express difficult feelings, see them from a different perspective, and transform them into a resource for growth.

Why is art effective for working on yourself?

  • Freedom of expression
  • Art allows us to express emotions that are difficult to describe in words.
  • Emotional release
  • Through creativity, you can safely release tension and resentment.
  • Self-knowledge
  • The creative process helps you better understand yourself, your desires and fears.
  • Energy transformation
  • Negative emotions are transformed into creative energy.
  • Raising awareness
  • Art helps you to be in the moment, to concentrate on the process, not on the result.

Basic art forms for working with yourself

1. Drawing and painting

  • How to use:
  • Draw your current emotional state in the form of abstract shapes, lines and colors.
  • Try drawing your problem or feeling, and then changing the drawing to reflect the solution or desired state.
  • Example:
  • Anger can be depicted as red, chaotic lines, and then transformed into calm shapes using softer colors.

2. Music and sound

  • How to use:
  • Play musical instruments or create sounds that reflect your emotional state.
  • Listen to music that helps you calm down, get inspired, or release tension.
  • Example:
  • When you feel anxious, choose a relaxing melody and concentrate on its sound, allowing your body to relax.

3. Dance and movement

  • How to use:
  • Let your body move to the beat of the music without thinking about technique.
  • Express your emotions through improvised movements.
  • Example:
  • If you feel your energy is stagnant, perform sharp, intense movements, then slow down and feel the changes in your body.

4. Literature and writing

  • How to use:
  • Write about your feelings, thoughts and experiences without limiting yourself in words.
  • Create stories or poems that reflect your inner states.
  • Example:
  • If you feel hurt, write a letter to the person who hurt you (even if you don't intend to send it) to express your emotions and release them.

5. Sculpture and working with materials

  • How to use:
  • Sculpt from clay or create objects from other materials to express your current state.
  • Transform what you have created to reflect the process of internal transformation.
  • Example:
  • Create a clay figure that symbolizes your fear, and then transform it into a symbol of strength or confidence.

Practices of using art to work on yourself

1. Drawing of emotion

  • How to perform:
  • Take a piece of paper and paints or pencils.
  • Draw your current state using color, lines and shapes.
  • Ask yourself:"What do I want to change in this state?"
  • Create a new drawing that reflects the desired state.
  • Result: Awareness of emotions and their transformation.

2. Sound Release Technique

  • How to perform:
  • Turn on music that matches your mood.
  • For 5-10 minutes, make sounds or say words that come to mind.
  • Complete the process by choosing sounds or words that bring you comfort.
  • Result: Releasing accumulated emotions through sound.

3. Writing without stopping

  • How to perform:
  • Set the timer for 10 minutes.
  • Write whatever comes to mind without stopping or editing the text.
  • After finishing, reread what you have written, highlighting important thoughts or feelings.
  • Result: Awareness of hidden experiences and their expression.

4. Elemental dance

  • How to perform:
  • Choose an element that is close to you now (for example, water, fire, air or earth).
  • Imagine your body expressing this element through movement.
  • Dance freely until you feel liberated.
  • Result: Strengthening your connection with yourself through the expression of elemental energy.

Tips for Successful Work Through Art

  • Don't judge the result
  • Focus on the process, not how good the final product looks.
  • Be sincere
  • Express your feelings without censorship, letting art become your mirror.
  • Give yourself time
  • The creative process can take time, don't rush yourself.
  • Integrate practices into your life
  • Find a place for art in your daily life: draw, dance, or write regularly.

Conclusion

Art is a unique way to work with oneself, helping one to deeply understand one's emotions, express them, and transform them into a source of strength and inspiration. In the TREE concept, creativity becomes a guide to inner harmony and development. Regular creative practice strengthens the connection with oneself and makes the process of transformation more conscious and natural.
