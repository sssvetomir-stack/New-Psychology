5.3 Application in collective activities

Applying mindfulness and emotional processing practices to group activities helps improve interaction, strengthen mutual understanding, and create a harmonious atmosphere within the group. This is especially important for increasing productivity, preventing conflict, and building trust among participants.

Why apply mindfulness in group activities?

  • Improving communication
  • Mindfulness helps you express your thoughts and feelings clearly and respectfully.
  • Reducing conflicts
  • Managing emotions allows you to resolve tense situations peacefully and effectively.
  • Increasing engagement
  • Understanding your roles and goals in the group motivates active participation.
  • Creating a trusting environment
  • Collective awareness strengthens mutual trust and support.

Principles of conscious interaction in a team

  • Non-judgmental
  • Respect the opinions and experiences of other participants without judging them critically.
  • Active listening
  • Focus on what your interlocutor is saying, trying to understand his point of view.
  • Clear expression of thoughts
  • Talk about your feelings and needs without blaming or manipulating.
  • Collective responsibility
  • Recognize your roles and actions in achieving common goals.

Practices for collective activities

1. Circles of Trust

  • Target: Create a safe space to discuss emotions, ideas and questions.
  • How to conduct:
  • All participants gather in a circle.
  • Everyone speaks on the topic without interrupting each other.
  • For example, the topic:"How do I feel in our team?"
  • Result: Strengthening mutual respect and openness.

2. Conscious decision making

  • Target: Take into account everyone's opinion when choosing a direction.
  • How to conduct:
  • Set aside time to discuss proposals.
  • Each participant shares their opinion without judgment.
  • The decision is made on the basis of collective consensus.
  • Result: Increasing inclusion and fairness in decision-making.

3. Emotional relief

  • Target: To relieve tension and restore balance in the group.
  • How to conduct:
  • Organize a physical activity, such as group dance or exercise.
  • Conduct a group harmonization meditation.
  • Participants can share their emotions through creativity: drawing, music or storytelling.
  • Result: Release of accumulated emotions and restoration of energy.

Roles and responsibilities in a team

1. The role of the leader

  • Tasks:
  • Create a safe space for discussion.
  • Maintain a balance between the goals of the group and the emotional needs of the participants.

2. Role of the participant

  • Tasks:
  • Be engaged and sincere in expressing your thoughts.
  • Respect the time and space of other team members.

3. The role of the observer

  • Tasks:
  • Monitor group dynamics and propose solutions for harmonization.
  • Maintain a balance between individual and group interests.

Examples of collective practices

Example 1: The "Common Intention" Ritual

  • All participants sit in a circle.
  • Each person takes turns sharing their intention or goal for the group.
  • The presenter formulates a common intention that unites all statements.
  • Result: Strengthening collective energy and sense of common purpose.

Example 2: Collective meditation on harmonization

  • Participants close their eyes and focus on their breathing.
  • The presenter asks to imagine how each participant is connected by light with the others.
  • Everyone visualizes how this light strengthens the connection, filling the group with warmth and harmony.
  • Result: Reducing tension and strengthening team spirit.

How to deal with conflicts in a group?

  • Awareness of emotions
  • Each participant in the conflict must identify their feelings and reasons.
  • Expression through "I-statements"
  • You say:"I feel irritated because it is important for me to be heard,"instead of accusations.
  • Focus on the solution
  • Instead of discussing who is to blame, focus on finding a way out of the situation.

Tips for successful teamwork

  • Regular meetings
  • Hold meetings to discuss emotions, goals and proposals.
  • Recognition of merit
  • Thank participants for their contribution to the common cause.
  • Caring for balance
  • Consider both the group's objectives and the emotional state of its members.
  • Developing flexibility
  • Be prepared to adapt your approaches depending on the current situation.

Conclusion

Applying mindfulness and emotional intelligence to teamwork strengthens mutual understanding, prevents conflict, and helps achieve goals harmoniously. TREE practices help create an atmosphere of trust, support, and unity within the team, where each participant feels like an important part of the overall process.
