4.4 Energy and body exercises

Energy and body exercisesIn the TREE concept, these are practices that help relieve emotional tension, restore inner balance, and enhance the flow of energy in the body. These methods allow one to consciously work with the body, emotions, and energetic states, strengthening the connection between the physical and spiritual aspects of a person.

Why do we need energy and body exercises?

  • Relief from tension
  • Helps release pent-up emotions such as stress, anger or resentment.
  • Energy activation
  • Increases vitality by enhancing the flow of energy throughout the body.
  • Connection with the body
  • Develops awareness of the body and promotes understanding of its signals.
  • Harmonization of the state
  • Helps to achieve balance between physical, emotional and spiritual aspects.

Basic types of energy and body exercises

1. Grounding practices

Target: Connect with the present moment and reduce internal tension.

  • How to perform:
  • Stand barefoot on the ground or a hard surface.
  • Feel your feet touching the ground.
  • Imagine the energy of tension leaving your body through your feet, going into the ground.
  • As you inhale, visualize the earth filling you with stability and strength.
  • Result: Peace and a sense of stability.

2. Body scanning

Target: Awareness of tensions and their release.

  • How to perform:
  • Lie down or sit in a comfortable position.
  • Focus on each part of your body, starting with your head and moving towards your feet.
  • In areas of tension, imagine them relaxing with each exhalation.
  • Result: Relieves muscle tension and restores balance.

3. Dynamic Meditation

Target: Release emotional blocks through movement.

  • How to perform:
  • Turn on some rhythmic music.
  • Allow your body to move freely, expressing your emotions through dancing, jumping or darting movements.
  • After the intense phase, pause for a few minutes to feel the silence and calm.
  • Result: Emotional release and a surge of energy.

4. Working with breathing

Target: Balance your emotional state and improve energy flow.

  • Examples of breathing techniques:
  • Deep breathing: Inhale through your nose for 4 counts, hold for 4 counts, exhale through your mouth for 8 counts.
  • Wave breathing: Breathe so that your chest rises first, then your stomach, creating a smooth movement.
  • Result: Calmness, mental clarity and increased energy.

5. Energy exercises with the elements

Target: Use natural elements to restore harmony.

  • Earth Element:
  • Stand on the ground, feel its density and stability.
  • Imagine how it takes away your fatigue and fills you with energy.
  • Element of Water:
  • Place your hands or feet in the water, imagining it washing away your worries and refreshing your mind.
  • Element of Fire:
  • Sit in front of a candle or fire, focusing on the warmth and light, feeling the fire cleanse you.
  • Element of Air:
  • Step out into the breeze or stand by an open window. Imagine the air blowing away your doubts and fears.

Examples of complex exercises

1. Exercise "Centering"

  • Stand straight with your feet shoulder-width apart.
  • Place one hand on your chest and the other on your stomach.
  • Take a few deep breaths, focusing on the center of your body.
  • Feel the energy concentrating in the center and distributing throughout the body.
  • Result: Restoring internal balance and concentration.

2. Exercise "Opening the Heart"

  • Stand straight with your arms along your body.
  • As you inhale, raise your arms up through the sides, opening your chest.
  • As you exhale, lower your arms and feel the tension go away.
  • Repeat 5-7 times, each time visualizing your heart filling with warmth and light.
  • Result: Strengthening a sense of openness and acceptance.

Tips for practice

  • Regularity
  • Perform exercises daily or as needed to maintain balance.
  • Listen to your body
  • Tune in to your sensations and choose the exercises that are comfortable for you.
  • Integration with nature
  • Practice outdoors to enhance the effects of the exercises.
  • Mindfulness in the process
  • Focus on every movement and sensation to get the most benefit from your exercises.

Conclusion

Energy and body exercises within the TREE concept are a powerful tool for restoring harmony, relieving emotional tension, and strengthening the connection with the body. They allow one to work with energy, directing it toward creation, and provide the opportunity to achieve inner peace and strength through simple yet effective practices. Regularly performing these exercises strengthens the body, spirit, and emotional state, promoting harmonious personal development.
