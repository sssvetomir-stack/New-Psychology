5. Working with emotions in real time

Working with emotions in real time is a practice of consciously perceiving and managing your feelings as they arise. This approach helps you maintain balance, prevent impulsive reactions, and use emotions as a resource for personal growth.

Why work with emotions in real time?

  • Stress reduction
  • Conscious perception of emotions helps to avoid their accumulation and transformation into chronic tension.
  • Conflict prevention
  • Managing emotions reduces the risk of impulsive reactions that can lead to misunderstandings.
  • Improving the quality of life
  • Being aware of your feelings allows you to choose how to respond to them, rather than being held hostage by them.
  • Developing mindfulness
  • Working with emotions strengthens the ability to be in the present moment and understand oneself.

The main stages of working with emotions

1. Awareness of emotion

  • Acknowledge that you are experiencing an emotion. For example:
  • "I feel irritated."
  • "It's fear."
  • Notice how the emotion manifests itself in your body: tension, rapid breathing, heat or cold.

2. Acceptance of emotion

  • Don't try to suppress or ignore your feelings. Tell yourself:
  • "It's normal that I feel this way. Everyone has moments like this."

3. Analysis of the cause

  • Ask yourself:
  • "What caused this emotion?"
  • “Is this an external situation or my internal expectations?”

4. Choosing a conscious response

  • Instead of an automatic response, decide how you want to respond:
  • Calm down and explain your point of view.
  • Walk away from the situation to think about it.

Practical techniques

1. Breathing technique "4-4-8"

  • How to perform:
  • Inhale through your nose for a count of 4.
  • Hold your breath for 4 counts.
  • Exhale through your mouth for a count of 8.
  • Result: Calms the mind and reduces stress levels.

2. Stop and Observe Practice

  • How to perform:
  • Stop and take a break.
  • Ask yourself:
  • "What am I feeling now?"
  • "How does this affect my behavior?"
  • Allow yourself to feel the emotion, and then make a conscious decision about how to proceed.
  • Result: Interrupts automatic reactions.

3. Conscious Grounding

  • How to perform:
  • Stand or sit comfortably.
  • Feel your feet on the surface of the earth.
  • Imagine the energy of the earth stabilizing you and reducing emotional stress.
  • Result: Restores a sense of stability and control.

4. Visualization of emotion

  • How to perform:
  • Imagine your emotion as an image: for example, it could be a dark cloud (anger) or a raging stream of water (fear).
  • Visualize this image gradually transforming: the cloud dissipating, the stream becoming a calm stream.
  • Result: Helps soften the intensity of emotion.

Real-life application example

Situation: You feel irritated because you have to wait in line for a long time.

  • Awareness:
  • Acknowledge your feeling:"I'm annoyed because the wait is taking longer than I expected."
  • Acceptance:
  • Tell yourself:"It's normal that I feel irritated. It's a natural reaction."
  • Analysis of the cause:
  • Understand that the irritation is caused by your expectation of fast service, not by the fact of the line itself.
  • Conscious reaction:
  • Use the time for mindful breathing or reflection:
  • "I can calmly wait my turn. This is a temporary situation."

Tips for Effectively Working with Emotions

  • Practice mindfulness regularly
  • The more time you spend being aware of your emotions, the easier it is to work with them in real time.
  • Don't be afraid to pause
  • Sometimes it's better to delay your reaction and come back to the situation with a calmer mind.
  • Develop empathy for yourself
  • Remind yourself that emotions are part of the human experience and they don't make you weaker.
  • Use support
  • Discuss your emotions with a friend or mentor if the situation seems too difficult.

Conclusion

Working with emotions in real time is a skill that can be developed through regular mindfulness and self-reflection practices. It helps transform every emotion into a tool for growth, strengthens internal balance, and improves interactions with the outside world. The key is to start small and be kind to yourself along the way.
