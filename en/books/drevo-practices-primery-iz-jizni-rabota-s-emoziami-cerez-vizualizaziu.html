Real-Life Examples: Working with Emotions through Visualization

These examples illustrate how visualization can be used to recognize, transform, and manage emotions in various situations. They demonstrate that even the most difficult feelings can become a resource if approached mindfully.

Example 1: Anger at a colleague

Situation:A colleague failed to complete his part of the work on time, which affected the entire project.

  • Awareness of emotion:
  • "I get angry because it's important to me that everyone does their job."
  • Image presentation:
  • Anger is perceived as a red, fiery ball that rotates in the chest area.
  • Image transformation:
  • The ball gradually decreases in size, its color becomes orange, then golden, and it begins to glow softly.
  • Resource creation:
  • Anger is transformed into energy for action: you calmly discuss with your colleague how to avoid similar situations in the future, proposing solutions.
  • Result:
  • Instead of conflict, you strengthen working relationships and find ways to improve interactions.

Example 2: Fear of public speaking

Situation:You have to perform in front of a large audience and you feel nervous and afraid.

  • Awareness of emotion:
  • "I feel fear because I'm afraid of failure and judgment."
  • Image presentation:
  • Fear appears as a grey cloud that surrounds you and presses down.
  • Image transformation:
  • The cloud gradually dissipates, and in its place appears bright sunlight, which warms you and fills you with confidence.
  • Resource creation:
  • The light becomes an inner source of strength and reminds you that you are prepared and capable of coping.
  • Result:
  • You speak with confidence, focusing on your message rather than fear.

Example 3: Sadness due to loss

Situation:You have lost something important (a relationship, a job, or an opportunity) and feel very sad.

  • Awareness of emotion:
  • "I feel sad because loss reminds me of what is truly important to me."
  • Image presentation:
  • Sadness feels like a heavy grey stone in the stomach area.
  • Image transformation:
  • The stone begins to dissolve, turning into a transparent stream of water that flows gently and carries away the pain.
  • Resource creation:
  • The flow of water becomes a symbol of purification and new life, filling you with a feeling of lightness and hope.
  • Result:
  • You begin to accept loss as part of your journey and find in it experience for the future.

Example 4: Anxiety before an important event

Situation:You have a job interview and you are feeling anxious.

  • Awareness of emotion:
  • "I feel anxious because this event is important for my future."
  • Image presentation:
  • Anxiety looks like a rapidly spinning black wheel.
  • Image transformation:
  • The wheel begins to slow down, its color turns light gray, and then it turns into a clear circle of light that stabilizes your thoughts.
  • Resource creation:
  • The circle of light becomes a symbol of concentration and confidence. You feel prepared for any challenge.
  • Result:
  • During the interview, you appear calm and showcase your strengths.

Example 5: Joy that you want to increase

Situation:You have received good news and want to keep this feeling of joy for a longer time.

  • Awareness of emotion:
  • "I feel joy because my efforts have been crowned with success."
  • Image presentation:
  • Joy is felt as a golden light that pulsates in the chest area.
  • Enhance the image:
  • Imagine this light becoming brighter, expanding throughout your body and filling you with warmth.
  • Resource creation:
  • The golden light transforms into a feeling of gratitude and motivation for new achievements.
  • Result:
  • You maintain a sense of joy throughout the day, which fills you with energy.

What do these examples teach us?

  • Emotions are energy.
  • Even the most unpleasant feelings can become a source of strength if you approach them mindfully.
  • Images make it easier to work with emotions.
  • Visualization makes complex feelings more understandable and accessible for transformation.
  • Practice makes perfect.
  • The more often you use visualization, the easier it will be for you to manage your states.

Conclusion

Visualization in real-life situations helps you see emotions from a different perspective, understand their meaning, and transform them into actionable resources. Regular use of this method will make you more resilient, confident, and mindful in any life situation.
