1.2 The TREE Concept: The Path to Harmony

ConceptTREEis a philosophy based on the principles of mindfulness, interconnectedness, and inner development. It views people as part of a holistic system in which personal growth, self-harmony, and interaction with the surrounding world become fundamental elements.

Symbolism of the TREE

TREE is a powerful image that captures the essence of inner growth and connection with the world:

  • Roots— a person's inner world: their emotions, thoughts, values, and beliefs. Roots provide stability and nourishment.
  • Trunk— the conscious self, connecting the inner and the outer. The trunk symbolizes the process of transformation and growth.
  • Crown— the result of development: harmony, creative self-expression, interaction with the surrounding world.

Each part of the TREE represents stages of working on oneself, creating a system that helps a person grow and develop.

Basic principles of the concept

  • Mindfulness as the basis of harmony
    Awareness of one's emotions, thoughts and actions allows a person to manage their internal state and find balance.
  • Interconnectedness
    TREE emphasizes that humans are inseparable from the world around them. Harmony within oneself creates harmony in relationships and the space around us.
  • Transformation through difficulties
    Emotions and states such as fear, anger, envy or guilt are perceived not as obstacles, but as points of growth and sources of strength.
  • Creation through interaction
    Personal growth is impossible without interaction with others. Support, collaboration, and shared efforts strengthen connections between people and help each person realize their full potential.

TREE as a development system

  • Inner World: Roots
  • Awareness of your emotions and states.
  • Working with internal beliefs and attitudes.
  • Accepting your vulnerability and strength.
  • Conscious Action: Trunk
  • Practicing mindfulness in everyday life.
  • Transformation of emotions into energy for development.
  • Building a balance between the internal and the external.
  • The Outside World: Krona
  • Harmonious interaction with others.
  • Creative self-expression.
  • Contribution to the development of community and the world.

Tools WOOD

  • Individual practices
    Meditation, breathing exercises, reflection, and creativity help us work with emotions and states on a personal level.
  • Collective practices
    Interaction within circles of trust, shared rituals, and common projects strengthen mutual understanding and support.
  • Nature as a teacher
    Connection with nature – using the elements (earth, water, air, fire) to harmonize the internal state and restore energy.

The role of emotions in the TREE concept

Emotions are seen as an essential part of the human experience. They are not suppressed or ignored, but rather explored and transformed. Each emotion has its own potential for growth:

  • Angercan become the energy of action.
  • Fear— a guide to awareness.
  • Guilt- an incentive for correction.
  • Envy— a source of motivation.

TREE as a path to harmony

The path to harmony in the TREE concept is a process that includes:

  • Awareness of yourself and your emotions.
  • Accepting difficulties as part of growth.
  • Transformation of the internal state.
  • Creating a balance between the personal and the collective.
  • Interacting with the world through creativity and support.

TREEIt symbolizes inner strength and connection with the world. It is not only a philosophy but also a practical system that helps each person find their path to harmony, unlock their potential, and create space for love, awareness, and growth.
