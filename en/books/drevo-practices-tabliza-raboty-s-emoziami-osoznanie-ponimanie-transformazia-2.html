Emotional Workflow: Awareness → Understanding → Transformation

This chart is designed for deep analysis of emotions and working with them on different levels. It helps you recognize feelings, understand their causes, and transform emotional energy into a resourceful state.

EmotionWhat do I feel?What does this emotion signal?How to transform it?What resource can be obtained?
AngerTension in the chest, rapid breathing, a desire to express dissatisfaction.Violation of boundaries, unmet expectations.Take a break, take deep breaths, and express your feelings constructively.Energy to protect interests and solve complex problems.
FearIncreased heart rate, coldness in the body, desire to avoid the situation.Danger or uncertainty.Accept fear as a signal, prepare for the situation, visualize a positive outcome.Attentiveness, ability to analyze and adapt.
SadnessHeaviness in the chest, slowness, desire to be alone.Loss or disappointment, the need to slow down.Allow yourself to experience sadness, use the time to reflect and recover.Calmness, revaluation of values, wisdom.
AlarmTightness in the stomach, fussiness, anxiety about the future.Uncertainty or lack of information.Make a list of actions, perform a grounding technique, focus on the present moment.Motivation for preparation and problem solving.
IrritationUnpleasant tension, desire to avoid contact or argument.Discrepancy between expectations and reality.Take irritation as a signal, take a break, and reconsider your expectations.Ability to find solutions and improve interactions.
ShameThe desire to hide, a feeling of inadequacy.Realization of one's mistakes or violation of one's own principles.Remind yourself that mistakes are part of growth, and learn from the situation.Opportunity to learn, develop self-acceptance.
EnvyThe desire to have what others have, a feeling of dissatisfaction.Unconscious dreams or unrealized goals.Turn envy into motivation, make a plan to achieve your desires.Striving for development, clarity of one’s desires.
InsultA feeling of injustice, a desire to distance oneself from the offender.Unmet expectations in relationships.Be aware of your feelings, talk to the offender, and express your boundaries calmly.Building trust, emotional freedom.
FatigueLoss of strength, apathy, lack of motivation.Overload, lack of rest or resources.Allow yourself to rest, set priorities, delegate tasks.Restoring energy, understanding the limits of one’s capabilities.
ConfusionInability to make decisions, chaos in thoughts.Information overload, lack of clarity.Systematize the data, highlight the main points, make a temporary, uncertain decision.Ability to structure information and find solutions.

How to use the table

  • Identify the emotion.
    Ask yourself:"What am I feeling now?"
  • Analyze the signals.
    Identify what is causing this emotion and think about what it is trying to tell you.
  • Follow the recommendations.
    Use the suggested steps to work with emotion.
  • Find a resource.
    Understand what power or skill you can gain through consciously transforming emotion.

Example of working with a table

Emotion: Anxiety

  • What do I feel?Tightness in the abdomen, fussiness.
  • What does this emotion signal?Uncertainty about the outcome of the task.
  • How to transform it?Make a list of steps to prepare, use breathing techniques.
  • What resource can be obtained?The ability to prepare and feel confident.

Conclusion

This chart helps you see each emotion not as a problem, but as an opportunity for growth. Regularly working with it will improve your awareness and strengthen your emotional resilience.
