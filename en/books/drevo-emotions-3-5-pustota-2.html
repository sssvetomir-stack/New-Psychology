3.5 Emptiness

Emptiness— is a feeling of inner emptiness, when a person loses connection with themselves, the world around them, or the meaning of life. This state can cause discomfort, anxiety, or loneliness, but in the TREE concept, emptiness is viewed as a space for inner rebirth, deep introspection, and a new beginning.

The nature of emptiness

  • State of transition
    Emptiness often occurs during periods of change, when something has ended but something new has not yet begun.
  • A signal for rethinking
    It points to the need to slow down to become aware of your inner needs and desires.
  • Space for the new
    Emptiness symbolizes a clean canvas on which you can paint a new picture of your life.

Why is it important to work with emptiness?

  • Realizing its value
    Emptiness gives the opportunity to let go of the old, making room for new experiences and ideas.
  • Avoiding suppression
    If left unchecked, emptiness can develop into depression or a chronic feeling of dissatisfaction.
  • Reboot
    Working with emptiness helps restore inner balance and energy.

How to realize emptiness?

  • Accept emptiness as a part of life
  • Realize that emptiness is not an enemy, but a natural state that arises in the process of life.
  • Tell yourself:"The void is temporary, it gives me a chance to reboot."
  • Monitor your condition
  • Notice how the emptiness manifests itself: it might be a feeling of tiredness, loss of interest, or a desire to be alone.
  • Explore the causes of emptiness
  • Ask yourself:What led me to this state? What needs remain unmet?

Practice of working with emptiness

  • Solitude and reflection
  • Find time for yourself. Try to simply be in this state, observing it rather than running away from it.
  • Keep a journal, recording your thoughts and feelings.
  • Creative expression
  • Use emptiness as inspiration for creativity: draw, write, make music or other art.
  • Filling through nature
  • Spend time in nature, connect with the earth, water, and air. Nature helps restore inner balance.
  • Small steps to filling
  • Start filling the void with small joys: reading, communication, self-care.

An example of working with emptiness

  • Situation: You feel like you have lost interest in your work and hobbies.
  • Awareness: Emptiness indicates the need to re-evaluate your values ​​and priorities.
  • ActionYou make time to relax, try new activities, and gradually find inspiration in what brings you joy.

Meditation for working with emptiness

  • Sit in a quiet place, close your eyes and take a few deep breaths.
  • Imagine emptiness as a space inside you.
  • Observe this emptiness without trying to fill it or change it.
  • Visualize light or warm energy entering this space, filling it with peace and harmony.

What to avoid when working with emptiness

  • Hasty filling
  • Don't try to quickly fill the void with superficial pleasures (e.g., overconsumption, entertainment).
  • Insulation
  • Emptiness can trigger the desire to retreat into complete isolation. Instead, find a balance between solitude and connection.
  • Ignoring the signal
  • Emptiness is a sign that you need to stop and reevaluate your priorities. Don't ignore it.

Emptiness as a resource in the TREE

  • Room for growth: Emptiness allows you to let go of the old and make room for the new.
  • Opportunity for rethinking: It gives you time to think about what's really important.
  • Source of inspirationFrom emptiness new ideas, dreams and directions are born.

Conclusion

Emptiness isn't a void in the literal sense, but an opportunity for transformation. In the TREE concept, it becomes a source of self-knowledge, restoration, and rebirth. Consciously working with this state helps us see it not as a problem, but as a resource that opens the way to a new beginning.
