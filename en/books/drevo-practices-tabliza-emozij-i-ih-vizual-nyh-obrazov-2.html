Table of emotions and their visual images

This chart helps you visualize your emotions so you can understand, process, and transform them. Visualization makes feelings more tangible and allows you to manage them through your imagination.

EmotionVisual imageSymbol of transformationDescription of the transformation process
AngerFireball or volcanoWarm lightImagine the fire diminishing, becoming a soft and warm light that fills the body with energy.
FearDark cloud or cold windClear skyImagine the cloud dissolving and in its place appears a clear blue sky and light.
SadnessA heavy stone or raindropsA flowing streamImagine the stone melting into a stream of water that flows easily and freely.
JoySunlight or pulsating golden ballExpanding LightImagine how the light grows, fills the entire body and shares with the surrounding space.
AlarmA rapidly spinning wheel or chaotic linesCalm LakeImagine the rotation slowing down, the lines becoming more orderly, and the surface turning into a smooth lake.
IrritationA thorny bush or a ball of threadsUntangled soft threadsImagine thorns turning into smooth petals, or a ball of thread gently unraveling.
EnvyGreen flame or cold gazeGlowing green crystalImagine the flame stabilizing and becoming a shining crystal with inner light.
InsultA heart covered in cracksBlooming budImagine the cracks disappearing and your heart becoming soft and filled with tenderness.
FatigueDark veil or wet sandClean cool streamImagine the veil disappearing and the flow of water cleansing and filling the body with freshness.
ShameBlack shadow or closed doorOpen gold frameImagine the door opening and a light appearing behind it, freeing you from heaviness.
HatredStorm or sharp rocksWarm, even lightImagine the storm subsiding and the sharp rocks melting into a soft, warm surface.
ConfusionLabyrinth or swarm of beesStraight pathImagine a maze straightening out into a straight path, or a swarm turning into a clear line of movement.
DoubtTurbid water or oscillating scalesA clear source of waterImagine the water becoming crystal clear and the scales becoming stable.
LovePink light or a blossoming flowerExpanding LightImagine the light or flower growing, spreading warmth and tenderness to you and the world around you.

How to use the table

  • Select an emotion.
    Ask yourself:"What emotion am I feeling right now?"
  • Create an image.
    Close your eyes and imagine the emotion in the form of the described visual image or come up with your own.
  • Transform the image.
    Use the transformation symbol from the table. Imagine the emotion changing its form and becoming a resource.
  • Feel the changes.
    After visualizing, pay attention to your state. You will feel more clarity, calm, or energy.
  • Secure the effect.
    Take a few deep breaths and mentally thank yourself for working with the emotion.

Example

Emotion:Fear

  • Image: dark cloud.
  • Symbol of transformation: clear sky.
  • Process: Imagine the cloud gradually dissolving, becoming light, and you see a clear blue sky.
  • Result: fear is reduced and a feeling of calm and confidence appears.

This table will become your practical tool for working with emotions and transforming them into a resource.
