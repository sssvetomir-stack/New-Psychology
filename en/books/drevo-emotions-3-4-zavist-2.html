3.4 Envy

EnvyEnvy is a feeling of dissatisfaction or a desire to possess what others have. It's often perceived as a negative emotion, but in the TREE concept, envy is seen as a sign of unfulfilled desires or hidden potential. When used correctly, envy can become a source of motivation and growth.

The nature of envy

  • The emotion of comparison
    Envy arises when a person compares himself with others and feels a lack in his life.
  • Pointer to desires
    Envy shows what exactly a person would like to have or achieve.
  • Energy of action
    If envy is recognized, its energy can be directed towards achieving goals and self-realization.

Why is it important to work with envy?

  • Awareness of your needs
    Envy helps you understand what you really lack.
  • Reducing internal stress
    Untreated envy creates feelings of inferiority that can destroy relationships and self-esteem.
  • Transformation into motivation
    Envy can be directed towards inspiration and movement towards your goals.

How to recognize envy?

  • Acknowledge a feeling
  • Tell yourself:"I feel jealous, and that's normal. It shows what's important to me."
  • Identify the source
  • Ask yourself:What or who am I envious of? What exactly is causing this feeling?
  • Separate the emotion and the other person's actions
  • Envy is not connected with other people's achievements, but with your inner desires.

Practice of working with envy

  • Exploring your desires
  • Determine what you want to achieve based on what you envy.
  • Ask yourself:What can I do to get closer to this?
  • Inspiration instead of comparison
  • Look at the person you envy as an example of what is possible.
  • Gratitude for your achievements
  • Write a list of your accomplishments and reflect on what you've already accomplished. This will help you see the value of your life.
  • Goal setting
  • Transform envy into a concrete action plan. For example, if you're envious of someone else's career success, develop steps for your own professional growth.

An example of working with envy

  • SituationYou envy a friend who has opened a successful business.
  • Awareness: Envy indicates your desire to realize yourself in the professional sphere.
  • ActionYou analyze what exactly inspires you about his success and create a plan to develop your ideas.

Meditation for dealing with envy

  • Sit in a quiet place, close your eyes and take a few deep breaths.
  • Imagine the person you envy and what he has.
  • Say about yourself:"I'm happy for you and grateful that you show me new possibilities."
  • Imagine this feeling of envy transforming into a warm light that fills you with energy and inspiration.

What to Avoid When Dealing with Envy

  • Self-condemnation
  • Envy is a natural emotion, condemnation only intensifies internal conflict.
  • Actions out of envy
  • Don't let envy push you to try to put someone else down or belittle their achievements.
  • Ignoring your needs
  • Envy indicates something that needs your attention. Don't ignore these signals.

Envy as a resource in the TREE

  • Deepening self-knowledgeEnvy helps to reveal hidden desires and dreams.
  • Motivation for action: It becomes the driving force for setting goals and achieving them.
  • Improving relationshipsWorking with envy develops the ability to be happy for others.

Conclusion

Envy is not an enemy, but a clue that helps you recognize your desires and inspires development. In the TREE concept, envy becomes a resource for personal growth if you work with it consciously and channel its energy into creation. It helps you see opportunities, develop inner confidence, and create a harmonious space for self-realization.
