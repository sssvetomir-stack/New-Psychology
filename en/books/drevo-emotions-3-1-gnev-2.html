3.1 Anger

AngerAnger is a powerful emotion that arises when a person experiences a violation of their boundaries, injustice, or unmet expectations. The TREE concept views anger as a powerful source of energy that, with conscious effort, can be channeled toward creating and protecting one's values.

The nature of anger

  • Anger as the energy of action
  • Anger mobilizes the body, giving strength and determination to overcome obstacles.
  • This energy can be destructive if not recognized, or creative if directed correctly.
  • Anger as a signal
  • Anger points to what's important to a person. It's often associated with the violation of boundaries, values, or a sense of injustice.

Why is it important to work with anger?

  • Avoiding destructive consequences
  • Unprocessed anger can cause conflict, worsen relationships, and lead to internal tension.
  • Transformation into a resource
  • Conscious anger becomes a force that helps you take action, defend your rights and achieve your goals.
  • Harmony with yourself and others
  • Working with anger improves the ability to communicate constructively and reduces stress levels.

Practice working with anger

  • Awareness of anger
  • Ask yourself:What am I feeling? What is causing my anger? What boundaries have been violated?
  • Acknowledge your emotion:"I'm angry, and that's okay. Anger is a signal I can use."
  • Space for emotion
  • Find a safe place to vent:
  • Take deep breaths, focusing on your body.
  • Write down on paper what exactly made you angry.
  • Physical release of energy
  • Do some active movements: running, exercising, or hitting a pillow. This helps relieve tension.
  • Transforming anger into action
  • Ask yourself:What can I do to improve the situation?
  • Channel your anger into creating an action plan or improving your relationship.

An example of working with anger

  • SituationA colleague took your project without permission.
  • Awareness: You feel angry because your efforts were not recognized.
  • Acceptance: You realize that your anger signals a violation of your boundaries.
  • ActionYou calmly discuss the situation with your colleague, explaining your feelings and agreeing on new rules of interaction.

Meditation for dealing with anger

  • Sit comfortably, close your eyes and take a few deep breaths.
  • Imagine your anger as a red stream of energy.
  • Watch it move within you without judging it.
  • Imagine this flow transforming into light or warmth that fills you with strength and calm.

What to Avoid When Working with Anger

  • Suppression of emotion
  • Suppressed anger can develop into chronic stress or erupt into an uncontrollable outburst.
  • Impulsive reactions
  • Avoid aggressive behavior before understanding the cause of anger and its consequences.
  • Transfer to others
  • Don't take your anger out on people who are not involved in the situation.

Anger as a resource in TREE

  • Anger can become a source of inner strength and confidence.
  • It teaches you to protect your boundaries and values.
  • Through awareness of anger, a person learns to consciously express his needs, not by destroying, but by creating.

Conclusion

Anger is a natural emotion that can be both destructive and creative. Consciously working with anger helps channel its energy toward protecting one's boundaries, achieving goals, and improving one's quality of life. The TREE concept views anger as an important stage of inner growth and harmony with oneself and the world around us.
