Accepting the emotion and understanding its source

Accepting the emotion and recognizing its source is the second step in working with feelings, which helps you recognize their value rather than suppress them. Instead of fighting yourself, you learn to accept your emotions as a natural reaction, and understanding their cause allows you to address the root cause of the problem.

Why accept emotions?

  • Reducing internal resistance
  • When you deny your feelings, it increases tension and creates internal conflict.
  • Acceptance gives you the opportunity to release this resistance.
  • Understanding yourself
  • Emotions are signals that indicate your needs, desires, or unmet expectations.
  • Creating space for transformation
  • Only by accepting the emotion can you begin to work with it and direct its energy in a constructive direction.

Steps to accepting emotions

1. Acknowledge the emotion

  • Tell yourself:
  • "I feel anger."
  • "This is sadness."
  • "It's fear."
  • By acknowledging an emotion, you create the basis for accepting it.

2. Remind yourself that emotion is natural

  • Every emotion has its place and meaning. Tell yourself:
  • "It's normal that I feel this way."
  • "Emotions come and go, it's a part of life."

3. Allow yourself to feel

  • Instead of suppressing an emotion, allow yourself to experience it. This doesn't mean indulging the emotion, but rather allowing it to be:
  • "I'll allow myself to be sad now."
  • "This anger tells me that my boundaries have been violated."

How to understand the source of emotion?

1. Ask yourself questions

  • Ask yourself:
  • "Why do I feel this?"
  • "What caused this emotion?"
  • "What events or thoughts triggered it?"

2. Look for internal and external causes

  • Sometimes an emotion is caused by external circumstances (events, words of other people), and sometimes by internal expectations or beliefs.

3. Deepen your awareness

  • Ask yourself clarifying questions:
  • "What needs were not met?"
  • "What belief of mine could have provoked this reaction?"

Practices for accepting and understanding the source of emotion

1. Observer Technique

  • Sit in a quiet environment, close your eyes, and focus on your emotion. Imagine yourself observing it from the outside, without judgment.
  • Ask yourself:
  • "What does this emotion want to tell me?"
  • "How can I answer her?"

2. Emotional journal

  • Write down your emotions, their possible causes, and the situations in which they arose. This will help you see patterns.
  • Example entry:
  • Emotion: Anger. Reason: A colleague didn't take my opinion into account at a meeting. Expectation: I wanted to be heard.

3. Meditation on acceptance

  • Sit comfortably, close your eyes and tell yourself:
  • "I feel this emotion, and I accept it. It's here to teach me something."

4. Ask yourself the question "So what?"

  • If an emotion feels strong or scary, try simplifying it.
  • "I'm angry. So what? It's just a feeling. It'll pass soon."

An example of working with emotion

Situation: You feel angry because a friend forgot about an important agreement.

  • Acceptance:
  • "I feel angry, and that's normal. I'm disappointed because it's important to me to be treated with respect."
  • Understanding the source:
  • "I'm angry because I expected my friend to keep his promise. I need to discuss this with him to clarify the situation."

Tips for Effectively Accepting Emotions

  • Avoid judging yourself
  • Remind yourself: emotions do not make you weak or a bad person.
  • Give yourself time
  • Don't rush the acceptance process. Sometimes emotions take longer to process.
  • Separate emotions and actions
  • Accept the emotion, but don't rush to act under its influence.

Conclusion

Accepting an emotion and recognizing its source helps you see the true meaning behind the feeling. This gives you the ability not only to cope with emotions but also to use them to better understand yourself, your needs, and your boundaries. When you accept your emotions, you take responsibility for your state, opening the way to inner freedom and harmony.
