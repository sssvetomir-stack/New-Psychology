4. Practices and methods

The practices and methods within the TREE concept represent a set of tools that help one recognize, accept, and transform emotions. These approaches are aimed at restoring inner balance, developing mindfulness, and harmonizing relationships with oneself and the world around us.

4.1 Individual practices

Individual practicesallow a person to focus on themselves, their emotions and needs, helping to more deeply understand and work through internal states.

  • Mindfulness Diary
  • Write down your emotions, their causes and reactions to them.
  • Keep records of how you cope with difficult situations and document your successes.
  • Emotional Awareness Meditation
  • Sit in a comfortable position, focus on your breathing and allow your emotions to emerge.
  • Observe them without judging or trying to change them.
  • Visualization of transformation
  • Imagine your emotions as images (for example, light, water, fire).
  • Visualize them changing shape, color, or intensity, becoming part of your power.
  • Breathing techniques
  • Use deep breathing or rhythmic breathing practices to reduce stress and emotional tension.
  • Example: “4-7-8” technique (inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, exhale for 8 seconds).
  • Body practices
  • Perform body awareness movements (such as yoga, gymnastics, or stretching). This helps release trapped emotional energy.

4.2 Collective circles and rituals

Collective practicescreate a safe space for the expression of emotions, mutual support and harmonization within the group.

  • Circles of trust
  • Participants gather to share their feelings and experiences.
  • Everyone is given the opportunity to speak without interruption or judgment.
  • Shared rituals of transformation
  • Organize rituals to work with specific emotions (for example, a ritual of forgiveness or letting go of grievances).
  • Use symbolic actions such as lighting candles, throwing paper with written grievances into fire or water.
  • Energy practices in a group
  • Joint breathing or movement exercises to work with collective energy.
  • Meditations for unification and harmonization of the group.

4.3 Meditations and visualizations

Meditations and visualizationsallow you to delve deeper into the awareness of your emotions and state, directing their energy into a creative channel.

  • Meditation on Acceptance
  • Focus on the emotion that is causing you discomfort.
  • Imagine it as a warm stream that fills you, and say to yourself:"I accept this feeling and learn from it."
  • Meditation on Forgiveness
  • Visualize the person or situation that caused the pain and imagine yourself releasing that energy, finding inner peace.
  • Visualization of the desired state
  • Imagine yourself in a state of harmony, confidence, or joy. Feel how this affects your body and mind.

4.4 Energy and body exercises

These exercises help release stagnant emotions and restore harmony to the body and mind.

  • Grounding practices
  • Standing barefoot on the ground, feel your connection with the earth. Imagine it absorbing your negative energy and filling you with strength.
  • Dynamic Meditation
  • Use active movement (such as dancing or running) to release pent-up emotions.
  • Working with the elements
  • The elements of nature (water, fire, air and earth) can become tools for transforming emotions.
  • Example: standing in the wind, imagine that it is carrying away your fears and doubts.
  • Gymnastics and other physical practices
  • Perform movements that increase the flow of energy in the body, helping you to become aware of and transform emotions.

4.5 Integration into everyday life

  • Conscious pauses
  • Take short breaks throughout the day to become aware of your state.
  • Ask yourself:"What am I feeling right now? What do I need?"
  • Gratitude practice
  • Keep a gratitude list to help you focus on the positive aspects of your life.
  • Small actions for harmony
  • Incorporate simple joys into your day: a cup of tea, a walk, reading.

Conclusion

The practices and methods of the TREE concept help you consciously work with emotions, transforming them from a source of discomfort into a resource for growth. Individual exercises, collective rituals, and integration into everyday life create a harmonious space for growth, strengthening inner balance, and deep self-knowledge.
