The law of life

Life strives for development.

Every living organism has an internal drive for growth, adaptation and self-preservation.

Development is a natural state of man.

Stopping development leads to gradual degradation.

Law of Integrity

A human being is a single system.

The body, subconscious, psyche, consciousness, superconsciousness, soul, spirit, values, social connections and the environment constantly influence each other.

It is impossible to change one part of the system without affecting the others.

The law of cause and effect

  • Every thought influences emotions.
  • Emotions influence decisions.
  • Decisions become actions.
  • Actions become habits.
  • Habits shape character.
  • Character determines a person's life path.

The Law of Awareness

  • Development begins with observation.
  • A person can change only what he can understand.
  • Mindfulness turns automatic life into free choice.

The Law of Will

Willpower is the ability to control oneself and make conscious choices.

The greater a person's ability to control his thoughts, emotions and actions, the greater his inner freedom.

Law of Responsibility

Freedom is impossible without responsibility.

Every decision has consequences.

A person becomes mature when he accepts responsibility for his thoughts, words, actions and their impact on other people and the world around him.

The Law of Harmony

True development does not destroy the system.

The development of the body should not come at the cost of the destruction of the psyche.

Personal development should not destroy the family.

Economic development should not destroy nature.

Harmony is the ability to develop while maintaining the stability of the entire system.

The Law of Continuous Learning

  • A person develops throughout his life.
  • Every new experience becomes a source of knowledge.
  • Every mistake becomes an opportunity for growth.
  • Learning ends only with life.

The Law of Overcoming

  • Growth is impossible without overcoming difficulties.
  • Challenges are not the opposite of growth.
  • They are a natural part of it.

By overcoming obstacles, a person develops strength, wisdom and resilience.

Law of Cooperation

  • A person reveals his potential through interaction with other people.
  • Competition promotes the development of individual abilities.
  • Cooperation promotes the development of civilization.

The Law of Psychological Ecology

A person's inner world depends on the environment in which he lives.

Information, relationships, culture, nature and technology constantly shape the state of the psyche.

Taking care of your internal and external environment is a necessary condition for psychological health.

The Law of Creation

The highest form of development is creation.

Human development becomes complete when he begins to create not only for himself, but also for others.

Creation transforms knowledge into culture, work into heritage, and life into a source of social development.

The Law of Destiny

Each person has a unique combination of abilities, experiences and values.

Purpose is revealed when a person uses his abilities for the development of life, society and future generations.

Law of Inheritance

The true value of life is determined not by the amount accumulated, but by what a person leaves behind.

  • Knowledge.
  • Children.
  • Culture.
  • Scientific discoveries.
  • Good works.
  • Created communities.
  • Restored nature.
  • True development continues even after man's departure.

The Main Law of New Psychology DREVO

A person develops harmoniously when each of his decisions simultaneously strengthens him, benefits other people and contributes to the preservation and development of life in all its manifestations.

This is precisely the path of a mature individual, a creative society and a sustainable civilization.

Psychology of the Future

From the science of problems to the science of development

The psychology of the future studies not only the causes of suffering, but also the laws of human development.

Its goal is not simply to help people adapt to the existing world, but to reveal their ability to create a healthier, more just and harmonious world.

A person is viewed not as an object of treatment, but as an active participant in his own life and the development of society.

Man as a living system

The psychology of the future considers a person as an integral system in which the following are interconnected:

  • body;→
  • subconscious;→
  • psyche;→
  • consciousness;→
  • will;→
  • superconsciousness;→
  • soul;→
  • spirit;→
  • values ​​and meaning;→
  • social connections;→
  • environment.→

The development of one level inevitably affects all the others.

Therefore, it is impossible to understand a person by studying only the brain, only behavior, or only the social environment.

From personality to ecosystem

A person never develops alone.

His health depends on:

  • families;
  • culture;→
  • education;
  • nature;→
  • economics;
  • technologies;→
  • quality of human relationships.

Future psychology views the individual as part of a broader ecosystem of life.

Therefore, caring for people is impossible without caring for society and the environment.

Science and openness to new ideas

The psychology of the future is based on the achievements of modern science:

  • psychology;
  • neuroscience;
  • biology;
  • medicine;
  • pedagogy;
  • sociology.

At the same time, it remains open to exploring new questions about the nature of consciousness, creativity, subjective experience, and other phenomena that have not yet received a full scientific explanation.

A new idea becomes part of science only after it has been tested, reproducible, and critically analyzed.

Development instead of correction

The main goal of the psychology of the future is not to make a person “normal”.

The main goal is to help him become more mature, free and capable of creation.

Psychological development includes:

  • health promotion;
  • development of thinking;
  • education of will;
  • disclosure of abilities;
  • development of emotional maturity;
  • strengthening relationships;
  • formation of responsibility;
  • search for meaning;
  • realization of purpose.

Psychology of creation

The highest form of development becomes the ability to create.

Create:

  • knowledge;→
  • culture;→
  • technologies;→
  • family;→
  • communities;
  • works of art;
  • scientific discoveries;
  • environmental projects;
  • conditions for the development of future generations.

Creation becomes a natural continuation of inner growth.

Man and technology

Artificial intelligence, robotics and biotechnology will change the life of humanity.

Therefore, the psychology of the future must answer not only the question:

  • "How does a person interact with a person?"
  • but also to the questions:
  • "How does a person interact with artificial intelligence?"
  • "What qualities will remain uniquely human?"

Technology should expand human capabilities, not replace freedom of choice, responsibility and individual dignity.

Psychology of the Future Civilization

The development of the individual is inseparable from the development of civilization.

True progress is not determined solely by economic growth and technological advances.

It is determined by the fact that

how much people become:

  • healthier;
  • more free;
  • more responsible;
  • more cooperative;
  • more creative.

It is the quality of a person that determines the quality of the future society.

DREVO's Vision

The new psychology of DREVO views humans as living, self-developing systems capable of consciously participating in the development of life.

Its goal is to combine the achievements of science, practical experience and human philosophical understanding into a single system of knowledge.

It does not contrast body and consciousness, personality and society, man and nature, technology and humanism.

She strives to understand the laws by which life develops and to help people live in harmony with these laws.

The main goal

The psychology of the future is the science of unlocking human potential, strengthening the individual, developing society, and preserving life in all its manifestations.

  • Her main question is not:
  • "What's wrong with man?"
  • A:

“What can a person become if conditions are created for his harmonious development?”

From the psychology of survival to the psychology of creation

Throughout most of its history, man has been learning to survive.

The struggle for food, safety, and protection of family and clan shaped many of our psychological mechanisms. Fear, anxiety, aggression, vigilance, and the desire to control the surrounding world helped us maintain life in the face of constant danger.

Modern psychology pays close attention to these mechanisms. It studies trauma, stress, anxiety disorders, depression, addictions, and other conditions that reduce quality of life. This approach is of great value because it helps people restore inner balance and overcome suffering.

However, recovery is not yet development.

A person who has freed himself from fear does not necessarily become a creator.

A person who has overcome depression does not necessarily find the meaning of life.

A person who has learned to cope with stress does not necessarily begin to create something valuable for the world.

Therefore, New Psychology DREVO suggests taking the next step.

Its task is not only to help a person survive, but also to help him learn to live a full life.

The psychology of creation begins where the struggle for simple survival ends.

  • She asks new questions.
  • Not only:
  • "How to get rid of suffering?"
  • but also:
  • "How to reveal your abilities?"
  • "How to learn to love?"
  • "How to create?"
  • "How can I leave the world better than I found it?"

In the psychology of survival, protection becomes the most important thing.

  • In the psychology of creation, development becomes the main thing.
  • Survival psychology teaches us to avoid danger.
  • The psychology of creation teaches us to create opportunities.
  • Survival psychology seeks to preserve life.
  • The psychology of creation seeks to reveal its potential.

This doesn't mean abandoning the understanding of trauma, pain, or illness. On the contrary, helping someone overcome suffering remains a necessary step. But it is seen as the beginning of the journey, not its final destination.

The new psychology of DREVO considers man as a creative being.

The highest manifestation of maturity is not the ability to defend oneself, but the ability to create.

  • Create knowledge.
  • Create culture.
  • Create families.
  • Create communities.
  • Create technologies.
  • To create beauty.

Create conditions in which other people and future generations can develop.

True development begins when a person stops living only by the question of his own survival and begins to recognize himself as a participant in the development of life.

The main goal of the New Psychology DREVO is to help people move from survival to creation, from reaction to conscious choice, from fear to responsibility, from consumption to creativity, from the struggle for life to participation in its development.

The psychology of the future is not only the science of how to reduce suffering.

This is the science of how to develop a person so that he becomes a source of development for himself, society, nature and future generations.
