It is impossible to create a stable external world if the internal world is in ruins.

  • Therefore, man's first creation is himself.
  • It creates:
  • your character;
  • your habits;
  • your worldview;
  • your culture of communication.
  • Personality becomes the main work of a person.

Seven levels of creation

Self-creation

Development of body, psyche, consciousness, will, soul and spirit.

Building relationships

  • Love.
  • Friendship.
  • Family.
  • Trust.

Creation of knowledge

  • Training.
  • Research.
  • Transfer of experience.
  • Teaching.

Creation of labor

  • Useful work.
  • Craft.
  • Entrepreneurship.
  • Engineering.
  • Science.

Creation of culture

  • Music.
  • Art.
  • Language.
  • Traditions.
  • Beauty.

Building a society

  • Communities.
  • Education.
  • Social projects.
  • Cooperation.

Creating the future

  • Ecology.
  • Raising children.
  • New technologies.
  • Caring for future generations.

What destroys creation?

Creation is hindered by:

  • indifference;
  • constant consumption without return;
  • fear of mistakes;
  • envy;
  • destructive thinking;
  • refusal to take responsibility;
  • lack of long-term goals.

Destruction often occurs faster than creation.

But it is creation that ensures the sustainable development of life.

Creation and happiness

A person rarely experiences deep satisfaction from consumption alone.

The most lasting feeling of fulfillment arises when a person sees the results of his labor and understands that his activity is beneficial.

Therefore, creation becomes one of the main sources of happiness.

Practice of the Law of Creation

  • Every day it is useful to ask yourself:
  • What new did I create today?
  • Who did it benefit?
  • What has become better thanks to my actions?
  • What skills did I develop today?
  • What contribution have I made to the future?
  • Even a small creative act gradually changes personality.

The main principle

Life develops through creation.

A person discovers his purpose not when he only takes from the world, but when he begins to give and create something.

Definition

The Law of Creation is a fundamental law of the New Psychology DREVO, according to which the highest form of personal development is manifested in the ability to create values ​​that contribute to the development of man, society, nature, and future generations.

Creation is a natural extension of awareness, will, responsibility, and cooperation. It transforms a person's inner potential into real change in the world and makes their life a source of benefit and legacy.

Like a tree that draws strength from the earth not for itself, but to grow its crown, provide shade, fruit, and new seeds, a person reaches maturity when he begins to create more life than he consumes.

The Law of Destiny

The Law of Purpose in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, purpose is not seen as a predetermined fate or as the only “ideal profession”.

Purpose is the direction in which a person most fully reveals his abilities, realizes his values ​​and brings the greatest benefit to life.

It is not found by chance.

It is gradually revealed through personal development, experience, choice and creative activity.

Purpose is the path that a person builds through his actions.

What is the Law of Destiny?

The Law of Purpose is a fundamental principle according to which the harmonious development of an individual occurs when their abilities, values, interests, and activities are combined into a creative direction that benefits the individual, society, and future generations.

  • Purpose answers not only the question:
  • "What do I do?"
  • It answers the question:
  • "What do I live for?"

The purpose is revealed gradually

  • Many people expect a sudden epiphany.
  • But more often than not, the purpose becomes clearer step by step.
  • Through training.
  • Through mistakes.
  • Through new projects.
  • Through service to people.
  • Through experience.
  • Each stage of life opens a new part of the path.
  • Therefore, purpose is a process of unfolding, not a one-time discovery.

Four Components of Purpose

Capabilities

What am I best at?

What talents can I develop?

Values

What is really important to me?

What principles do I want to remain true to?

Benefit

How can my knowledge and abilities make other people's lives better?

The joy of creation

What activities fill me with energy and a sense of meaning even when they require effort?

When these four elements come together, a person begins to feel his direction.

What prevents you from finding your purpose?

Often a person is disturbed by:

  • fear of making a mistake;
  • the desire to meet other people's expectations;
  • living only for material gain;
  • lack of self-knowledge;
  • passivity;
  • refusal to try new things.

Purpose is not revealed to one who does nothing.

It is revealed in activity.

The Purpose and Ten Levels of Man

  • Purpose unites all levels of personality.
  • Bodygives strength to act.
  • Subconsciouscreates habits that support the path.
  • Psychehelps maintain stability.
  • Consciousnessallows you to understand the world and learn.
  • Will helps not to stop.
  • Soulfills activities with love and sincerity.
  • Spiritmaintains direction even in difficult times.
  • Valuesdetermine the meaning of the path.

Social connectionshelp to realize one's destiny together with other people.

Naturereminds that human activity should support life, not destroy it.

Purpose and profession

Profession can be part of destiny.

But it does not always coincide with it.

The same doctor can treat people only for the sake of earning money or see his work as a service to life.

The same engineer can simply carry out tasks or create technologies that change the future.

Purpose is determined not by the name of the profession, but by the meaning that a person puts into his or her activities.

Practice asked for purposes

  • It is useful to regularly ask yourself questions:
  • What do I like to do?
  • What am I best at?
  • What do people thank me for?
  • What problems in the world do I want to solve?
  • What am I willing to do even when it's difficult?
  • What do I want to leave behind?
  • The answers gradually take shape into a direction for life.

The main principle

Purpose does not come to a person from outside.

It is revealed when a person develops, acts and begins to use his abilities for the benefit of life.

Definition

The Law of Purpose is a fundamental law of the New Psychology DREVO, according to which a person achieves the greatest fullness of development when he or she reveals his or her abilities, lives in accordance with his or her values, and uses his or her potential for creation and service to life.

Purpose is the natural result of harmonious personal development. It connects a person's inner potential with the needs of the world, transforming personal success into a long-term contribution to the development of society and future generations.

Like a tree that does not choose whether to bear fruit, but reveals its nature through growth, a person reveals his purpose when he becomes what he is capable of being and begins to benefit everything around him.

Law of Inheritance

The Law of Inheritance in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, human development does not end with personal achievements.

  • The highest stage of maturity is the creation of a legacy.
  • Every person receives something from previous generations:
  • life;
  • language;
  • culture;
  • knowledge;
  • experience;
  • nature;
  • society.

But life reaches its fullness when a person begins not only to receive, but also to transmit.

Thus, he becomes a link between the past and the future.

What is the Law of Inheritance?

The Law of Legacy is a fundamental principle that holds that the true value of life is determined by what a person leaves behind and what continues to benefit people, nature, and future generations.

Heritage is the natural culmination of a creative life.

The legacy begins today

  • Many people think that legacy is created at the end of life.
  • In fact, it is born every day.
  • Every decision.
  • Every action.
  • Every word.
  • Every student.
  • Every tree planted.
  • Every idea conveyed.
  • Every completed project becomes a part of the future.

Seven Forms of Heritage

  • Children.
  • Health of future generations.
  • Continuation of life.

Moral heritage

  • Honesty.
  • Love.
  • Responsibility.
  • Personal example.
  • Culture of behavior.

Intellectual heritage

  • Books.
  • Research.
  • Inventions.
  • Knowledge.
  • Teaching.

Creative legacy

  • Projects.
  • Organizations.
  • Enterprises.
  • Architecture.
  • Technologies.
  • Works of art.

Social heritage

  • Created communities.
  • Culture of cooperation.
  • Trust.
  • Traditions of mutual assistance.

Environmental heritage

  • Planted forests.
  • Restored ecosystems.
  • Preserved natural resources.
  • Careful attitude towards life.

Spiritual heritage

  • Wisdom.
  • Philosophy.
  • Ideas.
  • Inspiration.
  • The meaning of life.
  • Something that helps other people become better.

Heritage and Purpose

  • Purpose answers the question:
  • "What do I live for?"
  • Heritage responds:
  • "What will live because of me?"

When a person connects these two issues, his life begins to go beyond personal success.

Legacy and Immortality

In the New Psychology of DREVO, immortality is understood not only as a philosophical or religious idea.

  • It manifests itself in the continuation of human influence.
  • People continue to live:
  • in your children;
  • in students;
  • in the created culture;
  • in knowledge;
  • in good deeds;
  • in projects;
  • in a changed nature;
  • in the lives of those they helped.
  • Thus, heritage becomes an extension of the individual.
