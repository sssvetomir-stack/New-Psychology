In the New Psychology DREVO, a person is viewed as not only a biochemical, but also a bioelectrical system.

  • Every cell in the body is involved in electrical processes.
  • The nervous system transmits signals using electrical impulses.
  • The heart creates powerful electrical activity.
  • The brain constantly generates electrical rhythms.

Muscles, internal organs and other tissues also have their own bioelectrical activity.

The combination of these processes forms the electromagnetic fields of the body, which can be recorded by modern scientific methods.

As part of the New Psychology DREVO human biofieldis considered as one of the manifestations of its physiological state and the interaction of the organism with the environment.

We use the term"aura"as a philosophical and cultural designation of the integral human field, understanding that its properties and boundaries require further research.

Thus, DREVO's New Psychology does not claim that all traditional notions about the aura have already been scientifically confirmed. It proceeds from the premise that humans possess measurable bioelectrical activity, while the question of its more complex manifestations remains open to scientific study.

Practical principle

Changes in the state of the body, psyche and lifestyle are reflected in the physiological processes of the body, including its bioelectrical activity.

Therefore, caring for the health of the body, emotional state, quality of sleep, movement, breathing, relationships and the environment is simultaneously caring for the holistic state of a person.

The more harmoniously the body functions, the more coordinated its internal physiological processes become. Studying this coordination is one of the promising goals of DREVO's New Psychology.

Principle of biofield interaction

In the New Psychology of DREVO, a person is viewed as an open living system, continuously interacting with the surrounding world and other people.

Each organism creates its own bioelectrical and biomagnetic activity associated with the functioning of the heart, brain, nervous system and other organs.

When people meet, several levels of interaction occur simultaneously:

  • biological;
  • psychological;
  • social;
  • informational;
  • and, according to the DREVO research hypothesis, biofield.

Under biofield interactionThe possible influence of physiological electromagnetic processes in one organism on another is understood. This hypothesis requires further experimental verification and is not considered an established scientific fact.

Regardless of the outcome of such studies, it is clear that people are capable of synchronizing through breathing, speech rhythm, movement, facial expression, attention, emotions, and shared activities. These processes create a sense of mutual understanding, trust, or, conversely, tension.

The new psychology of DREVO suggests that a harmonious human state promotes healthier interactions with others. Therefore, personal development is viewed not only as an internal process but also as a contribution to the quality of relationships and the environment in which others live.

The principle of mutual influence

Every person influences others with their state of mind, words, actions and lifestyle.

Every interaction leaves a trace.

Therefore, by developing one's own integrity, a person simultaneously contributes to the development of his community.

In DREVO's New Psychology, personal health is viewed not as an isolated state, but as part of a broader ecology of human relationships, in which the internal state of one person can affect the well-being of others through a multitude of interconnected mechanisms, some of which have already been well studied, while others still await scientific explanation.

Biofield, aura and horizontal flows

Biofield in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, a person is viewed as an open living system, constantly exchanging information, matter, heat, signals, and emotional states with the outside world.

The body has measurable bioelectrical activity.

The work of the heart, brain, nervous system, muscles and internal organs is accompanied by electrical and magnetic processes.

The combination of these processes in the DREVO model is calledhuman biofield.

However, the biofield is not considered as a separate mystical substance, but as one of the levels of manifestation of the holistic state of the organism.

What is an aura?

In the New Psychology DREVO, the aura is considered as an external area of ​​manifestation of a person’s state.

It can be understood as a combination of several simultaneously existing factors:

  • bioelectric and biomagnetic processes;
  • thermal radiation of the body;
  • breath and odors;
  • postures, movements and facial expressions;
  • emotional state;
  • voice and rhythm of speech;
  • attention;
  • psychological influence on others.

Thus, the aura is not only a supposed energy field.

This is the holistic space of a person’s presence, through which others perceive his state.

A person may not yet utter a single word, but others already notice his tension, calmness, openness, fear, strength or fatigue.

Biofield and aura

In the DREVO model, biofield and aura are related, but not completely identical concepts.

Biofieldrefers primarily to the internal physiological and bioelectrical processes of the body.

Aurais a broader concept and describes how a person's holistic state manifests itself around him and is perceived by other people.

  • You can say:
  • the biofield is created by the vital activity of the organism;
  • The aura is an image of its external manifestation.

Horizontal flows

Horizontal flows are the exchange between a person and the surrounding world.

They are aimed at:

  • from person to person;
  • from individual to family;
  • from individual to community;
  • from society to the individual;
  • from man to nature;
  • from the environment to humans.

While the vertical flow in the DREVO model is associated with spirit, purpose, and direction toward higher values, the horizontal flows relate to earthly life, relationships, and exchange.

  • The vertical answers the question:
  • “What higher thing am I connected with and what do I live for?”
  • The horizontal answers:
  • “Who and what do I interact with in this world?”

Types of horizontal flows

Physical flow

Exchange of heat, touch, movement, substances and physiological signals.

Emotional flow

People transmit mood, anxiety, calmness, joy, confidence and tension to each other.

This happens through voice, facial expression, behavior and quality of presence.

Information flow

Exchange of words, knowledge, images, ideas and symbols.

Through it, culture, learning and collective intelligence are formed.

Social Stream

Exchange of support, labor, trust, responsibility and resources.

Biofield flow

The DREVO philosophical research model suggests that bioelectrical and biomagnetic processes of organisms can be one of the additional elements of human interaction.

The extent and mechanism of such influence require further scientific study.

Aura and the human condition

  • The aura is not permanent.
  • It changes along with the person's condition.
  • It can be influenced by:
  • dream;
  • health;→
  • breath;
  • stress;
  • nutrition;
  • physical activity;
  • emotional experiences;
  • relationship;→
  • environment;→
  • inner meaning;→
  • meditation and state of attention.

A calm, healthy, and internally consistent person is usually perceived differently than a person who is exhausted, frightened, or aggressive.

This does not necessarily require a mystical explanation.

Much of this perception can occur through known physiological and psychological cues.

Interaction of auras

When people are close to each other, their spaces of presence overlap.

They begin to influence each other through:

  • breath;
  • voice;
  • sight;
  • movement;
  • touch;
  • emotional state;
  • attention;
  • expectations;
  • trust or fear.
  • Common conditions may arise within a group.
  • For example:
  • calmness;
  • voltage;
  • inspiration;
  • aggression;
  • trust;
  • a sense of unity.

In the DREVO model this can be described as the interaction and coordination of auras and horizontal flows.

Aura boundaries

  • A healthy aura does not mean maximum openness to everything.
  • A person needs psychological and physical boundaries.
  • Boundaries help:
  • distinguish between your own and other people's emotions;
  • preserve your own values;
  • do not give in to constant pressure;
  • choose with whom and how to interact;
  • protect attention and internal state.

A strong aura does not mean dominance over others.

It is the ability to maintain integrity in interaction.

Harmonization of horizontal flows

Horizontal flows become healthy when the exchange is based on:

  • respect;→
  • honesty;
  • freedom;→
  • reciprocity;
  • responsibility;→
  • clear boundaries;
  • cooperation;→
  • creation.→

Destructive currents arise where:

  • manipulation;
  • humiliation;
  • violence;
  • constant fear;
  • exploitation;
  • lie;
  • emotional infection of hatred.

Therefore, psychological ecology includes concern not only for the internal state, but also for the quality of exchange between people.

Vertical and horizontal

A holistic person exists at the intersection of two directions.

Vertical flowconnects it with spirit, meaning, purpose and higher values.

Horizontal flowsconnect him with people, society, nature and the material world.

Without vertical, a person can lose direction.

Without horizontal line - connection with real life.

Harmony arises when inner values ​​are expressed in relationships and actions.

The main principle

  • The aura reflects a person's state.
  • Horizontal flows show the quality of its exchange with the world.
  • The vertical flow sets the direction.

The biofield connects these processes with the vital activity of the organism.

The more the body, psyche, consciousness, soul and spirit are aligned, the more integral a person’s presence becomes.

Definition

The biofield is the totality of the body's bioelectric and biomagnetic processes. The aura is the holistic space of the external manifestation of a person's physical, emotional, psychological, and philosophical state. Horizontal flows are the processes of exchange between the individual, other people, society, and nature.

In the New Psychology DREVO, these concepts form a unified model of interaction between the individual and the world.

The vertical gives a person direction. The horizontal creates relationships. The aura reveals the inner state. The biofield reflects the life of the organism. In their coherence, a person's holistic presence in the world is born.
