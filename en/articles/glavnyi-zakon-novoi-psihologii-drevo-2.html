A person develops harmoniously when each of his decisions simultaneously strengthens him, benefits other people and contributes to the preservation and development of life in all its manifestations.

This is precisely the path of a mature individual, a creative society and a sustainable civilization.

The law of life

The Law of Life in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, life is viewed as a continuous process of development, interaction and creation.

  • All living things are born.
  • It's growing.
  • Studies.
  • Adapts.
  • Creates.
  • Passes on experience.
  • Leaves a legacy.
  • And becomes part of the future.
  • This process can be called the Law of Life.

It manifests itself in nature, in the development of man, family, society and civilization.

What is the Law of Life?

The law of life is a natural principle according to which all living things strive to preserve life, develop, interact with the surrounding world, create something new, and pass on the results of their existence to future generations.

Life does not exist in stillness.

It exists in constant development.

Five Manifestations of the Law of Life

Preservation of life

Every living organism strives to maintain its existence.

Therefore, caring for health, safety and well-being is a natural part of life.

Development

  • Life is constantly changing.
  • The child becomes an adult.
  • The seed becomes a tree.
  • Knowledge becomes wisdom.
  • The cessation of development gradually leads to the loss of vitality.

Cooperation

  • No person develops completely alone.
  • Life exists through interaction.
  • Family.
  • Community.
  • Nature.
  • Ecosystems.
  • Cooperation is as natural a law of life as development.

Creation

  • Life not only preserves itself.
  • She creates something new.
  • Children.
  • Ideas.
  • Culture.
  • Technologies.
  • Works of art.
  • Good deeds.
  • Creation becomes a natural continuation of development.

Heritage

  • Life goes on through transmission.
  • Transfer of knowledge.
  • Experience.
  • Values.
  • Cultures.
  • Caring for nature.

Each generation receives peace from the previous one and passes it on to the next.

The law of life and man

  • A person becomes truly alive when he participates in all five processes.
  • He takes care of his health.
  • Developing.
  • Builds relationships.
  • Creates something new.
  • Leaves behind a legacy.

If even one of these directions disappears, life gradually loses harmony.

The law of life and happiness

  • Happiness arises when a person lives in accordance with the Law of life.
  • It is developing.
  • Loves.
  • Creates.
  • Helps others.
  • Feels the meaning of his existence.

Therefore, happiness is not a separate goal, but a natural consequence of life lived in accordance with its laws.

The Law of Life and Civilization

  • This law does not apply only to the individual.
  • It also applies to society.
  • A civilization becomes sustainable when:
  • preserves life;
  • develops education and science;
  • strengthens cooperation;
  • creates new opportunities;
  • cares about nature;
  • thinks about future generations.

Violation of these principles gradually weakens any society.

Practice of the Law of Life

  • Every day it is useful to ask yourself:
  • What helped save your life today?
  • What new things have I learned?
  • What have I created?
  • Who did you help?
  • What from today will become part of the future?

These questions help you live consciously and see your place in the continuous development of life.

The main principle

Life does not exist for the sake of accumulation.

Life exists for the sake of continuing life.

The more a person contributes to the development of life around him, the more fully his own life unfolds.

Definition

The law of life is a universal principle according to which life strives to preserve itself, develop, cooperate, create and pass on its legacy to future generations.

In DREVO's New Psychology, the Law of Life is one of the fundamental principles of personal and social development. It demonstrates that the meaning of human life lies not only in personal well-being, but also in participating in the continuous process of development, creation, and transmission of life.

Like a tree that draws strength from its roots, grows toward the light, bears fruit, and gives seeds to new trees, a person becomes part of the Law of Life when he not only lives himself, but also helps life to continue around him.

Law of Integrity

The Law of Integrity in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, a person is viewed as a single living system.

  • Body.
  • Subconscious.
  • Psyche.
  • Consciousness.
  • Will.
  • Soul.
  • Spirit.
  • Values.
  • Social connections.
  • Interaction with nature.
  • All these levels do not exist separately from each other.
  • They interact constantly.
  • Changing one level inevitably affects the others.
  • This is why personal development must be holistic.

What is the Law of Integrity?

The law of integrity is a principle according to which a person develops as a single interconnected system, and the harmony of the personality is achieved not by the maximum development of one of its parts, but by the coordinated development of all levels.

Development without harmony creates internal imbalance.

The unity of man

  • The body influences the psyche.
  • The psyche influences thinking.
  • Consciousness influences decisions.
  • Will turns decisions into actions.
  • Actions form habits.
  • Habits change character.
  • Character determines one's life path.

It is impossible to change one part of a personality without affecting the others.

Imbalance

Sometimes a person develops only one side of his personality.

For example:

He has a lot of knowledge, but he doesn't know how to build relationships.

Or has a strong will, but has lost his sense of compassion.

Or he achieved material success, but lost the meaning of life.

Such achievements may be significant, but they do not create an inner sense of integrity.

Harmony requires the development of all the main aspects of personality.

Integrity and health

Mental health cannot be reduced to the absence of mental disorders.

It includes:

  • physical well-being;
  • emotional stability;→
  • clarity of thinking;
  • ability to make decisions;
  • moral maturity;
  • healthy relationships;
  • sense of meaning;
  • ability to create.

The more consistent these elements are, the more stable a person is.

Integrity and Society

  • This law applies not only to the individual.
  • Family.
  • Community.
  • Organization.
  • State.
  • Civilization.
  • Any system remains stable only when its parts cooperate with each other.

If one part develops at the expense of the destruction of others, the entire system gradually begins to suffer.

Practice of Integrity

It is helpful to ask yourself questions regularly.

Am I focusing too much on one area of ​​my life and neglecting others?

Am I developing simultaneously as an individual, as a family member, as a professional, and as a human being?

  • Do I devote enough time to my health?
  • Relationships?
  • Training?
  • Rest?
  • Creation?

Every month it is useful to evaluate all ten levels of personal development and seek balance between them.

Law of the Tree

  • A tree cannot develop only by roots.
  • Or just the trunk.
  • Or just leaves.
  • All parts must grow at the same time.
  • The roots feed the tree.
  • The trunk holds it.
  • The branches reach towards the light.
  • Leaves provide energy.
  • The fruits continue to live.
  • A person develops in the same way.

The main principle

It is impossible to become a harmonious personality by developing only individual abilities.

True development occurs when the body, psyche, consciousness, will, soul, spirit, relationships and lifestyle support each other.

Definition

The Law of Integrity is a fundamental principle of DREVO's New Psychology, which holds that a person is a unified system of interconnected levels, and sustainable development is only possible through their harmonious interaction.

An imbalance at one level eventually affects others. Conversely, strengthening one level can support the development of others if it aligns with overall values ​​and life direction.

Just as a tree lives through the unity of its roots, trunk, branches, leaves and fruits, a person becomes truly strong when all aspects of his personality develop as a whole.

The law of cause and effect

The Law of Cause and Effect in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, life is viewed as a continuous chain of causes and effects.

  • Nothing comes into being by itself.
  • Every thought has consequences.
  • Every decision affects the future.
  • Every action changes a person and the world around him.

Therefore, the quality of life gradually becomes a reflection of many daily choices.

The law of cause and effect is one of the fundamental laws of personal development.

What is the Law of Cause and Effect?

The law of cause and effect is the principle that every action, decision, or inaction produces consequences that affect the individual, their relationships, society, and the future.

This law operates regardless of whether a person notices it or not.

Causes create consequences

  • Thoughts become words.
  • Words become decisions.
  • Decisions become actions.
  • Actions become habits.
  • Habits shape character.
  • Character gradually determines the path of life.
  • That's why big changes rarely happen overnight.
  • They are the result of many small causes.

Responsibility for choice

The law of cause and effect makes a person free and at the same time responsible.

Freedom lies in the ability to choose.

Responsibility is the willingness to accept the consequences of your choices.

You can't always choose the consequences.

But you can choose the reasons from which they arise.
