The creation of a legacy is hindered by:

  • life only for one's own consumption;
  • indifference;
  • refusal to transfer knowledge;
  • destructive attitude towards people and nature;
  • the pursuit of short-term gain at any cost;
  • lack of long-term thinking.

A person can leave behind both a constructive and a destructive legacy.

Therefore, responsibility for the legacy begins today.

Practice of the Law of Inheritance

  • It is useful to regularly ask yourself questions:
  • What will remain after me?
  • What will be better because of my life?
  • What knowledge will I impart?
  • What kind of people will I help raise?
  • Which projects can survive without my participation?
  • What future am I creating with my actions today?
  • These questions help you look beyond your own life.

The main principle

The true greatness of a man is not determined by how much he has accumulated.

It is determined by how much life, goodness, knowledge, beauty and opportunity continues to exist because of it.

Definition

The Law of Heritage is a fundamental law of the New Psychology of DREVO, according to which the highest form of personal development is the creation of a tangible and intangible legacy that continues to benefit people, society, nature, and future generations.

Heritage becomes the natural outcome of a conscious, responsible, and creative life. It connects the past, present, and future, allowing people to become part of the continuous development of human civilization.

Like a tree that does not live for its own fruit, but leaves behind new seeds, shade and fertile soil, a person reaches the highest maturity when his life continues to be useful even after his departure.

Law of exchange

The Law of Exchange in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, life is viewed as a continuous process of exchange.

  • Nothing living exists entirely by itself.
  • The body exchanges substances and energy.
  • Plants exchange gases with the atmosphere.

People exchange knowledge, experience, emotions, labor, culture and care.

Development becomes possible through exchange.

When exchange ceases, life gradually fades away.

What is the Law of Exchange?

The Law of Exchange is a fundamental principle according to which sustainable development of individuals, society, and nature occurs through the mutual exchange of knowledge, energy, labor, resources, experience, care, and responsibility.

Exchange is the basis of cooperation and development.

Life exists through exchange

Every person constantly receives:

  • air;
  • water;
  • food;
  • knowledge;→
  • language;
  • culture;→
  • Love;→
  • help from other people.

At the same time, a person is able to give:

  • your work;
  • knowledge;→
  • time;
  • attention;
  • support;
  • creativity;
  • care;
  • experience.

Life becomes fulfilling when receiving and giving are in reasonable balance.

Types of exchange

Biological exchange

  • Breath.
  • Nutrition.
  • Metabolism.
  • The basis of physical life.

Emotional exchange

  • Support.
  • Love.
  • Trust.
  • Gratitude.

Emotions are transmitted from person to person and influence the psychological atmosphere.

Intellectual exchange

  • Training.
  • Teaching.
  • Research.
  • Collaborative search for knowledge.
  • The more people share knowledge, the faster society develops.

Social sharing

  • Joint work.
  • Mutual assistance.
  • Economy.
  • Voluntary cooperation.

Everyone makes their contribution and receives the results of joint activities.

Cultural exchange

  • Passing on traditions.
  • Arts.
  • Language.
  • Stories.

Culture develops through constant exchange between generations and peoples.

Spiritual and moral exchange

  • Good deeds.
  • Inspiration.
  • Wisdom.
  • Service.

These forms of exchange cannot be measured in material terms, but they are often the ones that change people's lives the most.

Violation of the Law of Exchange

The exchange is disrupted when a person:

only takes and gives nothing;

or constantly gives away, completely forgetting about his own health and recovery.

Both extremes destroy harmony.

Healthy exchange is based on reciprocity and respect.

The Law of Exchange and the Ten Levels of Man

  • Each level of personality participates in the exchange.
  • Body— metabolism and energy.
  • Subconscious- exchange of habits through imitation and learning.
  • Psyche— exchange of emotions and mood.
  • Consciousness— exchange of knowledge and ideas.
  • Will— exchange of trust through fulfilling promises.
  • Soul— exchange of love, beauty and kindness.
  • Spirit— exchange of meaning and inspiration.
  • Values— exchange of culture and moral principles.
  • Social connections— exchange of assistance, experience and cooperation.
  • Nature— continuous exchange between a person and the surrounding world.

Practice of the Law of Exchange

  • Every evening it is useful to ask yourself the following questions:
  • What useful things did I get today?
  • What useful thing have I given away today?
  • Is the world a little better because of my actions?
  • Have I lost the balance between caring for others and caring for myself?
  • What can I convey to people tomorrow?
  • Questions like these help maintain a healthy exchange with life.

The main principle

  • Life does not develop through accumulation.
  • It develops through movement.
  • That which circulates freely sustains life.
  • What is constantly kept only for oneself gradually loses value.

Definition

The Law of Exchange is a fundamental law of the New Psychology DREVO, according to which the development of man, society, and nature occurs through the continuous mutual exchange of resources, knowledge, experience, labor, emotions, culture, and care.

Exchange is seen as a natural mechanism of life, uniting people, strengthening cooperation, and creating conditions for shared development. Harmony is achieved when a person is able not only to receive but also to consciously give, maintaining a balance between personal well-being and contribution to the world around them.

Just as a tree receives water and sunlight and then gives off oxygen, shade, fruits and seeds, a person becomes a part of life when he learns to turn everything he receives into benefit for others.

The law of growth

The Law of Growth in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, growth is seen as a natural property of life.

  • All living things strive for development.
  • The seed becomes a tree.
  • The child becomes an adult.
  • Knowledge becomes wisdom.
  • Experience becomes maturity.
  • Life cannot remain unchanged for long.
  • It either develops.
  • Or it gradually loses its capabilities.
  • Therefore, growth is one of the fundamental laws of life.

What is the Law of Growth?

The law of growth is a fundamental principle according to which a person, like all living things, has a natural desire for development, and his personality is revealed through the continuous improvement of his abilities, character, relationships and life purpose.

Growth is a natural state of life.

Growth begins within

  • Real growth doesn't begin with external achievements.
  • It starts with internal changes.
  • Thinking is changing.
  • Habits change.
  • Values ​​are changing.
  • Actions change.
  • It is the inner growth that gradually changes the outer life.

Growth takes time

In nature, it is impossible to accelerate the growth of a tree tenfold without consequences.

  • A person develops in the same way.
  • Real growth requires:
  • time;
  • patience;
  • regular practice;
  • errors;
  • experience.
  • Trying to get quick results often leads to superficial development.

Human growth and ten levels

  • Every level of personality has the potential to grow.
  • Bodybecomes stronger through movement and care.
  • Subconsciousdevelops through new healthy habits.
  • Psychelearns resilience.
  • Consciousnessexpands knowledge and understanding of the world.
  • Will is strengthened through discipline.
  • Soulis revealed through love, creativity and compassion.
  • Spiritbecomes stronger through fidelity to purpose.
  • Valuesbecome deeper through life experience.
  • Social connectionsdevelop through trust and cooperation.
  • Interaction with naturehelps a person feel part of life.
  • Growth becomes harmonious only when the whole personality develops.

What hinders growth?

Growth is slowed down by:

  • fear of change;
  • striving for constant comfort;
  • the belief that “I already know everything”;
  • refusal to study;
  • life without goals;
  • lack of practice;
  • fear of mistakes.

The biggest obstacle is often not a lack of ability, but a cessation of development.

How does growth occur?

  • Growth goes through several stages.
  • Awareness.
  • The person understands the need for change.
  • ↓
  • Training.
  • Gains new knowledge.
  • ↓
  • Practice.
  • It's starting to work.
  • ↓
  • Repetition.
  • Forms habits.
  • ↓
  • Mastery.
  • New qualities become a natural part of the personality.
  • ↓
  • Transfer of experience.
  • A person begins to help others develop.

It is the transfer of knowledge that becomes the pinnacle of one’s own growth.

Growth and mistakes

  • Mistakes are part of development.
  • It is not the mistake that hinders growth.
  • Growth stops when a person stops drawing conclusions.
  • Every mistake becomes a new step if it turns into experience.

Practice of the Law of Growth

  • Every evening it is useful to ask yourself:
  • What new things did I learn today?
  • Which of my abilities has become stronger?
  • What have today's difficulties taught me?
  • What kind of person have I become today compared to yesterday?
  • What will be the next step in my development?
  • These questions help us see growth even in small changes.

The main principle

Growth never ends.

As long as a person is able to learn, love, create and share experiences, his development continues.

Definition

The Law of Growth is a fundamental law of the New Psychology DREVO, according to which life naturally strives for development, and a person reveals their potential through the continuous improvement of the body, psyche, consciousness, will, soul, spirit, relationships, and creative activity.

Growth is not a one-time event, but a way of life. It occurs gradually, through learning, practice, overcoming challenges, and accumulating experience, transforming a person's potential into maturity, wisdom, and legacy.

Like a tree that imperceptibly adds new rings each year, a person grows not through one great event, but through thousands of daily steps. True growth is impossible to see in a single day, but it is precisely this growth that, over time, changes a person's entire life.
