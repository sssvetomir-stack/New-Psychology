Biotechnology in New Psychology DREVO

The 21st century is becoming an era of rapid development of biotechnology.

  • Genetic engineering.
  • Cellular technologies.
  • Neurointerface.
  • Bioprosthetics.
  • Artificial organs.
  • Personalized medicine.

These advances are gradually changing not only medicine, but also the way people think about themselves.

Therefore, the New Psychology DREVO considers biotechnology as a new factor in the development of the individual, society and civilization.

What is biotechnology?

Biotechnology is a collection of scientific and engineering methods that use living cells, tissues, microorganisms, and biological processes to solve medical, agricultural, environmental, and industrial problems.

Modern biotechnology allows:

  • restore tissues;
  • create biomaterials;
  • treat some hereditary diseases;
  • grow cells and organelles;
  • develop personalized treatment methods;
  • create biosensors and neural interfaces.

Every new technology opens up new possibilities, but at the same time requires responsible use.

Biotechnology and the psyche

The psyche is closely connected with the functioning of the brain, nervous system, hormonal regulation and the entire body.

Therefore, changes in biological systems can affect a person’s psychological state.

For example:

  • Restoring hearing or vision can change your perception of the world;
  • Treating chronic pain can improve emotional well-being;
  • Some medications may affect mood, attention, or memory;
  • Neural interfaces can change the way humans interact with technology.

The impact of each technology depends on its purpose, conditions of use and individual characteristics of the person.

Biotechnology and personality

Technology has the potential to change how the body functions.

However, changing biology does not automatically mean changing personality.

Personality is formed under the influence of many factors:

  • life experience;
  • education;
  • culture;→
  • relationships;
  • conscious choice;
  • values.→

Biological changes may create new opportunities or new limitations, but they do not completely determine who a person becomes.

New psychological questions

  • The development of biotechnology poses new questions for humanity.
  • How will a person's sense of self change after using bionic implants?
  • How will you perceive your own body after growing artificial organs?

How will relationships between people change with the widespread use of genetic technologies?

How can we preserve human dignity in the face of increasing interference in biological processes?

These questions require the collaboration of psychologists, doctors, biologists, engineers, philosophers and lawyers.

Ethics of biotechnology

Any technology should be assessed not only by its effectiveness.

No less important are the questions:

  • security;
  • voluntary consent;
  • fair access;
  • protection of personal information;
  • respect for human dignity;
  • long-term consequences for society.

The psychology of the future should not be limited to studying the impact of technology on humans.

It should be involved in shaping the principles for their responsible use.

Biotechnology and human development

In the DREVO philosophy, biotechnology is seen as a tool.

They are capable of:

  • reduce suffering;
  • restore health;
  • expand the body's capabilities;
  • improve the quality of life.

However, technology itself does not make people wiser, more responsible or more moral.

Personal development still requires upbringing, education, culture, awareness and cooperation.

Future

In the future, the boundary between biology and technology will become increasingly less noticeable.

Therefore, a person will need a new psychological maturity.

He must learn to use the achievements of science without losing respect for life, freedom of choice and human dignity.

Real progress is not determined by the number of new technologies.

It is determined by whether they help a person live a healthier, freer, more responsible and creative life.

The main principle

Biotechnology has the potential to change the capabilities of the human body.

But the direction of their application is determined by the person himself.

  • Technology expands capabilities.
  • Values ​​determine goals.
  • Consciousness makes a choice.
  • Responsibility determines consequences.

Definition

Biotechnology and the Psyche is a section of DREVO's New Psychology that studies the impact of modern biological and medical technologies on psychological well-being, self-perception, behavior, and personal development.

DREVO's new psychology views biotechnology as a powerful tool for the development of civilization, which should be used to improve human health and quality of life while preserving human dignity, freedom of choice, and responsibility.

The future of man depends not only on what technologies he creates, but also on what kind of person he becomes using these technologies.

Digital ecology

Digital Ecology in New Psychology DREVO

  • In the 21st century, people live in two worlds simultaneously.
  • The first is physical.
  • The second is digital.

Both worlds influence health, thinking, relationships, education, culture and personal development.

Therefore, DREVO's New Psychology views digital ecology as a system of principles that help people interact harmoniously with digital technologies while maintaining freedom, psychological health, and quality of life.

What is digital ecology?

Digital ecology is the conscious organization of a person's digital environment, in which technologies contribute to the development of the individual rather than control his or her behavior.

Just as a healthy natural environment supports a healthy body, a healthy digital environment supports a healthy mind, psyche, and social relationships.

The digital environment as a part of life

  • Today, digital technologies accompany people almost constantly.
  • Work.
  • Education.
  • Communication.
  • Banking services.
  • Medicine.
  • Creativity.
  • Artificial intelligence.
  • The digital environment has ceased to be a separate tool.
  • It has become part of the human environment.

Therefore, the quality of the digital environment directly affects the quality of life.

Digital pollution

Just as nature can become polluted by waste, the digital environment can become polluted by information overload and destructive forms of interaction.

Digital pollution includes:

  • information noise;
  • constant notifications;
  • unverified information;
  • manipulation;
  • aggressive communication;
  • digital addiction;
  • excessive screen time;
  • loss of ability to concentrate for long periods of time;
  • uncontrolled use of artificial intelligence.

The main problem is not the quantity of technologies, but the lack of conscious management of them.

Digital health

A healthy digital environment helps a person:

  • study;
  • work;
  • create;→
  • cooperate;
  • develop thinking;
  • maintain relationships;
  • gain access to knowledge.

Technology should free people to be creative, social and develop, rather than absorbing all their attention.

Digital freedom

Freedom in the digital world means the ability to choose how to use technology.

A person remains the master of his decisions.

It defines:

  • how much time to spend online;
  • what sources of information to use;
  • what technologies help development;
  • when you need to disconnect and return to real communication.

True freedom is impossible without self-control.

Digital security

The development of technology requires concern not only about physical security.

No less important are:

  • protection of personal data;
  • cybersecurity;
  • protection of intellectual property;
  • responsible use of artificial intelligence;
  • verification of the accuracy of information.

Digital literacy is becoming a new form of culture.

Man and technology

In DREVO's philosophy, technology is seen as an extension of human capabilities.

But no technology should replace:

  • independent thinking;
  • responsibility;→
  • live communication;
  • Love;→
  • conscience;
  • creativity;
  • freedom of choice.

Technology is a tool.

Man remains their creator and responsible user.

Principles of digital ecology

  • A healthy digital life is built on several principles.
  • Awareness.
  • Use technology for a purpose, not out of habit.
  • Quality is more important than quantity.
  • It is better to have less information, but more reliable and useful.
  • Balance.
  • Digital life should complement real life, not replace it.
  • Attention protection.
  • Attention is one of the most valuable human resources.
  • Technologies for creation.

Use digital tools primarily for learning, creativity, collaboration, and development.

The main principle

The digital environment should serve people.

A person should not become its appendage.

The faster technology develops, the more important it is to maintain the ability to think, feel, choose, and build genuine human relationships independently.

Definition

Digital ecology is a system of principles and practices aimed at harmonious human interaction with the digital environment, ensuring the preservation of psychological health, freedom of choice, critical thinking, human relationships, and the creative use of technology.

In DREVO's New Psychology, digital ecology is viewed as a necessary condition for personal development in the era of artificial intelligence and global communications.

The goal of digital ecology is not to abandon technology, but to learn to use it in a way that strengthens people rather than weakens their attention, freedom, and creative capacity.
