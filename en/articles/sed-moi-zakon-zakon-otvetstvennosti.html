Freedom is impossible without responsibility.

Every action has consequences.

A mature person accepts responsibility not only for his own life, but also for the impact of his actions on other people and the world around him.

The Eighth Law is the Law of Cooperation

A person develops through other people.

Personality is revealed in family, community, culture and society.

Knowledge sharing, collaboration, and mutual support accelerate the development of each participant.

True development is impossible without respect for the dignity of another person.

The Ninth Law is the Law of Creation

The highest form of development is the ability to create.

Creation manifests itself in different forms:

  • raising children;
  • scientific discoveries;
  • art;
  • technologies;→
  • entrepreneurship;
  • education;
  • helping people;
  • restoration of nature.

The more a person creates value for others, the more fully his potential is revealed.

The Tenth Law is the Law of Heritage

  • Real development continues after man.
  • Every person leaves a trace behind.
  • It can manifest itself in:
  • knowledge;→
  • well-mannered people;
  • family;→
  • created organizations;
  • works of culture;
  • scientific discoveries;
  • restored nature;
  • social changes.

Heritage becomes a continuation of the individual over time.

The main law of DREVO

All previous laws are united by one principle.

Personal development is harmonious when a person consciously reveals their abilities, strengthens their body, develops their psyche and consciousness, cultivates their will, remains true to their values, collaborates with other people, and directs the results of their labor toward the development of life.

It is this path that transforms a person from a participant in the struggle for survival into a creator of the future.

DREVO's development formula

Awareness → Choice → Will → Action → Habit → Character → Creation → Heritage.

This sequence shows that personal development begins with an internal understanding of oneself, progresses through daily actions, and culminates in a person's contribution to the lives of others and future generations.

Part II. Human Architecture

Man as a living multi-level system

In the New Psychology of DREVO, a person is viewed as a holistic living system, in which each level performs its own function and simultaneously interacts with all the others.

It is impossible to fully understand a person by looking only at the body, only at the brain, or only at behavior.

Its development occurs due to the interaction of biological, psychological, social and ideological processes.

Human architecture describes the internal structure of this system.

It shows how thoughts are born, decisions are made, values ​​are formed, personality is revealed and human life is created.

First level - Body

  • The main question:
  • What allows me to live?
  • The body is the material basis of a person.

It ensures life, movement, metabolism, perception of the surrounding world and interaction with the external environment.

Through the body a person receives energy, sensations and experience.

Without a healthy body, the full development of other levels of personality is impossible.

The second level is the Subconscious

  • The main question:
  • What experience already guides me?
  • The subconscious is a deep system of automatic processes of the body.
  • It includes:
  • innate programs;
  • instincts;
  • automatic skills;
  • physical memory;
  • emotional reactions;
  • hereditary characteristics;
  • formed habits.

The subconscious helps a person react quickly without overloading the consciousness with constant analysis.

The third level is the Psyche

  • The main question:
  • What's going on around me?
  • The psyche perceives the surrounding world.
  • She feels.
  • Analyzes.
  • Remembers.
  • Compares.
  • Imagines.
  • Studying.

It is the psyche that transforms the flow of information into personal experience.

The fourth level is Consciousness

  • The main question:
  • What do I choose?
  • Consciousness observes what is happening.
  • It is capable of analyzing thoughts, feelings and actions.

Consciousness makes free choice possible and allows a person to go beyond automatic reactions.

Fifth level - Will

  • The main question:
  • Am I able to implement my choice?
  • Will is a person's ability to control himself.
  • It connects decision with action.

Thanks to will, a person is able to follow his values ​​even when it requires effort.

The sixth level is the Superconscious.

The main question:

What new opportunities are opening up for me?

The superconscious is a space of creativity, inspiration, intuitive insights and the search for new solutions.

In DREVO's New Psychology, the nature of the superconscious is viewed as an open area of ​​research, encompassing creative experience, subjective experiences, and questions that have not yet received a definitive scientific explanation.

Seventh level - Soul

  • The main question:
  • Who am I?
  • The soul is the inner core of the personality.

It combines a sense of authenticity, love, conscience, the ability to empathize, the experience of beauty and deep values.

Within the framework of DREVO, this is a philosophical concept that describes a person’s inner experience.

Eighth level - Spirit

  • The main question:
  • What do I live for?
  • The spirit determines the direction of personality development.

It is associated with the will to create, the pursuit of ideals, the ability to remain true to one's beliefs and act for long-term goals.

Level Nine – Values ​​and Meaning

The main question:

Why this particular path?

Values ​​help us distinguish between good and evil, important and unimportant.

Meaning unites all levels of personality into a single direction of life.

It answers the question of why a person uses his abilities.

Level Ten - Social Connections

  • The main question:
  • Who am I developing with?
  • A person reveals himself through relationships.
  • Family.
  • Genus.
  • Community.
  • Society.
  • Culture.
  • Cooperation.

Social connections create a space for mutual learning, support and joint development.

Level Eleven - Interaction with Nature and the Environment

The main question:

What world do I live in and what world do I create?

Man is a part of nature and at the same time creates an artificial environment.

Nature, cities, culture and technology constantly influence his health, thinking and lifestyle.

In turn, a person changes the world around him with his decisions.

The twelfth level is the human biofield.

The main question:

How does the body interact with the environment at the physiological level?

The human body creates bioelectrical and biomagnetic activity associated with the functioning of the heart, brain, nervous system and other organs.

In New Psychology DREVO, the biofield is considered a physiological manifestation of the organism's vital activity and a promising direction for further research.

Unified system

  • Each level has its own purpose.
  • The body creates the basis of life.
  • The subconscious stores experience.
  • The psyche perceives the world.
  • Consciousness makes a choice.
  • Will turns choice into action.
  • The superconscious opens up new possibilities.
  • The soul helps to maintain authenticity.
  • The spirit sets the direction of development.
  • Values ​​define meaning.
  • Social connections allow a person to open up among people.
  • The environment creates living conditions.

The biofield reflects the physiological activity of the organism and its interaction with the outside world.

All levels form a single system.

A change in one level gradually affects all the others.

The main principle

  • Human architecture is like a tree.
  • The body and the subconscious are the roots.
  • Psyche, consciousness and will are the trunk.
  • Superconsciousness, soul and spirit - the crown.
  • Values ​​are the direction of growth.
  • Social connections form a forest.
  • Nature and the environment - soil, water, air and light.

It is precisely through the coordinated work of all levels that a person becomes a holistic, free and creative individual.

Body

The body is the material basis of a person and the first level of conscious life.

It is not a passive shell. The body is a complex, self-regulating living system that continuously perceives the surrounding world, processes information, and maintains life.

In the new psychology, the body is seen as the bearer of its own biological wisdom.

It has:

  • ability to self-heal;
  • innate development programs;
  • adaptation mechanisms;
  • memory of past experiences;
  • a system of internal communication between organs, the nervous, immune and endocrine systems.

The body constantly collects information from both the external and internal environment.

It perceives:

  • temperature;
  • pressure;
  • chemical composition of blood;
  • hormone levels;
  • position in space;
  • condition of muscles and joints;
  • pain;
  • famine;
  • thirst;
  • fatigue;
  • safety or danger.

Most of these processes occur without the participation of consciousness.

Therefore, the body can be considered as an independent system of perception and regulation.

It is the body that creates the basis for the work of the subconscious mind.

When the body is depleted, overloaded, or chronically inflamed, it impacts emotions, thinking, behavior, and decision-making.

Taking care of your body is not only about physical health, but also the foundation of psychological well-being.

In the new psychology, the body is seen not as a “machine,” but as a living partner of consciousness.

Through breathing, movement, nutrition, sleep, touch and interaction with the environment, a person maintains a connection with his own nature.

The main task of the body is to preserve life, maintain internal balance and create conditions for personal development.

The body is the foundation of a person. Without a strong foundation, it is impossible to fully realize the potential of the subconscious, conscious, superconscious, soul, and spirit.
