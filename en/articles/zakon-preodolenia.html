The Law of Overcoming in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, development is impossible without overcoming.

  • All living things face obstacles.
  • The seed overcomes the thickness of the earth.
  • The tree can withstand the wind.
  • The river goes around the stones.

A person develops by overcoming his own limitations and life’s difficulties.

  • Difficulties in themselves do not make a person stronger.
  • What makes him stronger is how he responds to them.
  • It is overcoming that transforms experience into maturity.

What is the Law of Overcoming?

The Law of Overcoming is a fundamental principle according to which personal development occurs through consciously overcoming internal and external obstacles, leading to increased strength, wisdom, freedom and maturity.

An obstacle is not only a test.

This is an opportunity for development.

Why are difficulties necessary?

If a person never encounters resistance, many of his abilities remain untapped.

It is difficulties that help develop:

  • will;→
  • patience;
  • responsibility;→
  • courage;
  • creativity;
  • ability to cooperate.

Overcoming becomes a natural part of development.

What needs to be overcome?

  • First of all, a person overcomes himself.
  • Your fears.
  • Laziness.
  • Uncertainty.
  • Destructive habits.
  • Impulsive reactions.
  • Limiting beliefs.

Often the main obstacle is not the outside world, but internal limitations.

Overcoming and the Ten Levels of Man

  • Each level of personality has its own challenges.
  • Body- fatigue, illness, the need to take care of health.
  • Subconscious- changing bad habits.
  • Psyche- managing fear, anxiety, anger and despair.

Consciousness- overcoming misconceptions, stereotypes and self-deception.

  • Will- choosing the right one, not the easy one.
  • Soul- maintaining love and humanity even after pain and disappointment.
  • Spirit- fidelity to one's path in times of uncertainty.

Values- the ability to not change one's principles for the sake of immediate gain.

Social connections- the ability to forgive, cooperate and resolve conflicts.

Interaction with nature- respect for the laws of life and the ability to live in harmony with the surrounding world.

What prevents you from overcoming?

Development is slowed down by:

  • fear of failure;
  • striving for constant comfort;
  • the habit of blaming circumstances;
  • expecting problems to go away on their own;
  • refusal to learn from mistakes.

Every avoidance of difficulty often makes future difficulties greater.

How to overcome difficulties correctly?

In New Psychology DREVO recommends:

  • accept the existence of the problem;
  • keep calm;
  • break a large task into small steps;
  • seek knowledge and help;
  • don't stop after the first failures;
  • perceive mistakes as a source of experience;
  • remember your values ​​and purpose.

Overcoming is not a fight at any cost.

This is a conscious movement forward.

Overcoming and happiness

Happiness does not come after all difficulties disappear.

It appears when a person feels that he is able to go through them with dignity.

Inner strength does not come from the absence of challenges.

It is born from the experience of overcoming them.

Practice of the Law of Overcoming

  • Every evening it is useful to ask yourself the following questions:
  • What was the hardest thing today?
  • What have I overcome?
  • What did this trial teach me?
  • Which of my inner strengths has become a little stronger?
  • What's the next step I'll take tomorrow?
  • These questions help turn difficulties into a source of development.

The main principle

  • Obstacles do not always stand in a person's way.
  • Very often they are the way.
  • Every challenge overcome makes a person stronger, wiser and freer.

Definition

The Law of Overcoming is a fundamental law of the New Psychology DREVO, according to which personal growth occurs through consciously overcoming internal and external difficulties, revealing a person's potential and strengthening their maturity.

Overcoming is seen not as a struggle against life, but as a natural process of development. Every challenge becomes an opportunity to strengthen the body, psyche, will, spirit, and the ability to create goodness even in difficult circumstances.

Just as a tree grows stronger through resistance to the wind, a person grows stronger through the difficulties he goes through with dignity, awareness and fidelity to his values.

Law of Cooperation

The Law of Cooperation in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, cooperation is considered one of the fundamental laws of life.

  • Life develops not only thanks to competition.
  • It develops primarily through interaction.
  • Cells form an organism.
  • The organs work together.
  • Trees unite into a forest.
  • Bees create a hive.
  • People build families, communities and civilizations.

The higher the level of development of life, the more complex and deeper cooperation becomes.

Therefore, the ability to cooperate is one of the main signs of a mature personality.

What is the Law of Cooperation?

The law of cooperation is a fundamental principle according to which the sustainable development of the individual, society, and civilization is achieved through voluntary interaction based on trust, mutual respect, knowledge sharing, and joint creation.

Collaboration does not cancel out individuality.

It helps her to open up.

Why is cooperation stronger than loneliness?

One person can do a lot.

But the capabilities of any person are limited.

When different knowledge, talents and experiences come together, something emerges that cannot be created alone.

  • This phenomenon can be called collective creation.
  • Each person retains his own uniqueness.
  • But together people are capable of creating new qualities of the system.
