A strong community is created not by rules, but by culture.

This culture is manifested in:

  • respect;→
  • open dialogue;
  • honesty;
  • mutual assistance;
  • conflict resolution skills;
  • gratitude;→
  • willingness to learn from each other.

Culture defines the atmosphere in which each participant develops.

Community and Individuality

  • A good community does not suppress individuality.
  • It helps a person to reveal his abilities.
  • Each participant has unique knowledge, experience and talents.
  • Diversity becomes a source of strength rather than a cause of division.

The richer the differences, the higher the potential for joint development.

Community and collective intelligence

In the New Psychology of DREVO, the community is the environment for the formation of collective intelligence.

People unite:

  • knowledge;→
  • experience;
  • ideas;
  • skills;
  • resources.

Collaborative thinking allows us to find solutions that rarely arise in isolation.

Collective intelligence becomes a natural extension of a collaborative culture.

Community and Leadership

  • A true leader does not control people for his own power.
  • He helps people to discover their abilities.
  • Its task is to unite.
  • Support.
  • Create conditions for development.
  • Transfer responsibility.
  • A strong leader makes the entire community stronger.

Community and Society

From small communities, a society gradually forms.

When communities are built on trust, cooperation and responsibility, society becomes more resilient.

When communities collapse, social institutions weaken.

Therefore, the development of civilization begins with the development of human communities.

Community Development Practice

Every community is strengthened when its members:

  • meet regularly;
  • make decisions together;
  • help new members;
  • share knowledge;
  • implement common projects;
  • support a culture of respect;
  • openly discuss emerging difficulties;
  • celebrate shared achievements.

Community grows through shared experiences.

The main principle

  • Community is a place where a person stops asking:
  • "What can I get?"
  • And he begins to ask:
  • "What can we create together?"
  • It is at this moment that cooperation becomes a source of development.

Definition

Community development is the process of strengthening a culture of trust, cooperation, collective responsibility, and co-creation, enabling people to pool their abilities for the common good.

In DREVO's New Psychology, community is seen as a natural environment for personal development, the formation of collective intelligence, and the creation of a sustainable society.

Just as a forest is stronger than any single tree because of the relationships between them, a human community becomes stronger when each person reveals their talents and voluntarily combines them with the talents of others for the sake of a common future.

Diary of desires

Self-knowledge practice in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, desires are seen as one of the ways in which a person gets to know himself.

  • Every desire communicates some need.
  • Sometimes - the needs of the body.
  • Sometimes - psyche.
  • Sometimes - the desire of the soul.
  • Sometimes - the direction of the spirit.
  • However, not every wish should be fulfilled immediately.
  • First, it needs to be understood.
  • This is exactly what the Wish Diary is for.

What is a Wish Diary?

A desire journal is a practice of regularly recording and analyzing your desires, which helps a person better understand their needs, values, motives, and life direction.

His goal is not to compile a dream list.

Its goal is to learn to distinguish random desires from those that truly help personal development.

Why keep a Wish Diary?

Regular entries help you see:

  • what is really important to me;
  • what desires are repeated;
  • what desires are imposed by others;
  • what desires are born from fear;
  • some - out of love and a desire for development.

Gradually, a person begins to understand himself better.

How to keep a Wish Diary?

Every day or several times a week, write down the desires that arise during the day.

  • Don't judge them right away.
  • Don't judge yourself.
  • Just write it down.

Even the most unusual desires can tell a lot about a person’s inner world.

Questions for analysis

  • After some time, it is useful to ask yourself questions.
  • Why did this particular desire arise?
  • What need does it express?
  • Is this a desire of the body?
  • Psychics?
  • Consciousness?
  • Souls?
  • Spirit?
  • Will its implementation help my development?
  • Will I be happy with this in a year?
  • In ten years?

If a desire is only for short-term pleasure, it's worth considering whether it satisfies deeper needs.

Desire and values

Not every desire corresponds to a person’s values.

Sometimes a desire brings short-term pleasure but conflicts with its long-term goals.

  • Therefore, it is important to ask yourself:
  • Does this align with my values?
  • Does this bring me closer to the person I want to become?
  • Does this benefit not only me, but also those around me?
  • Desires become creative when they are combined with values.

Desire and goal

  • Desire answers the question:
  • "What do I want?"
  • The target responds:
  • "What am I willing to do to achieve this?"
  • Will transforms desire into action.
  • Therefore, a diary helps to gradually turn desires into real plans.

Desire and Purpose

If a person notices over many months or years that certain desires constantly return and are associated with development, creativity or helping people, this may indicate the direction of his destiny.

Purpose is revealed not through random desires, but through stable aspirations, tested by time and actions.

Practice of the month

Reread your notes once a month.

Please note:

  • what wishes have already come true;
  • which have ceased to be important;
  • which ones are repeated most often;
  • what desires became goals;
  • which brought you closer to development.

This analysis allows us to see how the personality changes.

The main principle

  • Desires do not make a person weak.
  • They help to understand where his internal energy is directed.
  • Wisdom does not consist in giving up all desires.

Wisdom lies in choosing those desires that make life deeper, freer and more creative.

Definition

A desire journal is a practice of self-observation and self-analysis that allows a person to consciously explore their desires, understand the needs behind them, and transform true aspirations into life goals and creative actions.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, the Desire Diary is viewed as a tool for developing consciousness, willpower, and self-knowledge. It helps one distinguish random impulses from the true aspirations of the soul and gradually find one's direction in life.

Desires are like seeds. Not every seed is destined to sprout. But it's precisely by paying close attention to them that a person can cultivate the tree of their life into the one they truly desire.

Daily newspaper

Life Management Practice in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, the diary is seen as a tool for consciously managing one's life.

  • Most people only plan things to do.
  • But a mature person plans not only tasks.
  • She plans her development.
  • Every day becomes a step towards the person a person strives to become.

A diary helps connect thoughts with actions, goals with habits, and purpose with everyday life.

What is a diary?

A diary is a planning and self-organization tool that allows a person to consciously manage their time, resources, development, and life direction.

The main goal of a diary is not to fill the day with things to do.

The main task is to live the day meaningfully.

Morning planning

  • It is helpful to answer a few questions every morning.
  • What is the main goal of today?
  • What three things are really important?
  • What will help me become better today?
  • Who can I help today?
  • What am I grateful for?
  • This is how a conscious day begins.

Balance of the day

In the New Psychology of DREVO, it is advisable to devote attention to the main areas of life every day.

Body

  • Movement.
  • Nutrition.
  • Dream.
  • Rest.

Intelligence

  • Training.
  • Reading.
  • Reflection.
  • Learning new things.

Creation

  • Work.
  • Creativity.
  • Projects.
  • Practice.

Relationship

  • Family.
  • Friends.
  • Community.
  • Support from other people.

Soul and spirit

  • Silence.
  • Reflection.
  • Nature.
  • Gratitude.
  • Loyalty to your values.

The main rule

Don't overload your day.

It's better to do three really important things than to write down twenty and not complete any of them.

A completed task strengthens the will.

Unfinished business gradually drains attention.

Evening analysis

  • Before going to bed, it is helpful to answer a few questions.
  • What happened today?
  • What was the most valuable?
  • What have I learned?
  • What good deed have I done?
  • What can be done better tomorrow?
  • Thus the day turns into experience, and experience into wisdom.

Development diary

In addition to the to-do list, it is useful to note:

  • mood;
  • energy level;
  • physical activity;
  • new knowledge;
  • good deeds;
  • ideas;
  • gratitude;→
  • steps towards long-term goals.

After a few months, such a diary allows you to see real changes in your personality.

DREVO's One-Day Formula

It is advisable to take at least one step every day:

  • for health;
  • for knowledge;
  • for family;
  • for society;
  • for nature;
  • for its intended purpose.

Even a small step is better than no action at all.

The main principle

  • Man does not control time.
  • He controls his attention, his decisions and his actions.
  • It is they who gradually create his life.
  • Every day lived becomes part of one's character.
  • Every choice made becomes part of destiny.

Definition

A diary is a tool for conscious life management, allowing you to plan not only your daily activities, but also your personal development, health, relationships, knowledge, willpower, and life purpose.

In DREVO's New Psychology, a daily planner is seen as a practical bridge between values ​​and actions. It helps people transform their daily intentions into real actions and gradually build the life they desire.

Life doesn't change on the day of a great decision. It changes every ordinary day when a person consciously chooses the next right step.
