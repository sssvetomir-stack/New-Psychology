The psychology of the future is a science not only about the psyche but also about the development of human potential. Its goal is to help people live consciously, maintain inner balance, build healthy relationships, develop their abilities, and interact with the world around them.

It can be built on the following principles.

1. Integrity

A person is a single system in which the body, psyche, consciousness, values, social connections and the environment constantly influence each other.

2. Development instead of struggle

The main goal is not only to reduce suffering, but also to develop personal strengths: curiosity, resilience, creativity, responsibility and the ability to collaborate.

3. Understanding instead of condemnation

All behavior has a cause. Instead of making hasty judgments, psychology seeks to understand the biological, psychological, and social factors that led a person to their choices.

4. Individual approach

Each person has a unique combination of abilities, life experiences, temperament, and values. There are no universal solutions.

5. Mindfulness

Development begins with the ability to notice your thoughts, emotions, habits and reactions, and then consciously choose a course of action.

6. Scientificity

New ideas must be tested by research. Future psychology is open to new hypotheses, but does not accept them as fact without sufficient evidence.

7. Interdisciplinarity

It combines knowledge from psychology, neuroscience, medicine, pedagogy, sociology, ecology, computer science, and artificial intelligence to better understand people.

8. The relationship between man and society

Personality develops within family, culture, and society. Caring for psychological well-being includes creating healthy communities, education, and conditions for collaboration.

9. Ethics

Technology, artificial intelligence and psychological methods must be used with respect for human freedom, dignity and the right to make independent decisions.

10. Continuous development

Psychological development doesn't end at a certain age. People are capable of learning, rethinking experiences, and changing their lives throughout their lives.

Vision

Future psychology is an interdisciplinary science that helps us understand ourselves, develop our abilities, enhance our well-being, and build harmonious relationships with others and the world around us.

The Soul from the Point of View of New Psychology

New psychology can consider the soul not as an object of faith or only a religious concept, but asworking model of the inner core of personality- that which gives a person a sense of his own uniqueness, integrity and direction in life.

In this concept, the soul is not an organ or a region of the brain. It is a concept that describes the deepest level of human experience.

The soul manifests itself through:

  • conscience;
  • love and compassion;
  • striving for truth;
  • sense of beauty;
  • creativity;
  • inner meaning of life;
  • ability to make free choices;
  • a sense of authentic self.

The psyche answers the question"How does a person think, feel and act?"

The soul answers the question"What does a person live for and what does he strive to become?"

If the psyche can adapt to the environment, then the soul helps a person not to lose himself even under strong external pressure.

Health of the soul

The state of the soul can be understood not mystically, but through observable manifestations:

  • internal integrity;
  • consistency between beliefs and actions;
  • the ability to love and trust;
  • presence of meaning;
  • a sense of connection with other people and the world;
  • the ability to create something new.

Diseases of the soul

In a philosophical sense, one can speak not so much of “diseases of the soul” as of its alienation:

  • loss of meaning;→
  • internal schism;
  • living contrary to one's own values;
  • chronic feeling of emptiness;
  • loss of the ability to empathize and experience joy.

It is important to distinguish such philosophical descriptions from clinical diagnoses, which belong to the field of psychiatry and psychology.

The main task of the new psychology

  • If traditional psychology often answers the question:
  • "How to reduce suffering?"
  • then the psychology of the future can add a second question:

"How can we help a person discover their inner core and live their life in harmony with it?"

Possible definition

The soul is a philosophical concept that denotes the deepest core of personality, which encompasses values, the pursuit of meaning, the capacity for love, creativity, and free choice. The new psychology can explore how this inner core manifests itself in human behavior, relationships, and well-being, using both humanistic approaches and empirical methods where applicable.

The Spirit from the Point of View of New Psychology

Spirit isinner guiding force of personality, thanks to which a person is able to transcend their instincts, habits, and circumstances. It is the spirit that allows one to choose not what is easiest, but what one considers right or meaningful.

If you use this model:

  • Body— answers the question:"What can I do?"
  • Psyche— he answers:"What do I feel and how do I perceive the world?"
  • Soul— he answers:"Who am I? What is truly valuable to me?"
  • Spirit— he answers:"Where am I going? What am I willing to act for?"

Manifestations of the Spirit

The spirit manifests itself in the ability to:

  • maintain dignity in difficult circumstances;
  • be true to your principles;
  • overcome fear;
  • take responsibility;
  • to sacrifice personal gain for the sake of others or for the sake of an ideal;
  • continue on after defeats.

In this model, spirit is not emotion. A person can experience fear, fatigue, or doubt, but still act in accordance with their beliefs.

Strong spirit

A person with a strong spirit is characterized by:

  • internal stability;
  • discipline;→
  • responsibility;→
  • courage;
  • keeping one's word;
  • the ability to inspire others.

Weakening of the spirit

We can talk about a state when a person:

  • loses meaning and direction;
  • stops believing in the possibility of change;
  • renounces his values ​​under pressure of circumstances;
  • avoids responsibility.

It is important to distinguish such philosophical descriptions from mental disorders: they are not medical diagnoses.

The interaction of body, mind, soul and spirit

These four levels can be thought of as interconnected elements:

  • Bodygives the opportunity to act.
  • Psycheperceives, feels and analyzes.
  • Souldefines deep values ​​and a sense of authenticity.
  • Spiritdirects a person towards the chosen goal and helps to remain faithful to it.

Psyche

Psyche— is the system through which a person perceives the world, processes information, experiences emotions, remembers experiences, makes decisions, and adapts to the environment.

Figuratively speaking:

  • Body— action tool.
  • Psyche— perception and control system.
  • Soul— the inner core of personality, values ​​and meaning (within the framework of your philosophical model).
  • Spirit- the power of direction, will and following the chosen path.

Basic functions of the psyche

The psyche is responsible for:

  • perception;
  • attention;
  • memory;
  • thinking;
  • imagination;
  • emotions;→
  • speech;
  • training;
  • decision making;
  • adaptation to changing conditions.

It is the psyche that allows a person to learn from experience and change their behavior.

The main task of the psyche

Its main function isensure survival and effective interaction with the environmentTherefore, the psyche constantly evaluates:

  • safe or dangerous;
  • useful or harmful;
  • familiar or unknown;
  • possible or impossible.

Many automatic reactions—anxiety, alertness, habits—can be seen as adaptive mechanisms.

Healthy psyche

Signs of psychological well-being may include:

  • flexibility of thinking;
  • emotional stability;→
  • ability to learn;
  • adequate perception of reality;
  • self-regulation;
  • ability to build relationships.

Interaction

Within your model:

  • Bodyprovides energy and physical capabilities.
  • Psycheanalyzes the situation and suggests options for action.
  • Soulhelps to determine what corresponds to internal values.
  • Spirithelps to implement the chosen direction, even when it is difficult.

Consciousness

Consciousness— is a person’s ability to be aware of themselves, the surrounding world, and their own internal processes, as well as to direct attention and make choices.

If the psyche continuously processes information, then consciousness allows us to see this work and, to a certain extent, influence it.

In this model

  • Body— it works.
  • Psyche— perceives, feels, remembers, analyzes.
  • Consciousness- observes, realizes, chooses.
  • Soul— sets deep values ​​and a sense of authenticity.
  • Spirit- gives direction, meaning and determination to follow the chosen path.

Basic functions of consciousness

Consciousness allows a person to:

  • to be aware of one's existence;
  • notice your thoughts and emotions;
  • manage attention;
  • make informed choices;
  • predict the consequences of actions;
  • reflect on yourself and the world;
  • learn from your own experience.

Levels of consciousness

As a philosophical model, several levels can be distinguished:

  1. Consciousness of perception- "I see and hear."
  2. Self-awareness— “I understand that this is happening to me.”
  3. Reflexive consciousness- “I can observe my thoughts and feelings.”
  4. Value consciousness— “I realize what I consider right and important.”
  5. Creative consciousness- "I am able to create new ideas and transform the world."

Development of consciousness

The development of consciousness is not just the accumulation of knowledge, but the growth of ability:

  • notice automatic reactions;
  • separate facts from interpretations;
  • maintain clarity of thinking in difficult situations;
  • make decisions in line with your values;
  • understand other people.

Possible definition

Consciousness is a person's ability to be aware of themselves and the world, to direct their attention, to analyze their own mental processes, and to make informed decisions. It serves as the link between perception, values, and actions.
