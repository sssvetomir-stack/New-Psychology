This cooperation is built on five principles.

Trust

Without trust, long-term interaction is impossible.

It is created by honesty, consistency and keeping promises.

Respect

Every person has dignity, knowledge and experience.

Respect allows you to see the value of another person even when your views differ.

Voluntariness

True cooperation cannot be achieved through coercion.

It is born from free choice.

Common goal

People are able to unite when they understand why they are acting together.

A common goal unites different abilities into a single direction.

Mutual benefit

  • Healthy collaboration creates value for all participants.
  • It is not built on exploitation.
  • It is built on joint development.

Cooperation and personal development

The ability to collaborate requires development:

  • empathy;
  • communication;
  • responsibility;→
  • listening skills;
  • ability to negotiate;
  • willingness to share knowledge;
  • conflict resolution skills.

Thus, cooperation becomes a school of maturity.

The Law of Cooperation and the Ten Levels of Man

  • Cooperation manifests itself at all levels of personality.
  • Body— joint work and mutual care help maintain health.
  • Subconscious— joint activities form useful habits.
  • Psyche— support from other people strengthens emotional resilience.
  • Consciousness— knowledge sharing accelerates learning.
  • Will— shared commitments help maintain discipline.
  • Soul- Cooperation develops love, compassion and gratitude.
  • Spirit— common values ​​unite people around creative goals.
  • Social connections— become a space of trust and mutual assistance.

Nature— shows many examples of interdependence and cooperation of living systems.

What destroys cooperation?

It is weakened by:

  • lie;
  • egoism;
  • arrogance;
  • distrust;
  • inability to listen;
  • the desire to use people;
  • constant competition for superiority.

When trust disappears, cooperation breaks down.

Practice of the Law of Cooperation

  • It is useful to ask yourself questions every day:
  • Who did I help today?
  • Who did I learn something from today?
  • Did our overall results improve as a result of working together?
  • Do I create trust through my actions?
  • What can I do tomorrow to make our collaboration stronger?
  • Such questions gradually form a culture of co-creation.

The main principle

Personal development does not end with one's own success.

True maturity is demonstrated when a person is able to combine his or her abilities with those of other people for the common good.

Definition

The Law of Cooperation is a fundamental law of the New Psychology of DREVO, according to which the most sustainable development of the individual, society, and civilization is achieved through the voluntary unification of people based on trust, respect, a common goal, and joint creation.

Collaboration is seen as a natural law of life, allowing each person to unlock their potential and create results greater than the sum of their individual efforts.

A single tree can provide shade for one person. A forest can change the climate of an entire region. So it is with people: by pooling their knowledge, labor, and goodwill, they create a future that cannot be built alone.

The Law of Psychological Ecology

The Law of Psychological Ecology in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, the psyche is viewed as a living ecosystem.

Just as nature needs clean water, healthy soil, and clean air, the human psyche needs a healthy information environment, harmonious relationships, adequate rest, and a meaningful life.

Psychological health cannot be maintained if a person’s inner world is constantly exposed to destructive influences.

Therefore, caring for the psyche becomes as natural a necessity as caring for the body.

What is the Law of Psychological Ecology?

The law of psychological ecology is a fundamental principle according to which a person's psychological health depends on the quality of their internal and external environment, and harmonious development requires conscious care for the purity of thoughts, emotions, relationships, information, and lifestyle.

A person constantly interacts with his psychological environment.

And this environment gradually shapes his personality.

Internal ecology

The inner world of a person consists of:

  • thoughts;
  • emotions;→
  • beliefs;
  • habits;
  • memories;
  • values;→
  • internal dialogue.

If the inner space is constantly filled with fear, hatred, lies or self-destruction, the psyche gradually loses stability.

Therefore, caring for the inner world becomes a daily practice.

External ecology of the psyche

The psyche is constantly influenced by:

  • family;→
  • Friends;
  • work environment;
  • culture;→
  • books;
  • music;
  • social media;
  • news;
  • nature;→
  • architecture of the surrounding space.

A person develops in the environment in which he lives.

Therefore, it is important not only to change yourself, but also to create a healthy space around you.

Seven Sources of Psychological Ecology

Body

  • A good night's sleep.
  • Movement.
  • Healthy eating.
  • Physical health supports mental resilience.

Information

  • Quality knowledge.
  • Critical thinking.
  • Limiting information noise.
  • Conscious selection of information sources.

Emotions

  • The ability to understand and experience feelings.
  • Don't suppress them.
  • But don't let them completely control your life.

Relationship

  • Love.
  • Friendship.
  • Trust.
  • Support.

A person remains psychologically more stable when around people he can trust.

Nature

Living world helps restore focus, reduce stress levels and restore a sense of inner balance.

Creative activity

  • Work.
  • Creativity.
  • Training.
  • Helping others.

Creation strengthens psychological resilience because it fills life with meaning.

Inner meaning

When a person understands why he lives, it is easier for him to endure difficulties.

Meaning becomes an internal source of stability.

What pollutes the psyche?

Psychological ecology is violated by:

  • chronic stress;
  • constant fear;
  • information overload;
  • lie;
  • aggressive environment;
  • dependencies;
  • lack of rest;
  • destructive relationships;
  • life without meaning.

Like environmental pollution, psychological pollution rarely occurs instantly.

It accumulates gradually.

How to maintain psychological ecology?

In New Psychology DREVO recommends:

  • maintain a sleep schedule;
  • be outdoors regularly;
  • limit information noise;→
  • develop critical thinking;
  • maintain healthy relationships;
  • engage in physical activity;
  • devote time daily to silence and reflection;
  • keep self-observation diaries;
  • develop gratitude;
  • create a space of beauty and order around you.

Psychological ecology is not a one-time measure.

It's a way of life.

Psychological ecology and society

The psychological health of an individual is linked to the state of society.

  • Culture of communication.
  • Education.
  • Trust.
  • Justice.
  • Safety.
  • All this affects people's mental well-being.

Therefore, caring for psychological ecology becomes a task not only for the individual, but also for the family, community, state, and civilization.

The main principle

A person becomes similar to the environment in which he lives.

Therefore, while caring about the purity of his inner world and the environment, he simultaneously cares about his future.

Definition

The Law of Psychological Ecology is a fundamental law of the New Psychology DREVO, according to which psychological health and harmonious development of the individual depend on the quality of the internal and external environment, and their preservation requires conscious care of thoughts, emotions, relationships, information, lifestyle, and the surrounding environment.

Psychological ecology is viewed as a continuous process of maintaining conditions in which a person is able to maintain internal stability, develop, cooperate and create.

Just as clean soil allows a tree to grow strong, a healthy psychological environment allows a person to reveal their best qualities. By caring for their inner ecology, a person cultivates not only their own happiness but also the future of society.

The Law of Creation

The Law of Creation in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, creation is considered the highest form of realization of human potential.

  • Every person is capable of consuming.
  • Everyone is capable of destruction.
  • But only a mature personality consciously creates something new.
  • Creation is a natural continuation of development.

When a person reveals his abilities, he begins to transform the world around him.

  • It creates knowledge.
  • Relationship.
  • Projects.
  • Beauty.
  • Technologies.
  • Culture.
  • Future.

It is through creation that man becomes an active participant in the evolution of life.

What is the Law of Creation?

The Law of Creation is a fundamental principle that states that personal development reaches its full potential when a person begins to use their knowledge, abilities, and resources to create value that benefits other people, society, nature, and future generations.

Creation is a natural expression of maturity.

Why does man strive to create?

  • Every person has a need to leave a mark.
  • To create something greater than himself.
  • This could be:
  • child;
  • book;
  • painting;
  • now;
  • scientific discovery;
  • invention;
  • community;
  • business;
  • good deed;
  • an idea that changed the lives of other people.
  • Creation gives life depth and meaning.
