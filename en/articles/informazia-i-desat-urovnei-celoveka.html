  • Information affects all levels of personality.
  • Body- through knowledge about health and the formation of habits.
  • Subconscious- through repeated messages and behavior patterns.
  • Psyche- through the emotional impact of content.
  • Consciousness- through the formation of worldview and knowledge.
  • Will- through the ability to manage your attention.
  • Soul— through contact with beauty, culture and moral ideals.
  • Spirit— through the choice of life guidelines and meanings.

Social connections— through the nature of communication and the quality of public dialogue.

Interaction with nature- through understanding one’s place in the surrounding world.

Thus, the information environment influences almost the entire personality.

Practice of information ecology

Every day is useful:

  • read quality books;
  • check important information;
  • limit the flow of random content;
  • set aside time without your phone and social media;
  • develop critical thinking;
  • discuss ideas, not rumors;
  • create your own knowledge and projects;
  • ask a question:

Does this information make me wiser or just occupy my attention?

The main principle

Information becomes part of the personality only when a person allows it to enter his consciousness.

Therefore, responsibility for the quality of your inner world begins with responsibility for the quality of the information you consume.

Definition

Information ecology is a culture of conscious selection, verification, and use of information, aimed at maintaining psychological health, developing critical thinking, and forming a harmonious personality.

In DREVO's New Psychology, information ecology is considered one of the key elements of psychological ecology. It helps people protect their consciousness from information pollution, maintain freedom of thought, and focus their attention on knowledge that promotes development, collaboration, and creativity.

Just as pure water nourishes a tree, so does quality information nourish the mind. Conversely, if the roots are constantly poisoned, the tree gradually loses its strength. Therefore, a person must be not only a consumer of information but also its attentive gardener.

Architecture of a happy life

The Architecture of a Happy Life in New Psychology by DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, a happy life is seen not as a random coincidence, but as the result of consciously constructing one's own personality and the world around one.

Just as a beautiful house cannot be built without a design, a solid foundation and constant maintenance, a happy life also requires interior architecture.

  • It is created gradually.
  • Every choice.
  • Every habit.
  • With every action.

What is the architecture of a happy life?

The architecture of a happy life is a holistic system of principles, values, and practices that enables people to harmoniously develop themselves, build healthy relationships, realize their purpose, and create a prosperous future for themselves and others.

Happiness does not happen by chance.

It is being built.

Foundation

Any sustainable building starts with a foundation.

The foundation of a happy life is made up of:

  • health;→
  • safety;
  • honesty;
  • responsibility;→
  • respect for oneself and others;
  • internal values.

Without a solid foundation, even the greatest achievements become unstable.

Load-bearing walls, columns

The foundation contains the main supports of life.

Body

The source of vital energy.

Without health it is difficult to realize your potential.

Psyche

Source of emotional stability.

It helps to overcome difficulties and maintain inner balance.

Consciousness

The source of understanding the world.

Consciousness helps us distinguish truth from error and make informed decisions.

Will

Source of action.

She transforms knowledge and desires into real actions.

Soul

The source of love, conscience, beauty and inner humanity.

Spirit

The source of life direction.

It helps a person remember why he lives.

Living spaces

A happy life consists of several interconnected areas.

Family

Source of love, support and trust.

Friends and Community

A source of collaboration, exchange of experience and joint growth.

Labor and creativity

A source of self-realization and benefit to society.

Nature

A source of restoration, inspiration and a sense of oneness with life.

Culture and knowledge

The source of worldview, wisdom and development.

Roof of the house

  • The top part of the architecture of a happy life is purpose.
  • When a person understands:
  • what does he live for;
  • what he wants to leave behind;
  • what benefit can I bring to the world,
  • all other elements of life begin to come together into a single system.

Maintaining the home

  • Even the most beautiful house requires constant maintenance.
  • Likewise, a happy life is maintained through daily practices.
  • Taking care of the body.
  • Training.
  • Love.
  • Gratitude.
  • Creative work.
  • Rest.
  • Communication.
  • Development.
  • Happiness cannot be built once and then forgotten.
  • It is supported by every day we live.

Signs of a harmonious life

A person gradually feels:

  • inner peace;
  • physical energy;
  • clarity of thinking;
  • resilience in the face of difficulties;
  • strong relationships;
  • desire to develop;
  • sense of meaning;
  • the joy of creation;→
  • gratitude for life.

This does not mean there are no problems.

This means there is internal support.

Architect of your life

In the New Psychology DREVO, each person is seen as the architect of their own life.

  • Circumstances influence the project.
  • But the final appearance of a home depends on many daily decisions.
  • No one can live life for another person.

Therefore, the responsibility for its construction belongs to the person himself.

The main principle

Happiness does not come from outside.

It is gradually born within a person and manifests itself in the way he lives, loves, works, builds relationships and cares for the world.

The architecture of a happy life is not created by dreams.

It is created by actions.

Definition

The architecture of a happy life is a system for the harmonious development of the individual, based on health, awareness, will, love, purpose, creativity, and responsibility to society and nature.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, a happy life is viewed as the result of the coordinated work of all levels of a person and all spheres of their existence. The stronger the inner foundation of a person and the more harmonious their relationship with the outside world, the more stable their sense of happiness becomes.

Like a tree that grows thanks to strong roots, a strong trunk and care for each new shoot, a person builds a happy life when he daily strengthens his body, develops his mind, opens his soul, strengthens his spirit and bears fruits that others can enjoy.

Karma

Karma in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, karma is viewed not as a mystical punishment or reward, but as a natural law of cause and effect in a person’s life.

  • Every thought.
  • Every decision.
  • Every action.
  • Every habit.
  • Every word.

They gradually change the person himself, his relationships with others and the reality in which he lives.

Karma is the process by which a person creates the consequences of his actions.

What is karma?

Karma is the sum of the consequences of a person's thoughts, decisions, actions, and habits, which gradually shape their personality, relationships, life circumstances, and future.

Karma operates primarily through natural psychological, social and biological mechanisms.

It does not require a mystical explanation.

How is karma formed?

  • Every action leaves a trace.
  • In memory.
  • In habits.
  • In character.
  • In a relationship.
  • In reputation.
  • To health.
  • In society.
  • Gradually, these traces form a person’s life path.

The law of cause and effect

  • The New Psychology of DREVO operates on a simple principle:
  • Thoughts shape decisions.
  • Decisions shape actions.
  • Actions form habits.
  • Habits shape character.
  • Character shapes destiny.

It is this chain that is the psychological mechanism for the formation of karma.

Personal karma

Every person creates his own karma every day.

When he:

  • speaks the truth;
  • helps people;
  • takes care of health;
  • studies;
  • creates useful projects;
  • keeps promises,

he gradually strengthens his own personality.

When a person constantly:

  • deceives;
  • destroys relationships;
  • avoids responsibility;
  • lives only for immediate gain;
  • causes harm to oneself or others,

the consequences gradually become part of his own life.

Collective karma

  • Karma is created not only by an individual person.
  • Family.
  • Community.
  • Organization.
  • State.
  • Civilization.

Every shared culture of behavior gradually creates consequences for all participants.

When a society develops trust, education, cooperation, and respect for individuals, it creates the conditions for its own prosperity.

When violence, corruption and lies prevail, society gradually faces the consequences of these processes.

Karma and freedom

  • Karma does not cancel freedom.
  • On the contrary, it makes freedom meaningful.
  • Every new choice has the potential to change the direction of your life.
  • The past influences the present.
  • But it does not completely determine the future.
  • A person is capable of:
  • realize your mistakes;
  • change habits;
  • repair the damage caused, if possible;
  • start creating a new chain of causes and effects.
  • Therefore, karma is not a sentence.
  • It is a process of constant choice.
