Not all consequences arise from the actions of just one person.

Life is influenced by:

  • natural processes;
  • health;→
  • other people's decisions;
  • social conditions;
  • random events.

However, even in these circumstances, a person is able to choose his attitude, his further actions and the direction of development.

It is this ability that remains within his area of ​​responsibility.

The Law of Cause and Effect and Personality

Every day a person shapes his future.

If he:

  • studies;
  • takes care of health;
  • strengthens relationships;
  • keeps promises;
  • creates useful projects,

then gradually creates the grounds for a more stable and fulfilling life.

If you constantly put off important things, avoid responsibility, or destroy relationships, the consequences also accumulate over time.

The law of cause and effect in society

  • This law also operates at the societal level.
  • Honesty breeds trust.
  • Trust facilitates cooperation.
  • Cooperation creates development.
  • Development improves the quality of life.
  • And vice versa:
  • lies destroy trust;
  • mistrust destroys cooperation;
  • Destroyed cooperation weakens society.

Practice of conscious choice

Before making an important decision, it is useful to ask yourself questions:

  • What consequences could this act have?
  • Will it be useful in a year?
  • In ten years?
  • Does it align with my values?
  • Will it help the development of life?

Thus, a person begins to think not only about today’s results, but also about long-term consequences.

The law of cause and effect and karma

In the New Psychology of DREVO, the concept of karma is considered as one of the manifestations of the law of cause and effect.

Karma is the accumulated consequences of thoughts, decisions, actions and habits.

It is not a mystical punishment.

It is a natural result of human lifestyle.

The main principle

The future does not happen by chance.

It gradually grows out of today's decisions.

It is impossible to change your future without changing the reasons that create it.

Definition

The law of cause and effect is a fundamental principle of personal development, according to which every action, decision, habit, or inaction inevitably creates consequences that affect the person themselves, those around them, and the further development of life.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, this law is considered the foundation of personal responsibility. It reminds us that we cannot control everything that happens, but we can consciously choose the factors that gradually shape our character, relationships, and life path.

Every action today is a seed for the future. What the tree of life will become depends not on one great event, but on the seeds a person sows every day.

The Law of Awareness

The Law of Awareness in New Psychology DREVO

Awareness is the main difference between a person and automatic behavior.

  • A person is born with many innate reflexes and gradually forms habits.
  • Most of our daily activities are performed automatically.
  • This is natural.

But it is the ability to be aware of one’s thoughts, feelings, desires and actions that makes a person a free individual.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, awareness is the basis of all other laws of development.

  • Without it, it is impossible to develop will.
  • It is impossible to change habits.
  • It is impossible to reveal one's purpose.

What is the Law of Awareness?

The Law of Awareness is a fundamental principle that states that a person's level of development is determined by their ability to understand themselves, notice what is happening, make conscious choices, and accept responsibility for the consequences of their decisions.

Mindfulness transforms life from a chain of random reactions into a path of conscious development.

Mindfulness creates freedom

  • Every event causes a reaction.
  • But between the event and the reaction there is a space of choice.
  • It is in this space that freedom is born.

The more a person is able to notice his thoughts, emotions and impulses, the less he becomes their hostage.

Freedom does not begin when restrictions disappear.

It begins when a person is able to choose his reaction.

Four levels of awareness

The first level is body awareness

The man notices:

  • fatigue;
  • voltage;
  • pain;
  • breath;
  • energy;
  • the body's needs.

This is the basis of health.

The second level is awareness of the psyche

A person begins to understand:

  • your emotions;
  • fears;
  • desires;
  • habits;
  • automatic reactions.

He ceases to fully identify himself with his experiences.

The third level is awareness of personality

  • A person asks questions:
  • Who am I?
  • What do I believe in?
  • Why do I make these particular decisions?
  • What values ​​define my life?
  • This is where true self-knowledge begins.

The fourth level is awareness of life

  • A person begins to see himself as part of a wider world.
  • He realizes the connection:
  • with family;
  • community;
  • nature;
  • humanity;
  • future generations.
  • Personal development becomes part of life development.

What hinders awareness?

Awareness is reduced by:

  • constant rush;
  • chronic stress;
  • information noise;
  • life by habit;
  • dependencies;
  • fear of admitting one's own mistakes;
  • lack of time for reflection.

When attention is constantly scattered, it becomes difficult for a person to manage his life.

How to develop mindfulness?

In New Psychology DREVO this is helped by:

  • meditation;
  • autogenic training;
  • thought diary;
  • diary of emotions;
  • diary of desires;→
  • dream diary;→
  • daily reflection;
  • nature observation;
  • honest self-analysis;
  • deep conversations with people.

Mindfulness is developed through regular practice of observing without self-judgment.

Awareness and responsibility

  • Awareness shows a person a choice.
  • Responsibility helps to realize this choice.
  • Without awareness, a person lives automatically.
  • Without responsibility he does not use his freedom.
  • Only together do they become the basis of a mature personality.

Practice of the Law of Awareness

  • It is helpful to ask yourself five questions every day:
  • What do I feel now?
  • What am I thinking about?
  • Why do I do this?
  • Is my action consistent with my values?
  • What would be the most constructive decision now?
  • These questions gradually turn mindfulness into a habit.

The main principle

Mindfulness doesn't make life easier.

It makes a person wiser.

The more a person understands himself and the consequences of his actions, the freer, more responsible and happier his life becomes.

Definition

The Law of Awareness is a fundamental law of the New Psychology DREVO, according to which personal development begins with the ability to observe oneself, understand what is happening, make free choices, and accept responsibility for their consequences.

Mindfulness is the foundation of self-direction, maturity, and inner freedom. It is what allows a person to transform a haphazard life into a meaningful path of development, where thoughts become decisions, decisions become actions, and actions become the foundation of a happy and creative life.

Mindfulness is like light. It doesn't change the world on its own, but it allows you to see the path, discern obstacles, and take the next step with purpose. That's why personal development always begins with the ability to see.

The Law of Will

The Law of Will in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, willpower is viewed as a person’s ability to consciously manage themselves and direct their lives in accordance with their values, goals, and purpose.

  • Desires arise by themselves.
  • Emotions appear on their own.
  • Thoughts often come spontaneously.
  • But the action remains a person’s choice.
  • It is the will that transforms internal choice into action.
  • Without will, knowledge remains theory.
  • Values ​​are intentions.
  • Dreams are fantasies.
  • Will connects consciousness with real life.

What is the Law of Will?

The law of will is a fundamental principle according to which personal development is determined not by the amount of knowledge or desires, but by a person’s ability to consistently transform conscious choices into real actions.

Will makes freedom effective.

Will gives birth to actions

  • Awareness shows possible paths.
  • Will helps to choose one of them.
  • Every conscious action strengthens the ability to manage oneself.
  • Every inaction also becomes a choice and gradually forms a habit.
  • A man becomes what he regularly does.

Four levels of will

The first level is body control

A person learns:

  • observe the regime;
  • take care of your health;
  • overcome laziness;
  • maintain discipline.

This is the foundation for the development of will.

The second level is managing emotions

A person stops acting only under the influence of mood.

He is capable of experiencing fear, anger or anxiety, but does not allow them to completely determine his behavior.

The third level is life management

  • A person begins to make decisions independently.
  • Sets goals.
  • Keeps promises.
  • Finishes what he started.
  • Lives according to his values.

The fourth level is serving the purpose

The highest form of willpower is manifested when a person is able to remain faithful to his purpose and moral principles even under conditions of pressure, difficulties and uncertainty.

Will becomes a force directed not only towards personal success, but also towards creation.

What weakens the will?

The will is gradually weakened:

  • life without goals;
  • constant postponing of important matters;
  • lack of routine;
  • chronic fatigue;
  • dependencies;
  • the habit of looking for excuses;
  • disclaimer.

Weakening of the will rarely occurs immediately.

It develops through daily concessions to oneself.
