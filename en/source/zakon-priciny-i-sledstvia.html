3. The Law of Conscious Choice

  • Freedom begins where conscious choice appears.
  • Consciousness allows us to see different possibilities.
  • Willpower helps to implement the chosen decision.
  • The higher a person’s awareness, the greater his inner freedom.

4. Law of Responsibility

Every freedom comes with responsibility.

A person becomes mature when he stops looking for someone to blame and begins to take responsibility for his own thoughts, words and actions.

Responsibility is the ability to recognize the consequences of one's decisions and to take part in their correction or development.

5. The law of continuous development

  • Development continues throughout life.
  • Every day lived either strengthens a person or gradually weakens him.
  • There is no stop in development.
  • Even inaction changes personality.

6. The Law of Small Steps

Big changes come from small daily actions.

Habits have a more profound influence on personality than rare emotional outbursts.

It is consistency that creates sustainable development.

7. The Law of Overcoming

  • Growth is impossible without overcoming difficulties.
  • Trials in themselves do not make a person stronger.
  • What makes him stronger is how he responds to these challenges.

Every difficulty overcome increases experience, resilience and inner maturity.

8. The Law of Meaning

A person is capable of overcoming difficulties for a long time if he understands why he is doing it.

Meaning transforms efforts into development.

Without meaning, even success can seem empty.

9. Law of Cooperation

Personality develops faster in interaction with other people.

Knowledge sharing, mutual support, co-creation and trust enhance the development of each participant.

True personal growth does not isolate a person from society, but makes him more useful to it.

10. The Law of Psychological Ecology

Everything that a person regularly lets into his life gradually becomes part of his inner world.

  • Information.→
  • Communication.
  • Music.
  • Culture.→
  • Nature.→
  • Digital environment.
  • Every external factor influences the quality of inner life.

Therefore, caring for your internal ecology is just as important as caring for your physical health.

11. The Law of Creation

Development reaches maturity when a person begins not only to receive, but also to create.

Creativity can manifest itself in raising children, scientific discoveries, art, entrepreneurship, caring for nature, helping people, or sharing knowledge.

A person reveals himself most fully when his abilities become a source of benefit to others.

12. Law of Inheritance

The highest form of development is the ability to leave behind not only material results, but also knowledge, culture, good deeds, healthy relationships and conditions for the development of future generations.

A person becomes truly mature when he begins to think not only about his own life, but also about what kind of world will remain after it.

The main law of DREVO

  • Everything is interconnected.
  • By developing oneself, a person influences the family.
  • By developing the family, he influences society.
  • By developing society, it influences nature.
  • By changing the world around him, he again changes himself.

Therefore, true personal development is not a movement towards personal perfection at any cost, but a continuous strengthening of harmony between the individual, society and the surrounding world.

It is in this harmony that the New Psychology of DREVO sees the basis of health, freedom, maturity and a creative future.

New Psychology as a Unified Living System

Author: Oleksandr Rud

@sssvetomir

This is not just a book on psychology.

This is an attempt to take a new look at a person - as a holistic, living and continuously developing system in which the body, psyche, consciousness, will, soul, spirit, values, relationships and the surrounding world form a single whole.

The new psychology of DREVO combines modern scientific knowledge with a philosophical understanding of human nature. Its goal is not only to help people overcome psychological difficulties but also to unlock their inner potential, teach them to live consciously and harmoniously, collaborate, create, and leave a positive legacy.

This book invites the reader to become an explorer of their own personality and to follow the path from self-knowledge to development, from development to creation, and from creation to service to life.

Like a tree, a person grows throughout their life. And the deeper their roots, the higher they can rise and the more benefit they can bring to the world.

PREAMBLE

New Psychology DREVO

Humanity is entering an era of profound change.

The development of artificial intelligence, biotechnology, the digital environment, global communications, and changing lifestyles require a new understanding of humanity. We have learned to create highly complex technologies, but we still lack a clear understanding of ourselves.

Modern psychology has made significant advances in the study of emotions, behavior, memory, thinking, and brain function. However, a person cannot be reduced to neural processes, individual mental functions, or social roles.

It is a complex living system in which the body, subconscious, psyche, consciousness, will, soul, spirit, values, relationships with people and interaction with the outside world continuously influence each other.

The new psychology of DREVO suggests considering a person as such an integral system.

It does not reject the achievements of modern science, but seeks to combine knowledge from psychology, neuroscience, biology, medicine, pedagogy, philosophy, ecology and social sciences into a single model of personal development.

At the same time, New Psychology clearly distinguishes between two areas of knowledge.

Where scientific evidence exists, it relies on modern research.

Where the subject is the soul, spirit, meaning of life, values ​​or inner experience of a person, she views them as philosophical categories open to further research and understanding.

The main goal of New Psychology is not only to help people cope with psychological difficulties.

  • Its task is much broader.
  • Help a person become healthier.
  • More free.
  • More conscious.
  • More responsible.
  • More creative.
  • Happier.
  • More capable of love.
  • More able to collaborate.

More capable of creating a world in which future generations can live with dignity.

At the center of the New Psychology DREVO is the idea of ​​development.

A person is not seen as a bearer of problems, diagnoses or limitations.

He is seen as a constantly evolving individual with enormous potential for learning, creation, collaboration and transformation of the world around him.

  • The basis of this system is several interrelated principles.
  • Human integrity.
  • Development instead of fighting symptoms.
  • Awareness instead of automatism.
  • Responsibility instead of looking for the guilty.
  • Cooperation instead of destructive competition.
  • Creation instead of consumption.
  • Psychological ecology instead of information pollution.
  • Respect for life in all its manifestations.

The new psychology of DREVO views personal development as a path that passes through many interconnected stages.

  • From understanding your own body to understanding your psyche.
  • From the psyche to awareness.
  • From awareness to free choice.
  • From choice to will.
  • From will to habits.
  • From habits to character.
  • From character to creation.
  • From creation to service.
  • From service to legacy.

Like a tree, a person takes root in life, strengthens his inner trunk, reveals his abilities, creates fruits and leaves behind new seeds from which future generations can grow.

That is why the symbol of this system is a tree.

It reflects continuous growth, the interconnectedness of all parts of a living system, the ability to overcome difficulties and benefit the surrounding world.

  • The new psychology of DREVO is not only a personality theory.
  • This is a philosophy of development.
  • A practical system for raising a mature person.
  • The basis of psychological ecology of the future.

And an invitation to a shared exploration of human nature, in which science, philosophy, culture, and personal experience help us to understand humanity more deeply and unlock our creative potential.

By developing a person, we develop society. By developing society, we create the future. By developing the future, we preserve life.
