The Superconscious in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, the superconscious is considered as a level of personality associated with going beyond the limits of already accumulated experience.

If the subconscious stores the past, and the conscious is aware of the present and makes choices, then the superconscious helps a person discover what is not yet in his personal experience.

It is thanks to the superconscious that a person is able to create something new.

Main function

The main function of the superconscious is the birth of new opportunities.

It manifests itself in:

  • creativity;
  • inspiration;
  • scientific discoveries;
  • artistic thinking;
  • deep intuition;
  • ability to see the big picture;
  • non-standard solutions to complex problems;
  • the emergence of new meanings.

The superconscious does not repeat the known.

It helps to go beyond it.

How does the superconscious mind work?

The new psychology of DREVO views the superconscious as a process in which consciousness ceases to be limited only by already known knowledge and becomes open to new combinations of ideas, deep insights, and creative exploration.

Modern cognitive psychology describes many such phenomena through processes of unconscious information processing, insight, creativity, and associative thinking. In the DREVO model, the term "supraconscious" unites these processes into a single philosophical concept.

Sources of the superconscious

The superconscious develops thanks to:

  • deep study of knowledge;
  • reflection;
  • creativity;
  • observation of nature;
  • scientific research;
  • art;
  • long-term concentration;
  • open exploration of the new;
  • dialogue with other people.

The richer a person's experience and the freer his thinking, the more opportunities there are for new ideas to emerge.

Superconsciousness and consciousness

  • Consciousness asks a question.
  • The superconscious helps to see an unexpected answer.
  • Consciousness analyzes.
  • The superconscious unites.
  • Consciousness moves step by step.

The superconscious mind sometimes allows us to see the solution as a whole.

That is why great discoveries are often born after long preparation and a period of inner searching.

Superconsciousness and soul

  • The soul helps a person remain true to his inner nature.
  • The superconscious helps to find new ways of its manifestation.
  • One is responsible for authenticity.
  • The other is for creativity.

Together they allow a person to create something that did not exist before.

How does the superconscious develop?

  • The superconscious cannot be forced to work.
  • But it is possible to create conditions for its development.
  • These conditions are:
  • continuous learning;
  • curiosity;
  • exploratory thinking;
  • creativity;
  • silence and concentration;
  • contemplation of nature;
  • openness to new ideas;
  • respect for facts;
  • willingness to reconsider one's own views.

The superconscious develops where a person stops looking only for confirmation of what is already known and begins to seek a new understanding of the world.

The main principle

  • The subconscious stores the past.
  • Consciousness chooses the present.
  • The superconscious reveals the future.

It is thanks to the superconscious that a person is able not only to repeat the experience of previous generations, but also to expand the boundaries of human knowledge and culture.

Definition

The superconscious is a conceptual level of personality in the New Psychology of DREVO, associated with creative search, the emergence of new ideas, a holistic vision, and the ability to go beyond the boundaries of accumulated experience.

It helps people create knowledge, art, technology and new ways of understanding the world.

It is the superconscious that transforms the accumulated experience of humanity into discoveries that become the basis for future development.

Soul

The Soul in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, the soul is viewed as the inner core of the personality, the source of its authenticity, moral choice and striving for love, beauty and creation.

  • The soul does not answer the question,Howa man lives.
  • She answers the question:
  • "Who am I?"

It is through the soul that a person feels his own uniqueness and inner integrity.

What is the soul?

The soul is that inner principle through which a person experiences himself as a unique individual.

It unites:

  • self-esteem;
  • Love;→
  • conscience;
  • compassion;
  • the ability to admire beauty;
  • striving for truth;
  • deep values;
  • experiencing the meaning of life.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, the soul is considered as a philosophical concept that describes a person’s inner experience and their personal integrity.

The main function of the soul

The main function of the soul is to preserve the authenticity of the personality.

It is the soul that helps a person not to lose himself among circumstances, social roles and external pressure.

  • It helps to distinguish:
  • not only profitable,
  • but also correct;
  • not only pleasant,
  • but also valuable;
  • not only possible,
  • but also worthy.

Soul and consciousness

Consciousness helps us understand what is happening.

The soul helps to understand whether what is happening corresponds to the inner nature of a person.

  • Consciousness responds:
  • "What's happening?"
  • The soul answers:
  • "Does this match who I want to be?"

Soul and will

  • The soul determines inner values.
  • Will helps to live in accordance with them.
  • Without will, even the highest aspirations can remain just wishes.

Without a soul, a strong will can be used for both creation and destruction.

Soul and spirit

  • In New Psychology DREVO these concepts have different functions.
  • The soul is responsible for the inner essence of a person.
  • The spirit determines the direction of its development.
  • The soul retains its authenticity.
  • The spirit helps to bring it to life.

Revealing the Soul

  • The soul opens up gradually.
  • It becomes noticeable through a person's actions.
  • It cannot be revealed by reflection alone.
  • It is revealed through:
  • Love;→
  • honesty;
  • creativity;
  • care;
  • service;→
  • Gratitude;→
  • responsibility;→
  • respect for life;
  • striving for truth.

Every creative act helps a person to more fully express his inner essence.

Signs of an open soul

A man with an open soul:

  • maintains dignity;
  • capable of love;
  • respects other people;
  • strives for justice;
  • knows how to sympathize;
  • creates more than it destroys;
  • remains true to his values;
  • brings benefits to the surrounding world.

An open soul makes a person a source of trust, inspiration and creation.

The main principle

  • The soul is not revealed through possession.
  • It is revealed through manifestation.
  • It is not what a person has,
  • and what kind of person he becomes,
  • shows the depth of his soul's revelation.

Definition

The soul is the inner core of the personality, uniting its authenticity, conscience, love, moral values ​​and the desire for creation.

In DREVO's New Psychology, the soul is viewed as a philosophical concept that helps describe a person's inner integrity, their ability to remain true to themselves, and to translate their values ​​into action.

The soul is revealed not by words, but by life. The more love, wisdom, responsibility, and creativity a person displays in their actions, the more fully their soul is revealed.

Spirit

Spirit in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, the spirit is seen as an internal force that determines the direction of human development.

  • If the soul answers the question:
  • "Who am I?"
  • then the spirit answers:
  • "Who do I strive to become and what do I live for?"
  • The spirit guides a person forward.

It helps you not to rest on your laurels, to overcome difficulties and to realize your values ​​in life.

What is spirit?

Spirit is the inner capacity of a person to rise above his limitations for a higher purpose.

It manifests itself in:

  • striving for truth;
  • freedom;→
  • responsibility;→
  • courage;
  • dignity;
  • selflessness;
  • the desire for creation;
  • readiness to serve life.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, spirit is viewed as a philosophical concept that describes the inner direction of the individual and their ability to live for values ​​that go beyond momentary desires.

The main function of the spirit

  • The main function of the spirit is to set the direction of development.
  • The body is responsible for life.
  • The psyche is for perception.
  • Consciousness is for choice.
  • Will — for action.

The spirit is responsible for the direction in which a person uses all this.

It helps not just to move, but to understand where and why the movement is directed.

Spirit and will

  • Will is responsible for the ability to control oneself.
  • The spirit is responsible for the meaning of this management.
  • You can have a strong will, but use it for destruction.
  • The spirit connects willpower with moral values ​​and creative purpose.

Therefore, a strong spirit is not only the ability to overcome difficulties, but also the ability to choose a path that preserves human dignity and respect for life.

Spirit and soul

  • Soul and spirit are not opposed to each other.
  • They perform different tasks.
  • The soul preserves the inner authenticity of a person.
  • The Spirit helps this authenticity become a way of life.
  • The soul is responsible for the inner content.
  • Spirit - for direction and embodiment.
  • The soul is the source of values.
  • The spirit transforms values ​​into a way of life.

How is the spirit strengthened?

The spirit develops through real actions.

It is strengthened:

  • acceptance of responsibility;
  • true to one's word;
  • overcoming difficulties;→
  • service to people;
  • honesty;
  • discipline;→
  • the desire for knowledge;
  • creative work;→
  • protection of human dignity;
  • concern for future generations.

Every action that is consistent with internal values ​​makes the spirit stronger.

Signs of a strong spirit

A man with a strong spirit:

  • does not give up his values ​​under pressure of circumstances;
  • able to maintain dignity even in difficult times;
  • does not look for the guilty, but looks for solutions;
  • knows how to take responsibility;
  • inspires others by his own example;
  • uses his power for protection and creation, not for domination.

Strength of spirit is not measured by power over other people.

It is measured by the ability to remain true to the best in oneself.

The main principle

The spirit does not exist for the sake of power, superiority or glory.

It exists for the development of life.

The stronger a person's spirit becomes, the greater his ability to create, unite, protect and inspire.

Definition

Spirit is the inner strength and direction of personal development that allows a person to live in accordance with their highest values, consciously overcome difficulties, and use their abilities for creation.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, spirit is viewed as a philosophical concept that reflects a person's ability to transcend selfish interests, accept responsibility for their actions, and direct their lives for the benefit of people, society, and future generations.

If the soul answers the question “Who am I?”, then the spirit answers the question “Who am I becoming and what trace will I leave behind?”
