Personality Development Map

Personality Development Map in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, personality development is viewed as the gradual growth of a tree.

  • It is impossible for a tree to develop only one branch.
  • If the roots are weak, the whole organism suffers.
  • If there is no light, growth stops.
  • If the crown does not develop, the tree stops bearing fruit.
  • A person develops in the same way.
  • The personality is a single system where all levels are interconnected.
  • The development of one level helps the development of the others.
  • Weakening one level gradually affects the entire system.
