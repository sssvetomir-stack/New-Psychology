The legacy of man

The Legacy of Man in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, a person's life is valued not only by what they have received, but also by what they leave behind.

  • True heritage is not just property.
  • Not just money.
  • Not just fame.

The most valuable legacy is the change that continues to benefit long after the person is gone.

A person becomes part of the future through his heritage.
