Ten levels of man

Man as a multi-level system

In the New Psychology DREVO, a person is viewed as a holistic living system consisting of ten interconnected levels.

Each level performs its own function.

None of them exist separately.

Personal development occurs when all levels are in a state of harmony and support each other.

I. Body

Function:life and interaction with the material world.

The body is the foundation of a person.

It provides movement, metabolism, perception of the environment, restoration and biological existence.

The main question:

"How to preserve and strengthen life?"

II. Subconscious

Function:automatic life management.

The subconscious is a deep system of biological and acquired programs.

It includes innate mechanisms, habits, automatic reactions, bodily memory, adaptation and accumulated experience.

The main question:

"What experiences are already guiding my reactions?"

III. Psyche

Function:perception and processing of information.

The psyche unites emotions, memory, thinking, imagination, attention and learning.

It helps a person understand what is happening and adapt to the surrounding world.

The main question:

"What is happening around me and inside me?"

IV. Consciousness

Function:awareness and choice.

Consciousness allows a person to observe himself, analyze what is happening and make informed decisions.

  • It is consciousness that makes a person the author of his own life.
  • The main question:
  • "What do I choose?"

V. Will

Function:self-management.

Willpower is the ability to control oneself and make conscious choices.

It connects intention with action, helping people follow their values ​​even when it takes effort.

The main question:

"Am I able to implement my choice?"

VI. Soul

Function:the inner core of personality.

In the DREVO philosophical model, the soul is the source of authenticity, love, conscience, beauty and deep values.

  • She is responsible for the internal integrity of a person.
  • The main question:
  • "Who am I?"

VII. Spirit

Function:direction of development.

The spirit determines the vector of a person’s life.

It is associated with purpose, courage, responsibility and the desire to create.

The main question:

"What do I live for?"

VIII. Values ​​and Meaning

Function:internal compass.

Values ​​help us distinguish between what is important and what is unimportant.

Meaning unites all levels of personality into a single direction of life.

The main question:

"Why this particular path?"

IX. Social Connections

  • Function:joint development.
  • A person reveals himself through relationships.
  • Family.→
  • Genus.→
  • Community.→
  • Society.→

It is through cooperation that a person realizes a significant part of his potential.

The main question:

"Who am I growing with and what am I creating with others?"

X. Interaction with the environment

Function:the unity of man and the world.

Humans constantly interact with nature, society, culture and technology.

He not only receives the influence of the environment, but also changes it himself with his decisions and actions.

The main question:

“What world shapes me and what world do I create?”
