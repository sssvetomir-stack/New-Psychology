Community development

Community Development in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, community is viewed as a space for the voluntary association of people bound by common values, goals, and a desire to co-create the future.

If the family is the first environment for personality development, then the community becomes the first school of cooperation.

It is in communities that a person learns to combine his abilities with the abilities of other people.

Community development is the development of a culture of trust, mutual responsibility and collective creativity.
