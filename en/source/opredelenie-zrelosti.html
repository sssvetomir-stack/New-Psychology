Definition of maturity

Maturity in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, maturity is not viewed as age or as the number of years lived.

  • A person may be mature in age but remain immature in his actions.
  • And vice versa, a young person can exhibit a high degree of maturity.
  • Maturity is not determined by time.
  • It is determined by the level of personality development.

This is the ability to consciously manage your life, maintain internal stability and create good for yourself and the world around you.
