The Law of Creation

The Law of Creation in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, creation is considered the highest form of realization of human potential.

  • Every person is capable of consuming.
  • Everyone is capable of destruction.
  • But only a mature personality consciously creates something new.
  • Creation is a natural continuation of development.

When a person reveals his abilities, he begins to transform the world around him.

  • It creates knowledge.
  • Relationship.→
  • Projects.
  • Beauty.→
  • Technologies.→
  • Culture.→
  • Future.→

It is through creation that man becomes an active participant in the evolution of life.
