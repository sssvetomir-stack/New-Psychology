Interaction of Biofields in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, a person is viewed as an open living system, constantly interacting with the environment and other people.

Each organism creates bioelectrical and biomagnetic activity associated with the functioning of the heart, brain, nervous system and other organs.

  • When people meet, different types of interactions occur simultaneously.
  • Biological.
  • Psychological.
  • Social.
  • Informational.

And, according to the DREVO research model, physiological interactions related to the body's bioelectric processes are also possible.

Four levels of interaction

The first level is physiological

People influence each other through voice, touch, smell, facial expressions, body temperature, breathing, and a variety of other biological signals.

These mechanisms are well studied by modern science.

The second level is psychological

The emotions, mood and behavior of one person can influence the emotional state of another.

Empathy, emotional contagion, and behavioral synchronization are well-known psychological phenomena.

The third level is informational

People constantly exchange knowledge, ideas, symbols and culture.

It is this level that ensures the development of language, science, art and civilization.

The fourth level is biofield

In New Psychology DREVO, a research hypothesis is put forward that bioelectric processes of different organisms can also be involved in human interactions.

The extent of such interaction, its mechanisms and significance require further study using biophysics, neuroscience and physiology.

This hypothesis is not considered an established scientific fact.

Harmonization

When people work together, talk, sing, dance, meditate, exercise, or create something together, they often develop a coherence of movement, attention, breathing, and emotional state.

Scientific research shows that such synchronization can be observed at the level of behavior and some physiological processes.

The DREVO model suggests that such processes may be part of a broader phenomenon of human systems alignment.

Biofield and relationships

Interaction between people is never determined solely by biofield processes.

It is simultaneously influenced by:

  • words;
  • actions;
  • trust;→
  • past experience;
  • values;→
  • culture;→
  • physiological state;
  • environment.→

Therefore, the biofield is considered as one of the possible components of a complex system of human communication, and not as its only basis.

Psychological ecology of communication

Every meeting between people leaves a trace.

We pass on to each other:

  • knowledge;→
  • emotions;→
  • mood;
  • attitude;
  • confidence;
  • anxiety;
  • inspiration.

Therefore, caring about our own internal state simultaneously becomes caring about the people with whom we interact.

The Future of Research

New Psychology DREVO considers the study of the interaction of biofields as a promising direction of science.

The development of sensitive methods for recording physiological processes, biophysics, neuroscience, and computational modeling can expand our understanding of how people influence each other.

Any new findings must be based on reproducible research and critical scientific scrutiny.

The main principle

  • Man never exists in isolation.
  • Every meeting becomes a mutual exchange.
  • We influence each other with words.
  • By actions.
  • Emotions.→
  • Behavior.

And possibly some physiological processes that are not yet fully understood.

Definition

Biofield interaction is a research concept of the New Psychology DREVO, which suggests that the physiological bioelectric processes of the body can be one of the factors of interaction between people, along with psychological, social and informational mechanisms.

The new DREVO psychology views humans as open systems in which internal states influence the quality of communication, collaboration, and shared development. Regardless of the mechanisms of this influence confirmed by future research, developing respect, trust, empathy, and responsibility remains the foundation of healthy human relationships.

When one person develops, it's not just they who change. The space of relationships they are a part of changes.

Soul, biofield and spirit

Anthropological Model of New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, a person is viewed as a multidimensional system in which biological, psychological and philosophical levels form a single whole.

  • Each level performs its own function.
  • The body provides the material basis for life.
  • The subconscious controls automatic processes.
  • The psyche perceives and processes information.
  • Consciousness makes a choice.
  • Will turns choice into action.
  • The superconscious opens up new possibilities.
  • The soul preserves the internal integrity of the personality.
  • The spirit sets the direction of development.

Despite the differences in functions, all levels interact with each other.

Soul and biofield

In the DREVO philosophical model, the soul is considered as the inner core of the personality.

The biofield is considered a physiological manifestation of the body's vital activity, arising due to the work of the heart, brain, nervous system and other organs.

DREVO puts forward a research hypothesis that the biofield can serve as one of the levels of manifestation of a person’s holistic state and reflect the coherence of his physiological processes.

In this case, the soul is not identified with the biofield.

The biofield is a measurable physiological phenomenon.

Soul is a philosophical concept that describes the inner authenticity of a person.

The connection between them is considered a subject for further research, rather than an established scientific fact.

Spirit as a directed flow

In the New Psychology of DREVO, the spirit is seen as a force that gives direction to human development.

In many philosophical and spiritual traditions, the spirit is symbolically described as a vertical flow that connects a person with what he considers to be the highest reality, highest values, truth, nature, God, or the universal order of the world.

DREVO uses this image as a philosophical model.

The vertical direction symbolizes a person’s desire to go beyond everyday needs and live for a higher purpose.

For some people, this source is religious beliefs.

For others, it is truth, love, science, service to society, nature, or human dignity.

Thus, the spirit is understood not as a physical flow of energy, but as the direction of the individual towards what he considers to be the highest value.

The horizontal and vertical axes of a person

The DREVO model proposes to consider human development through two interconnected axes.

  • Horizontal axisreflects the interaction of man with the material world:
  • Body → Subconscious → Psyche → Consciousness → Society → Nature.
  • She answers the question:
  • "How am I living?"
  • vertical axisreflects the internal development of the individual:
  • Biofield → Soul → Spirit → Values ​​→ Purpose.
  • She answers the question:
  • "What do I live for?"
  • The intersection of these two axes forms the integral personality.

The principle of consistency

The more the body, psyche, consciousness, values, soul and spirit are aligned with each other, the more integral a person becomes.

This consistency is demonstrated by:

  • in inner peace;
  • in clarity of thinking;
  • in resilience in the face of difficulties;
  • in the ability to love;
  • in creative activities;
  • in respect for life.

The main principle

The new psychology of DREVO considers a person as a unity of two dimensions.

Horizontalhelps a person live in peace.

Verticalhelps to understand what he lives for.

Harmonious development begins when both axes are united into a single system, where knowledge becomes wisdom, freedom becomes responsibility, and life becomes a source of creation.

Definition

In DREVO's New Psychology, the soul is viewed as the philosophical core of the personality, the biofield as a physiological manifestation of the body's vital activity, and the spirit as an internal vector that guides a person toward higher values ​​and purpose.

Their interrelationship forms a holistic model of a person, uniting the physical, psychological and ideological dimensions of the personality and serving as a basis for further research and development practice.

Part III. Laws of Personality Development

The law of life

Life strives for development.

Every living organism has an internal drive for growth, adaptation and self-preservation.

Development is a natural state of man.

Stopping development leads to gradual degradation.

Law of Integrity

A human being is a single system.

The body, subconscious, psyche, consciousness, superconsciousness, soul, spirit, values, social connections and the environment constantly influence each other.

It is impossible to change one part of the system without affecting the others.

The law of cause and effect

  • Every thought influences emotions.
  • Emotions influence decisions.
  • Decisions become actions.
  • Actions become habits.
  • Habits shape character.
  • Character determines a person's life path.

The Law of Awareness

  • Development begins with observation.
  • A person can change only what he can understand.
  • Mindfulness turns automatic life into free choice.

The Law of Will

Willpower is the ability to control oneself and make conscious choices.

The greater a person's ability to control his thoughts, emotions and actions, the greater his inner freedom.

Law of Responsibility

Freedom is impossible without responsibility.

Every decision has consequences.

A person becomes mature when he accepts responsibility for his thoughts, words, actions and their impact on other people and the world around him.

The Law of Harmony

True development does not destroy the system.

The development of the body should not come at the cost of the destruction of the psyche.

Personal development should not destroy the family.

Economic development should not destroy nature.

Harmony is the ability to develop while maintaining the stability of the entire system.

The Law of Overcoming

  • Growth is impossible without overcoming difficulties.
  • Challenges are not the opposite of growth.
  • They are a natural part of it.

By overcoming obstacles, a person develops strength, wisdom and resilience.

Law of Cooperation

  • A person reveals his potential through interaction with other people.
  • Competition promotes the development of individual abilities.
  • Cooperation promotes the development of civilization.

The Law of Psychological Ecology

A person's inner world depends on the environment in which he lives.

Information, relationships, culture, nature and technology constantly shape the state of the psyche.

Taking care of your internal and external environment is a necessary condition for psychological health.

The Law of Creation

The highest form of development is creation.

Human development becomes complete when he begins to create not only for himself, but also for others.

Creation transforms knowledge into culture, work into heritage, and life into a source of social development.

The Law of Destiny

Each person has a unique combination of abilities, experiences and values.

Purpose is revealed when a person uses his abilities for the development of life, society and future generations.

Law of Inheritance

The true value of life is determined not by the amount accumulated, but by what a person leaves behind.

  • Knowledge.→
  • Children.
  • Culture.→
  • Scientific discoveries.
  • Good works.
  • Created communities.
  • Restored nature.
  • True development continues even after man's departure.
