Responsibility to the future

In the New Psychology of DREVO, man is seen not only as the heir of the past, but also as the creator of the future.

Each generation inherits language, culture, knowledge, technology, nature and social institutions.

But each generation also determines what will be passed on next.

Therefore, the psychology of future generations studies not only the development of modern man, but also the psychological principles of creating a world in which people of the future can live harmoniously.

Future generations begin today

  • Future generations are not yet born.
  • But the conditions for their life are already being created.
  • Every decision a person makes affects the future.
  • Raising children.
  • Development of sciences.
  • Conservation of nature.
  • Creation of new technologies.
  • Culture of communication.
  • Education.

All these actions shape the world that will one day become real for new generations.

Intergenerational responsibility

  • In DREVO's New Psychology, responsibility extends beyond contemporaries.
  • It includes responsibility to people who have not yet been born.
  • This means striving to:
  • preserve nature;
  • develop education;
  • create knowledge;
  • strengthen peace;
  • develop a culture of cooperation;
  • use technology responsibly;
  • build sustainable social institutions.

The future cannot be protected by itself.

It is created by today's decisions.

Psychological legacy

A person passes on not only material values ​​to future generations.

He conveys:

  • language;
  • culture;→
  • knowledge;→
  • scientific discoveries;
  • works of art;
  • moral guidelines;
  • methods of communication;
  • attitude towards nature;
  • attitude to work;
  • ability to cooperate.

In many ways, it is this legacy that determines the quality of future society.

Education of the future

The main goal of education is not only to transmit knowledge.

It should develop a person who is capable of thinking independently, learning, collaborating and making responsible decisions.

Future generations will require qualities that cannot be completely replaced by technology:

  • critical thinking;→
  • creativity;
  • emotional maturity;
  • ability to cooperate;
  • ethical thinking;
  • environmental responsibility;
  • readiness for continuous learning.

Technology and the Future

Artificial intelligence, biotechnology, robotics and new forms of communication will increasingly influence the life of society.

Therefore, future generations must not only master technology.

They must understand:

  • why are they used;
  • what opportunities do they open up;
  • what risks do they create;
  • What responsibility do their creators and users bear?

The development of technology must be accompanied by human development.

Future generations and nature

  • The future cannot be imagined without a healthy natural environment.
  • Clean water.
  • Fertile soil.
  • Diversity of living organisms.
  • Stable climate.

All these conditions are not only ecological, but also psychological resources for the future of humanity.

Caring for nature becomes caring for the people who will live after us.

Cosmic thinking

The new psychology of DREVO proposes to expand the time horizon of human thinking.

A mature person is able to think not only about today.

He considers the consequences of his decisions over years, decades and generations.

It is this kind of long-term thinking that becomes the basis for a sustainable civilization.

The main principle

Each generation receives the world for temporary use.

Its task is to pass it on to the next generation more developed, more secure, more just and richer in knowledge than it was received.

True development is not measured by how much a person has consumed.

It is measured by what he left behind.

Definition

The Psychology of Future Generations is a section of DREVO's New Psychology that studies the psychological principles of human responsibility to the people of the future, the patterns of transmission of cultural, moral, and intellectual heritage, and the conditions necessary for the sustainable development of civilization.

In DREVO's New Psychology, the future generation is viewed as a full-fledged participant in humanity's moral space. Although these people haven't yet been born, today's decisions already influence their quality of life.

A person's highest maturity is revealed when he begins to live not only for himself and his generation, but also for those who will one day continue the history of humanity.

Examples of positive affirmations, desires and thoughts

The practice of internal dialogue in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, words are viewed as a tool for shaping thinking, attention, and behavior.

A person constantly conducts an internal dialogue.

This dialogue can increase anxiety, fear and helplessness.

And it can strengthen confidence, calmness, responsibility and the desire for development.

Therefore, it is important to consciously choose thoughts that help you live and develop.

Affirmations are not magic spells.

They do not change reality by themselves.

Their task is to help a person change their own thinking, attitude towards themselves and willingness to act.

Health affirmations

  • My body is getting stronger and healthier every day.
  • I take care of my health with respect and love.
  • Every choice I make strengthens my body.
  • My breathing is calm and free.
  • I am filled with energy and vitality.

Affirmations of calm

  • I remain calm under any circumstances.
  • I can manage my emotions.
  • My consciousness is clear and focused.
  • I choose conscious reactions over impulsive ones.
  • Every day brings me new experiences and new opportunities.

Affirmations of will

  • I keep the promises I make to myself.
  • Every day I become more disciplined.
  • I finish what I start.
  • My actions are consistent with my values.
  • I control my habits, they don't control me.

Love affirmations

  • My heart is open to love and goodness.
  • I treat people with respect.
  • I know how to receive and give care.
  • I build relationships based on trust.
  • My presence makes the world a little kinder.

Affirmations for Development

  • Every day I learn something new.
  • I reveal my abilities.
  • I calmly learn from my mistakes.
  • My development continues throughout my life.
  • I create the future with my actions today.

Affirmations of Purpose

  • I am gradually discovering my purpose.
  • My abilities benefit people.
  • I create projects that make the world a better place.
  • Every day brings me closer to my path.
  • I live in harmony with my values.

Examples of desires

In the Diary of Desires, it is recommended to write them down in the present tense and in a positive form.

  • I live in a strong and loving family.
  • I am healthy, energetic and full of strength.
  • I speak several languages ​​fluently.
  • I am successfully developing my business.
  • I create projects that help people.
  • I live in a beautiful, cozy house.
  • I am surrounded by honest and reliable friends.
  • I am financially free and manage my resources responsibly.
  • I develop as a person every day.
  • I live in harmony with nature.

Thoughts that strengthen personality

It is helpful to regularly remind yourself:

  • Any great undertaking begins with the first step.
  • Mistakes are a source of experience if I draw conclusions.
  • Today I can become a little better than yesterday.
  • My attention determines the direction of my life.
  • I am responsible for my decisions.
  • My development is more important than other people's assessments.
  • True strength is found in the ability to control oneself.
  • Everything great is created gradually.
  • The good I do today changes the future.
  • I choose creation over destruction.

Morning Affirmations

  • After waking up:
  • Today will be a good day.
  • I meet him calmly and consciously.
  • My body is full of energy.
  • My mind is clear.
  • My heart is open to people.
  • Today I will take at least one step towards my destiny.

Evening affirmations

  • Before bed:
  • I am grateful for the day I lived.
  • Today I became a little wiser.
  • My body is recovering.
  • My psyche calms down.
  • Tomorrow I will continue my development.
  • I fall asleep calmly and with trust in life.

The main rule

  • The affirmation must comply with the three principles of DREVO.
  • Truthfulness.
  • She must not deny the obvious reality.
  • Instead of:
  • "I don't have any problems."
  • Better:
  • "I am able to gradually solve problems that arise."
  • Creativity.
  • It should help in personal development.
  • Action.
  • A good affirmation inspires real action.
  • After it you want to act.

The main principle

  • Thoughts gradually become words.
  • Words become decisions.
  • Decisions become actions.
  • Actions become habits.
  • Habits shape personality.

Therefore, a person changes his life not by repeating beautiful phrases, but by allowing these thoughts to gradually change his daily actions.

Definition

Affirmations are consciously chosen positive statements designed to strengthen constructive thinking, develop inner resilience, and support actions that align with a person's values.

In DREVO's New Psychology, affirmations are viewed as a tool for mental self-regulation and the development of internal dialogue. Their purpose is not to replace reality, but to help people see a direction for their development, strengthen motivation, and affirm their values ​​daily through concrete actions.

Words become power when they are followed by thoughts. Thoughts become reality when they are followed by actions.
