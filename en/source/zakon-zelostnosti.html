The Law of Integrity in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, a person is viewed as a single living system.

  • Body.→
  • Subconscious.→
  • Psyche.→
  • Consciousness.→
  • Will.→
  • Soul.→
  • Spirit.→
  • Values.→
  • Social connections.→
  • Interaction with nature.→
  • All these levels do not exist separately from each other.
  • They interact constantly.
  • Changing one level inevitably affects the others.
  • This is why personal development must be holistic.
