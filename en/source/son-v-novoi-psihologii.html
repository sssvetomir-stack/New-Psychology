Sleep in the New Psychology of DREVO

In the New Psychology DREVO, sleep is considered one of the most important processes of human recovery.

  • During sleep, it is not only the body that rests.
  • The brain continues to work.
  • The functioning of the nervous system is stabilized.
  • Hormonal balance is regulated.
  • Memory is consolidated.
  • The experiences of the past day are being processed.

Therefore, the quality of sleep affects almost all levels of personality.
