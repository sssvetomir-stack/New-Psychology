01 · Core idea
A person comes into the world not only to survive, but also to grow, love, cooperate, create, and leave a legacy.
Why a new perspective is needed

A person is more
than a set of symptoms

Fragmented approach

Psychological difficulties are considered separately from the body, values, relationships, and environment.

The DREVO approach

Personality is viewed as a holistic developing system of interconnected levels.

Human model

Seven interconnected levels

No level exists in isolation. Harmony emerges through their coordinated work.

01

Body

Health, movement, nutrition, and physiology — the material foundation of life.

02

Subconscious

Experience, habits, and automatic programs that guide reactions.

03

Consciousness

Attention, awareness, learning, and the ability to choose.

04

Psyche

Emotions, memory, thinking, character, and adaptation to the world.

05

Superconscious

Intuition, creativity, and the ability to see new possibilities.

06

Soul

Love, conscience, feelings, dignity, and the experience of life.

07

Spirit

Will, meaning, fidelity to values, and the chosen path.

ROOTSTRUNKCROWNFRUIT
Developmental logic

Not a struggle with oneself,
but the cultivation of wholeness

Roots provide support. The trunk develops resilience. The crown reveals abilities. Fruits transform inner development into actions, relationships, and creation.

Core principles

What distinguishes the DREVO approach

01

Wholeness

A change in one area of life affects the entire system.

02

Development

The goal is not only to remove difficulties, but to reveal human potential.

03

Awareness

A person can observe reactions and gradually change their life.

04

Responsibility

Freedom of choice is connected with responsibility for consequences.

05

Interconnection

Well-being cannot be separated from relationships, society, and nature.

06

Verifiability

Scientific claims require research; philosophical ideas are identified as models.

Purpose of the system

A mature person

Not an ‘ideal state,’ but the ability to preserve and restore inner harmony, consciously choose a direction in life, and participate in the development of society.

  • physical health;
  • psychological resilience;
  • clarity of consciousness;
  • strength of will;
  • unfolding of the soul;
  • strengthening of spirit;
  • responsibility toward society and the world;
Science and philosophy

Where scientific evidence exists, the system relies on it. The soul, spirit, meaning, and inner experience are presented as a philosophical model open to further research.
