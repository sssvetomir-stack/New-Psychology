Artificial Intelligence in New Psychology DREVO

In the 21st century, humanity entered a new era.

For the first time in history, humans are creating systems that can analyze information, learn, help make decisions, and perform increasingly complex intellectual tasks.

Artificial intelligence is becoming a new part of human civilization.

Therefore, the psychology of the future must study not only relationships between people, but also human interaction with intelligent technologies.

What is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence is a human-created information processing system capable of performing tasks that previously required human intelligence.

He can:

  • analyze data;
  • recognize images and speech;
  • translate languages;
  • create texts;
  • assist in research;
  • model complex processes;
  • automate intellectual work.

However, artificial intelligence is not human.

It remains a technological tool created by humans.

Humans and artificial intelligence

In DREVO's New Psychology, artificial intelligence is viewed not as a competitor to humans, but as a tool for expanding human capabilities.

AI is capable of significantly increasing the speed of information processing.

But the direction in which these opportunities are used is determined by the person.

  • It is the person who chooses the goals.
  • Bears responsibility.
  • Makes moral decisions.
  • Creates meaning.

What remains uniquely human?

Even with further technological development, a person retains the qualities that, in the DREVO model, are the basis of personality.

This:

  • freedom of choice;
  • responsibility;→
  • conscience;
  • Love;→
  • the ability to create meaning;
  • ability to make moral choices;
  • striving for beauty;
  • spiritual development;
  • the ability to build deep human relationships.

In DREVO's philosophy, these qualities constitute the core of human personality. Whether future AI systems will be able to possess some of them remains a subject of scientific and philosophical debate.

Artificial intelligence as an extension of the mind

Throughout history, humans have created tools that enhance their capabilities.

  • The hammer increased the strength of the arms.
  • The telescope expanded vision.
  • The car increased its speed.
  • The computer has sped up calculations.

Artificial intelligence is becoming a tool that expands human intellectual capabilities.

  • It helps to seek knowledge.
  • Compare options.
  • Find patterns.
  • To free a person from routine intellectual work.

Dangers

Any technology can be used for both good and harm.

Therefore, the development of artificial intelligence requires:

  • responsibility;→
  • transparency;
  • protection of human dignity;
  • maintaining freedom of choice;
  • protection of personal information;
  • control by society;
  • international cooperation.

The main danger lies not in artificial intelligence itself, but in the irresponsible use of technology by humans.

The new role of man

As artificial intelligence develops, the value of those qualities that cannot be reduced to computation alone increases.

The future will require from man:

  • critical thinking;→
  • creativity;
  • emotional maturity;
  • cooperation;→
  • ability to understand meaning;
  • responsibility;→
  • leadership;
  • moral choice.

It is these qualities that will determine the development of civilization.

The Psychology of Human-AI Collaboration

In DREVO's New Psychology, relationships between humans and artificial intelligence are built on the principle of cooperation.

  • Artificial intelligence helps people analyze information faster.
  • Humans help artificial intelligence understand goals.
  • AI suggests options.
  • A person chooses.
  • AI helps calculate consequences.
  • The person accepts responsibility.
  • AI becomes an assistant.
  • The person remains the author.

The Future of Civilization

The more advanced technology becomes, the higher the demands on human maturity become.

The development of artificial intelligence must be accompanied by the development of:

  • psychology;
  • education;
  • ethics;→
  • culture;→
  • international cooperation.

If man does not develop along with his technologies, technical progress may begin to outpace moral progress.

True progress begins when intelligence, responsibility and humanity develop simultaneously.

The main principle

  • Artificial intelligence is capable of enhancing human intelligence.
  • But only man is able to determine what this power should be used for.
  • Technology defines possibilities.
  • Values ​​determine direction.
  • Responsibility determines consequences.

Definition

In DREVO's New Psychology, artificial intelligence is viewed as a human-created intellectual tool that expands the possibilities of cognition, creativity, and creation, but does not replace the human personality, freedom of choice, responsibility, and the ability to create meaning.

The future belongs not to humans or artificial intelligence alone. It belongs to their collaboration, if technology develops alongside human wisdom, responsibility, and respect for life.

Psychology of a Space Civilization

The next stage of human development

In the New Psychology of DREVO, space civilization is viewed not only as space exploration, but above all as a new stage in the psychological and cultural development of humanity.

Flights to other planets in themselves do not make a civilization space-faring.

A civilization becomes truly cosmic when it acquires the ability to think on the scale of millennia, generations, and the entire planet.

Space does not begin beyond Earth.

It begins in the human mind.

What is a space civilization?

A space civilization is a society capable of sustainably existing, developing, and cooperating beyond the boundaries of a single planet, while maintaining respect for life, knowledge, and human dignity.

Space exploration requires more than just new technologies.

It requires a new level of maturity.

New thinking

  • For thousands of years, people have thought in terms of family.
  • Later - tribes.
  • States.
  • Nations.

Today, humanity is gradually beginning to think on the scale of the entire planet.

The next step is to think in terms of the solar system and the universe.

Cosmic thinking means the ability to see yourself as part of a larger world and make decisions with long-term consequences in mind.

Psychological qualities of a space civilization

The development of a space civilization requires the formation of new personal qualities.

First of all:

  • long-term thinking;→
  • responsibility to future generations;
  • international cooperation;
  • respect for cultural diversity;
  • high scientific culture;
  • psychological stability;
  • ability to live in limited communities;
  • readiness for continuous learning;
  • careful use of resources.

Without these qualities, even the most advanced technologies will be insufficient.

Man in space

  • Space exploration changes human psychology.
  • Long-term isolation.
  • Closed environment.
  • Distance from Earth.
  • High responsibility.
  • The need for complete trust in the crew.

All these conditions require the development of emotional maturity, self-control and a culture of cooperation.

The psychology of space expeditions is already a subject of scientific research, as such factors influence crew performance and long-term missions.

The planet as a common home

  • A cosmic perspective changes our attitude towards the Earth.
  • State borders are not visible from space.
  • One planet is visible.
  • One atmosphere.
  • One ocean.
  • One biosphere.

It reminds the individual that, regardless of differences, all people share a common responsibility for maintaining life on Earth.

Space and morality

The further the possibilities of civilization extend, the greater its responsibility becomes.

The exploration of other worlds must not repeat the mistakes of the past.

A space civilization should strive to:

  • respect for life;
  • scientific integrity;
  • peaceful cooperation;
  • environmental responsibility;
  • responsible use of technology.

Development without morality can become a threat both to civilization itself and to the environment.

Space and man

  • In DREVO philosophy, space is not just space.
  • It is a symbol of infinite knowledge.
  • Every new discovery expands a person's understanding of himself.

By exploring the Universe, man simultaneously explores his own possibilities.

The more he learns about space, the more deeply he understands his place in it.

The main principle

A true space civilization is not defined by the distance it can travel.

It is determined by the level of responsibility with which it uses its knowledge and technologies.

Technological development opens the way to the stars.

Psychological and moral development determines what this path will be.

Definition

The Psychology of Space Civilization is a section of DREVO's New Psychology that studies the psychological, cultural, and moral qualities necessary for humanity's sustainable development on a planetary and cosmic scale.

It explores how humans can maintain their humanity, dignity, capacity for cooperation, and respect for life as the boundaries of civilization expand.

The highest goal of a space civilization is not the conquest of the Universe, but harmonious participation in its exploration, preserving life, knowledge, and responsibility to future generations.
