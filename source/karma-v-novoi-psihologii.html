In the New Psychology of DREVO, responsibility becomes the main way to change karma.

  • The person stops asking:
  • "Why did this happen to me?"
  • And he begins to ask:
  • "What can I do now?"
  • It is from this moment that life begins to change.

Practice of generating favorable karma

  • It is useful to ask yourself questions every day:
  • What consequences do my actions create today?
  • What habits am I strengthening?
  • What will remain after me?→
  • Do my actions bring me closer to the person I want to become?
  • What did I do today that made the world a little better?
  • Such questions gradually form responsibility for one’s own life.

The main principle

  • Karma is not created by one big action.
  • It is created by thousands of daily decisions.
  • Every choice becomes a new seed.
  • From these seeds the tree of human destiny gradually grows.

Definition

Karma is the natural process of forming the consequences of a person's thoughts, decisions, actions, and habits, which gradually change their personality, relationships, life circumstances, and future.

In DREVO's New Psychology, karma is viewed as a psychological and social law of cause and effect. It manifests itself through the development of character, habits, relationships, and the environment a person creates around themselves. Karma is not an inevitable punishment or reward—it is the result of what a person consistently does every day.

The best way to change karma is to change today's actions. The present becomes the point at which a new future is born.

The legacy of man

The Legacy of Man in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, a person's life is valued not only by what they have received, but also by what they leave behind.

  • True heritage is not just property.
  • Not just money.
  • Not just fame.

The most valuable legacy is the change that continues to benefit long after the person is gone.

A person becomes part of the future through his heritage.

What is heritage?

A person's legacy is everything that continues to positively influence people, society, nature and culture through their life, knowledge, actions and creative activity.

Heritage is the continuation of personality over time.

Forms of heritage

Every person is capable of leaving behind different types of legacies.

Family

  • Love.→
  • Raising children.
  • Transferred values.
  • Family traditions.→
  • Good name of the family.

Knowledge

  • Books.
  • Scientific discoveries.
  • Experience.
  • Inventions.
  • Educational projects.
  • Teaching.

Creation

  • Enterprises.
  • At home.
  • Gardens.
  • Works of art.
  • Technologies.→
  • Public organizations.
  • Any work that continues to benefit people.

Community

  • Created groups.
  • Traditions of cooperation.
  • Trust between people.
  • Culture of mutual assistance.
  • Strong communities outlive their creators.

Nature

  • Planted trees.
  • Restored forests.
  • Clean rivers.
  • Conserved species.
  • Environmental projects.
  • Man can leave behind a more living Earth.

Culture

  • Music.
  • Language.
  • Customs.
  • Philosophy.
  • Architecture.
  • Paintings.
  • Movies.
  • Culture becomes a bridge between generations.

The legacy begins today

  • Many people believe that inheritance only comes in old age.
  • In fact, it is created daily.
  • Every good deed.
  • Every honest act.
  • Every knowledge transmitted.
  • Every well-mannered child.
  • Every completed project.
  • Every day becomes part of a person's future legacy.

Heritage and Purpose

  • Purpose answers the question:
  • "What do I live for?"
  • Heritage responds:
  • "What will remain after me?"→

When a person connects these two questions, his life takes on long-term meaning.

Legacy and Immortality

In the New Psychology of DREVO, immortality is understood primarily as the continuation of a person’s influence through his actions.

  • The man continues to live:
  • in your children;
  • in students;
  • in books;
  • in discoveries;
  • in created communities;
  • in planted trees;
  • in culture;
  • in good deeds that inspire others.

In this way, the life of an individual becomes part of the life of humanity.

Heritage Creation Practices

  • It is helpful to regularly ask yourself:
  • What will remain after me?→
  • What knowledge am I passing on?
  • What traditions am I creating?
  • What world will be better because of my life?

What have I done today that will be useful in ten, twenty, or a hundred years?

These questions help us live not only in the present, but also in the future.

Seven Types of DREVO Heritage

Every person can leave behind:

  1. Biological heritage- children and healthy future generations.
  2. Moral heritage- values, life example, culture of relationships.
  3. Intellectual heritage— knowledge, books, discoveries, education.
  4. Creative legacy— projects, technologies, works of art, organizations.
  5. Social heritage— communities, traditions of cooperation, mutual assistance.
  6. Environmental heritage— restored nature, preserved ecosystems, care for the planet.
  7. Spiritual heritage— ideas, inspiration, life wisdom that help other people become better.

The main principle

A person's life is measured not only by the years he lives.

It is measured by how much life is continued thanks to it.

The more goodness, knowledge, love, beauty and opportunities a person leaves behind, the richer his legacy.

Definition

A person's legacy is the sum of the tangible and intangible results of his or her life that continue to benefit people, society, nature, and future generations after him or her.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, creating a legacy is viewed as the highest stage of personal maturity. A person's development reaches its full potential when their knowledge, actions, love, and creative activity take on a life of their own, continuing to shape the world even after their passing.

A tree neither eats its own fruit nor rests in its own shade. It grows for the continuation of life. Similarly, a person reaches maturity when they begin to live not only for themselves but also for those who come after them.

How to become happy?

The Path to Happiness in New Psychology DREVO

In the New Psychology of DREVO, happiness is not seen as random luck, a gift of fate, or a constant feeling of joy.

  • Happiness is a way of life.
  • This is the result of harmonious development of the personality.
  • A person becomes happier not when he gets everything he wants.

He becomes happier when he realizes his abilities, lives in accordance with his values ​​and makes a difference in life.

Happiness is a journey, not a destination.

The first step is to know yourself

  • It is impossible to build a happy life without understanding yourself.
  • It is useful to ask questions:
  • Who am I?
  • What is really important to me?
  • What are my strengths?
  • What brings me a feeling of fulfillment?
  • What kind of person do I want to become?
  • Self-knowledge becomes the foundation of a happy life.

Take care of your body

  • The body is the basis of all other levels of personality.
  • A good night's sleep.
  • Movement.
  • Healthy eating.
  • Fresh air.
  • Rest.

Without taking care of your body, it is difficult to maintain psychological well-being.

Develop your consciousness

  • Learn throughout your life.
  • Read.
  • Explore.
  • Ask questions.
  • Develop critical thinking.
  • The more a person understands the world, the freer his choice becomes.

Strengthen your will

  • Happiness requires not only desires, but also actions.
  • Keep the promises you make to yourself.
  • Develop discipline.
  • Finish what you started.
  • Every small success strengthens your inner confidence.

Open your soul

  • Love.→
  • Create.→
  • Notice the beauty.
  • Be sincere.
  • Help people.
  • A person becomes happier when he reveals his best qualities.

Strengthen your spirit

  • Live for something more than your own comfort.
  • Seek purpose.
  • Follow your values.
  • Think about the future.
  • When life has meaning, even difficulties become part of the journey.

Build relationships

  • Happiness rarely exists alone.
  • Love.→
  • Friendship.
  • Family.→
  • Community.→
  • Trust.→
  • Support.

It is relationships that become one of the main sources of inner well-being.

Create

  • Don't limit yourself to consumption.
  • Create.→
  • Teach.
  • Build.
  • Write.
  • Be inventive.
  • Plant trees.
  • Help people.
  • Creation gives a sense of the true value of one's own life.

Live in harmony with nature

  • Man remains a part of the living world.
  • Nature helps to restore strength.
  • Teaches patience.
  • Reminds us of the natural rhythms of life.
  • Caring for nature simultaneously becomes caring for man himself.

Thank you

  • Every day, notice the good that is already present in your life.
  • Gratitude does not cancel out the desire for development.
  • It helps you see the value of the present moment.

Accept difficulties

A happy life does not mean a life without problems.

Difficulties help develop:

  • wisdom;
  • patience;
  • strength of spirit;
  • compassion;
  • experience.

It is not the trials themselves that make a person stronger.

What makes him stronger is how he goes through them.

Live in the moment

  • The future is being built today.
  • Don't put off life until better times.
  • Every day you can:
  • do a good deed;
  • learn something new;
  • improve health;
  • hug a loved one;
  • plant a tree;
  • take a step towards your dream.
  • It is from days like these that a happy life is made.

DREVO's Formula for Happiness

Happiness is born when a person:

  • takes care of your body;
  • maintains psychological health;
  • develops consciousness;
  • strengthens the will;
  • reveals the soul;
  • strengthens the spirit;
  • lives in accordance with his values;
  • builds healthy relationships;
  • cares about nature;
  • creates a good legacy.

When these elements are combined, a person feels inner integrity.

The main principle

It is impossible to become happy by constantly expecting happiness to come from outside.

It is born inside a person and gradually manifests itself in his way of life.

Happiness does not appear overnight.

It is created by every day we live.

Definition

Becoming happy means gradually building a life in which the body is healthy, the psyche is stable, the consciousness is free, the will is strong, the soul is open, the spirit has direction, relationships are filled with love, and activities benefit people and the world.

In the New Psychology of DREVO, happiness is viewed as a natural result of harmonious personal development. It arises when a person lives daily in accordance with their values, develops their abilities, and participates in creating the future.

Happiness isn't a reward for a life well lived. Happiness is a way of living. Every day, a person builds it with their thoughts, their decisions, their actions, and the goodness they leave behind.
